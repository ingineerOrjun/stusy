Boolean Algebra and Karnaugh Map — बुलियन बीजगणित र Karnaugh map

बुलियन बीजगणित र के–म्याप

10 hrs · 6 marks

You should already know · तपाईंलाई पहिले यति आउनुपर्छ

  • 2Concept of Logic Gatesलजिक गेटको अवधारणा

    Every Boolean law is a statement about gates, and SOP and POS are read straight off a truth table. Without the truth table of AND, OR and NOT, simplification is symbol-shuffling with nothing underneath it.हरेक Boolean नियम गेटकै कुरा हो, र SOP–POS सिधै truth table बाट पढिन्छ। AND, OR, NOT को truth table नभई simplification तल केही नभएको चिन्ह सार्ने काम बन्छ।

  • 1Number System and Binary Arithmeticसंख्या प्रणाली र बाइनरी गणित

    A K-map is labelled in Gray-code order — 00, 01, 11, 10 — and a minterm number is a binary number. Both come from Unit 1.K-map लाई Gray-code क्रममा — 00, 01, 11, 10 — लेखिन्छ, र minterm नम्बर बाइनरी सङ्ख्या हो। दुवै युनिट १ बाट आउँछन्।

By the end of this unit you can… — यो युनिट सकिँदा तपाईं यी कुरा गर्न सक्नुहुनेछ

  1. State the Boolean laws and use them to simplify an expression.
  2. Write the SOP form from a truth table.
  3. Write the POS form from the same truth table.
  4. Simplify an expression step by step, naming the law at each step.
  5. Simplify a 2-, 3- or 4-variable expression with a Karnaugh map, and say why a group is or is not legal.
यो युनिट सकिँदा: बुलियन नियमहरू भन्न र प्रयोग गर्न, ट्रुथ टेबलबाट SOP र POS लेख्न, हरेक चरणमा नियमको नाम दिँदै सरलीकरण गर्न, र के–म्यापबाट सरल बनाउँदै कुन समूह किन मिल्छ वा मिल्दैन भन्न सक्नुहुनेछ।
Why this unit exists

Boolean algebra is the algebra of 0 and 1. It has only three operations — AND (·), OR (+) and NOT (′) — and it exists to answer one practical question: can this circuit be built with fewer gates?

Fewer gates means less silicon, less power and less delay. Every simplification you do here is a real saving in a real chip.

Careful: + does not mean ordinary addition. In Boolean algebra 1 + 1 = 1, because OR asks "is at least one of them 1?"

यो युनिट किन

बुलियन बीजगणित ० र १ को बीजगणित हो। यसमा तीन क्रिया मात्र छन् — AND (·), OR (+) र NOT (′) — र यो एउटै व्यावहारिक प्रश्नको उत्तर दिन बनेको हो: के यो सर्किट कम गेटले बनाउन सकिन्छ?

कम गेट भनेको कम सिलिकन, कम बिजुली र कम ढिलाइ। यहाँ गरिने हरेक सरलीकरण वास्तविक चिपमा वास्तविक बचत हो।

ध्यान दिनुहोस्: + को अर्थ साधारण जोड होइन। बुलियन बीजगणितमा 1 + 1 = 1 हुन्छ, किनभने OR ले "कम्तीमा एउटा 1 छ?" भनी सोध्छ।

3.1  Boolean relationships and laws — Boolean सम्बन्ध र नियम

The two laws that do the actual work. Complement A + A′ = 1 is how a variable disappears. Absorption A + A′B = A + B is how a whole term shrinks. Almost every simplification in an SEE paper is one of these two, set up by the distributive law. काम गर्ने दुई नियम: Complement A + A′ = 1 ले चल हराउँछ, Absorption A + A′B = A + B ले पूरै पद खुम्च्याउँछ। SEE का प्रायः सबै सरलीकरण यिनै दुईमा आधारित हुन्छन्।

3.2 & 3.3  Sum of Products and Product of Sums — Sum of Products र Product of Sums

Both forms, one table

SOP (Sum of Products) is built from the rows where the output is 1. Each such row gives one product term (variables ANDed), and the terms are ORed together. Inside a 1-row, a variable that is 0 appears complemented.

POS (Product of Sums) is built from the rows where the output is 0. Each such row gives one sum term (variables ORed), and the terms are ANDed together. Inside a 0-row, a variable that is 1 appears complemented.

The two rules are mirror images. The one students get wrong is which value gets the complement — it swaps between the two forms.

दुवै रूप, एउटै तालिका

SOP (Sum of Products) आउटपुट 1 भएका पङ्क्तिबाट बन्छ। हरेक त्यस्तो पङ्क्तिले एउटा product पद (चलहरू AND गरिएका) दिन्छ, र पदहरू OR ले जोडिन्छन्। 1 भएको पङ्क्तिभित्र 0 भएको चल पूरक रूपमा लेखिन्छ।

POS (Product of Sums) आउटपुट 0 भएका पङ्क्तिबाट बन्छ। हरेकले एउटा sum पद (चलहरू OR गरिएका) दिन्छ, र पदहरू AND ले जोडिन्छन्। 0 भएको पङ्क्तिभित्र 1 भएको चल पूरक रूपमा लेखिन्छ।

दुवै नियम आमनेसामने ऐना जस्ता हुन्। विद्यार्थीले बिराउने कुरा एउटै हो — कुन मानले पूरक पाउँछ भन्ने, किनभने त्यो दुई रूपबीच साटिन्छ।

Example 1 Writing SOP and POS from a truth table

Question: a function of three variables is 1 for the input combinations 001, 011 and 111. Write its SOP and POS forms.

SOP — take the 1-rows. In each row, a 0 means the variable is complemented:

Working
001 → A'B'C 011 → A'BC 111 → ABC F = A'B'C + A'BC + ABC

POS — take the 0-rows (000, 010, 100, 101, 110). In each, a 1 means the variable is complemented:

Working
000 → (A + B + C) 010 → (A + B' + C) 100 → (A' + B + C) 101 → (A' + B + C') 110 → (A' + B' + C) F = (A+B+C)(A+B'+C)(A'+B+C)(A'+B+C')(A'+B'+C)

Notice the SOP is much shorter here, because there are only three 1s and five 0s. Choose whichever form has fewer rows — the answer is the same function either way. यहाँ SOP छोटो छ, किनभने 1 तीन र 0 पाँच छन्। जुन रूपमा कम पङ्क्ति हुन्छ त्यही छान्नुहोस् — दुवैले उही फङ्सन दिन्छन्।

3.4  Algebraic simplification

step 0 / 4
Example 2 Simplify F = A'BC + AB'C + ABC' + ABC

Simplifying always starts by finding a factor two terms share. Look at the four terms and name the pair that shares one — getting the first grouping right is most of the question. सरल बनाउने काम सधैँ दुई पदले साझा गरेको factor खोजेर सुरु हुन्छ। चारै पद हेरेर साझा भएको जोडी भन्नुहोस् — पहिलो grouping ठिक पार्नु नै आधा प्रश्न हो।

Step 1 — group terms that share a factor. The last two both contain AB:

F = A'BC + AB'C + AB(C' + C)

Step 2 — apply the complement law C' + C = 1:

F = A'BC + AB'C + AB

Step 3 — take C out of the first two terms:

F = C(A'B + AB') + AB

Answer: F = C(A′B + AB′) + AB, and since A′B + AB′ is the definition of XOR this can also be written F = C(A ⊕ B) + AB.

Marks come from the named steps, not the final line. Write "complement law" or "distributive law" beside each step and you keep the marks even if the last line slips. अंक अन्तिम लाइनले होइन, नाम दिइएका चरणले दिन्छ। हरेक चरणको छेउमा "complement law" वा "distributive law" लेख्नुहोस्।

Karnaugh map — simplifying by picture — Karnaugh map — चित्रबाट सरल बनाउने

Where this sits in the syllabus. The unit is titled "Boolean algebra and Karnaugh Map" and competency 3 names the K-map, but the numbered content list stops at algebraic simplification. It is taught here because the title and the competency both require it. यो युनिटको नाम नै "Boolean algebra and Karnaugh Map" हो र competency ३ ले पनि के–म्याप तोकेको छ, त्यसैले यहाँ सिकाइएको हो।
What a K-map is

A Karnaugh map is a truth table redrawn as a grid, arranged so that neighbouring cells differ in exactly one variable. That is why the headings run 00, 01, 11, 10 — not 00, 01, 10, 11.

Because neighbours differ by one variable, a group of adjacent 1s lets that one variable cancel. Grouping is therefore the complement law applied by eye instead of on paper.

The four rules of grouping: only 1s; size 1, 2, 4 or 8; a rectangle (edges wrap round); and always as large as possible.

के–म्याप भनेको के

Karnaugh map भनेको ट्रुथ टेबललाई ग्रिडमा फेरि कोरेको हो, तर यसरी मिलाइएको कि छिमेकी कक्षमा ठ्याक्कै एउटा चल मात्र फरक होस्। त्यसैले हेडिङ 00, 01, 11, 10 हुन्छ — 00, 01, 10, 11 होइन।

छिमेकीमा एउटै चल फरक हुने भएकाले छेउछाउका 1 को समूहले त्यही चल काट्न दिन्छ। समूह बनाउनु भनेको complement नियमलाई कागजमा नभई आँखाले लागू गर्नु हो।

समूहका चार नियम: 1 मात्र; आकार 1, 2, 4 वा 8; आयत (किनारा वरिपरि जोडिन्छन्); र सधैं सक्ने जति ठूलो।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

Which of these is not a legal K-map group? तलमध्ये कुन के–म्याप समूह मान्य होइन?

A group must hold a power of two — 1, 2, 4 or 8 cells. Three is not a power of two, so a group of three is never allowed no matter how it is shaped. The four corners are legal, because the left and right edges wrap round and so do the top and bottom. Try both in the tool below.

समूहमा दुईको घात जति कक्ष हुनुपर्छ — 1, 2, 4 वा 8। तीन दुईको घात होइन, त्यसैले तीनको समूह जुनसुकै आकारमा भए पनि मिल्दैन। चार कुनाचाहिँ मिल्छ, किनभने किनाराहरू वरिपरि जोडिन्छन्। तलको उपकरणमा दुवै प्रयास गर्नुहोस्।

Interactive experiment · अन्तरक्रियात्मक प्रयोग

The Karnaugh map workbench — Karnaugh map कार्यशाला

What you will learn: which groups are legal, why, and what term each one produces. The tool checks your group against the real rules and tells you which one you broke. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: कुन समूह मान्य हुन्छ, किन, र प्रत्येकले कुन पद दिन्छ। उपकरणले तपाईंको समूहलाई वास्तविक नियमसँग जाँचेर कुन नियम भङ्ग भयो भन्छ।

How to use it

The map starts loaded with F = Σm(0,1,2,3,5,7). Leave it as it is, switch to select group mode, and try to cover every 1 with as few groups as possible.

Try this first: select just two cells of the top row and press Check. The tool accepts the shape but refuses the group, because the whole row of four is available and a bigger group removes more variables. That is rule 4, and it is the rule that separates a correct answer from a simplified one.

म्यापमा सुरुदेखि F = Σm(0,1,2,3,5,7) राखिएको छ। select group मोडमा गएर सबै 1 लाई कम्ती समूहले ढाक्ने प्रयास गर्नुहोस्। पहिले यसो गर्नुहोस्: माथिल्लो पङ्क्तिका दुई कक्ष छानेर Check थिच्नुहोस्। उपकरणले आकार त स्वीकार्छ तर समूह अस्वीकार गर्छ, किनभने चारै कक्षको ठूलो समूह उपलब्ध छ। यही नियम ४ हो।
Example 3 Simplify F = Σm(0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7) with a K-map

Step 1 — draw a 3-variable map with columns BC in Gray order 00, 01, 11, 10 and rows A = 0, 1. Put a 1 in each of the six cells listed.

Step 2 — find the largest groups.

  • The entire A = 0 row is four 1s (minterms 0, 1, 3, 2). A is the only variable that stays the same across it, and it stays 0, so this group gives A′.
  • Minterms 1, 3, 5, 7 form the two middle columns. In all four, C is 1, so this group gives C.

Step 3 — OR the terms together.

Answer
F = A' + C

Six minterms became two letters. Doing this algebraically takes about eight lines; the map takes one look. That is what the K-map is for. छ वटा minterm दुई अक्षरमा झर्‍यो। बीजगणितले गर्दा आठ लाइन लाग्छ; म्यापले एक नजरमै। के–म्याप यसैका लागि हो।

Mistakes that cost marks here — यहाँ अङ्क गुमाउने गल्तीहरू

  • Labelling the map 00, 01, 10, 11. That ordering breaks adjacency, and every group taken from it is wrong. It must be 00, 01, 11, 10. म्यापलाई 00, 01, 10, 11 लेख्नु। यसले adjacency नै भत्काउँछ र सबै समूह गलत हुन्छन्। 00, 01, 11, 10 नै हुनुपर्छ।
  • Taking small groups. A pair inside an available block of four is not wrong arithmetic, but it is not simplified, and the mark is for simplifying. सानो समूह लिनु। चारको ब्लक हुँदाहुँदै दुईको समूह लिनु गणितीय रूपमा गलत छैन, तर सरल भएन — अंक सरलीकरणकै हो।
  • Forgetting that the edges wrap. The leftmost and rightmost columns are neighbours, and so are the top and bottom rows. किनारा वरिपरि जोडिन्छन् भन्ने बिर्सनु। सबैभन्दा बायाँ र दायाँ स्तम्भ छिमेकी हुन्, त्यसै गरी माथिल्लो र तल्लो पङ्क्ति पनि।
  • Confusing the SOP and POS complement rule. In a 1-row a 0 is complemented; in a 0-row a 1 is complemented. SOP र POS को पूरक नियम मिसाउनु। 1 भएको पङ्क्तिमा 0 पूरक हुन्छ; 0 भएको पङ्क्तिमा 1।

K-map order is 00 01 11 10 — one bit changes at a time, or adjacency is gone. K-map को क्रम 00 01 11 10 — एक पटकमा एउटै bit बदलिन्छ, नत्र adjacency नै हराउँछ।

2 marks Practice question

State any four laws of Boolean algebra. बुलियन बीजगणितका कुनै चार नियम लेख्नुहोस्।

Identity: A + 0 = A and A · 1 = A
Null: A + 1 = 1 and A · 0 = 0
Complement: A + A′ = 1 and A · A′ = 0
Idempotent: A + A = A and A · A = A

कुनै चार लेखे पुग्छ, तर हरेकको दुवै रूप (OR र AND) लेख्दा उत्तर पूरा देखिन्छ।
3 marks Practice question

Simplify F = AB + A(B + C) + B(B + C). F = AB + A(B + C) + B(B + C) लाई सरल बनाउनुहोस्।

F = AB + AB + AC + BB + BC expand (distributive law) F = AB + AC + B + BC A+A = A, and B·B = B (idempotent) F = AB + AC + B B + BC = B (absorption) F = B + AC AB + B = B (absorption)

Answer: F = B + AC.

हरेक चरणमा प्रयोग गरेको नियमको नाम लेख्नुहोस् — अंक त्यहीँ छ।
4 marks Practice question

Simplify F(A,B,C) = Σm(0, 2, 4, 5, 6) using a Karnaugh map. के–म्याप प्रयोग गरी F(A,B,C) = Σm(0, 2, 4, 5, 6) सरल बनाउनुहोस्।

Draw a 2×4 map, columns BC = 00, 01, 11, 10 and rows A = 0, 1. Place 1s at m0, m2, m4, m5, m6.

Group 1: m0, m2, m4, m6 — the two outer columns (BC = 00 and BC = 10), wrapping round the edge. C is 0 in all four, so this gives C′.

Group 2: m4, m5 — adjacent in the A = 1 row. A is 1 and B is 0 throughout, so this gives AB′.

F = C' + AB'
पहिलो समूह किनारा जोडिएको चार कक्षको हो र C′ दिन्छ; दोस्रो दुई कक्षको हो र AB′ दिन्छ। उत्तर F = C′ + AB′।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • Boolean + means OR, · means AND, ′ means NOT. 1 + 1 = 1. बुलियनमा + = OR, · = AND, ′ = NOT। 1 + 1 = 1।
  • Every law has an OR form and an AND form — learn one and swap the operator. हरेक नियमको OR र AND रूप हुन्छ — एउटा सिकेर अपरेटर साट्नुहोस्।
  • SOP reads the 1-rows and complements the 0s; POS reads the 0-rows and complements the 1s. SOP ले 1 भएका पङ्क्ति पढ्छ र 0 लाई पूरक बनाउँछ; POS ले 0 भएका पङ्क्ति पढ्छ र 1 लाई पूरक बनाउँछ।
  • Name the law at every simplification step — that is where the marks are. सरलीकरणको हरेक चरणमा नियमको नाम लेख्नुहोस् — अंक त्यहीँ छ।
  • K-map headings are Gray-coded: 00, 01, 11, 10. के–म्यापका हेडिङ Gray code मा: 00, 01, 11, 10।
  • Groups hold only 1s, are a power of two in size, are rectangles, wrap at the edges, and must be as large as possible. समूहमा 1 मात्र, आकार दुईको घात, आयत, किनारा वरिपरि जोडिने, र सक्ने जति ठूलो।
