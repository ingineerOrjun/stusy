Entity Relationship Model (ER-Model)

ई–आर मोडेल

10 hrs · 8 marks

By the end of this unit you can…

  1. Say what the ER model is for and why a database is modelled before it is built.
  2. Identify an entity, an entity set and a weak entity, and draw each symbol.
  3. Name the types of attribute and recognise each from its symbol.
  4. Identify a relationship and state its degree.
  5. Explain 1:1, 1:M and M:N, and say what each one costs in tables.
  6. Define super, candidate, primary, alternate, foreign and composite keys.
  7. Draw a complete ER diagram for a small scenario.
यो युनिट सकिँदा: ER मोडेल किन चाहिन्छ भन्न, इन्टिटी–कमजोर इन्टिटी चिन्न र चिन्ह कोर्न, एट्रिब्युटका प्रकार नाम लिन, सम्बन्ध र यसको degree भन्न, 1:1–1:M–M:N को अर्थ र तालिकामा पर्ने असर भन्न, कुञ्जीका प्रकार परिभाषित गर्न, र सानो परिदृश्यको पूरा ER चित्र कोर्न सक्नुहुनेछ।
Why model before you build

A database is expensive to change once it holds real data. The ER model is a picture of the database drawn before any table exists, so that mistakes are found on paper instead of after a year of records.

It is deliberately not SQL and not tables. It is a diagram of things and how they are connected, in language a school clerk could check. Only once the picture is agreed is it turned into tables — and that translation is Unit 3.

An ER diagram has exactly three kinds of shape: rectangles for things, ellipses for their properties, and diamonds for the connections between them.

बनाउनुअघि मोडेल किन

वास्तविक data भरिसकेपछि डाटाबेस बदल्न महँगो पर्छ। ER मोडेल भनेको कुनै तालिका बन्नुभन्दा अघि कोरिने डाटाबेसको चित्र हो, ताकि गल्ती वर्ष दिनको रेकर्डपछि होइन, कागजमै भेटियोस्।

यो जानाजान SQL पनि होइन, तालिका पनि होइन। यो वस्तु र तिनका सम्बन्ध को चित्र हो — विद्यालयको कर्मचारीले पनि जाँच्न सक्ने भाषामा। चित्र मिलेपछि मात्र तालिकामा बदलिन्छ, र त्यो अनुवाद युनिट ३ मा छ।

ER चित्रमा ठ्याक्कै तीन किसिमका आकार हुन्छन्: वस्तुका लागि आयत, तिनका गुणका लागि दीर्घवृत्त, र जोडाइका लागि चतुर्भुज।

2.2 – 2.4  The components of an ER diagram

Interactive experiment · अन्तरक्रियात्मक प्रयोग

Every ER component, one at a time

What you will learn: which shape belongs to which idea, and what each one becomes when the diagram is turned into tables. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: कुन आकार कुन विचारको हो, र चित्र तालिकामा बदलिँदा हरेक के बन्छ।

How to use this

Press each component in turn and watch which part of the diagram lights up. Read the shape name — that is what you have to draw in the exam — and then the sentence, which tells you what the component becomes in a real table.

Pay attention to weak entity. "Weak" does not mean unimportant. It means the thing cannot be identified without its owner: a dependant is identified by which employee they belong to, plus their name.

हरेक भाग क्रमैसँग थिच्नुहोस् र चित्रको कुन भाग बल्छ हेर्नुहोस्। आकारको नाम पढ्नुहोस् — परीक्षामा कोर्नुपर्ने त्यही हो — अनि वाक्य पढ्नुहोस्, जसले त्यो भाग वास्तविक तालिकामा के बन्छ बताउँछ। कमजोर इन्टिटी मा ध्यान दिनुहोस्। "कमजोर" को अर्थ महत्त्वहीन होइन; मालिकविना चिनिन नसक्ने भन्ने हो।
Degree of a relationship is how many entities take part in it — not how many rows. Unary (one entity related to itself: an employee manages an employee), binary (two entities — almost every relationship you will meet), ternary (three). Degree and cardinality are different questions: degree counts entities, cardinality counts how many rows link to how many. सम्बन्धको degree भनेको त्यसमा कति इन्टिटी सहभागी छन् भन्ने हो — कति पङ्क्ति भन्ने होइन। Unary (एउटै इन्टिटी आफैंसँग), binary (दुई — प्रायः सबै), ternary (तीन)। Degree र cardinality फरक प्रश्न हुन्: degree ले इन्टिटी गन्छ, cardinality ले कति पङ्क्ति कतिसँग जोडिन्छ भन्ने।

2.5  Mapping cardinalities

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A student may take many courses, and a course may be taken by many students. How many TABLES will this relationship need? एउटा विद्यार्थीले धेरै विषय लिन सक्छ, र एउटा विषय धेरै विद्यार्थीले लिन सक्छन्। यो सम्बन्धलाई कति तालिका चाहिन्छ?

Three. Student, Course, and a third table holding one row for every enrolment. No single foreign key can record "many on both sides" — a column holds one value, so it can point at one row. The moment a relationship is many-to-many, a junction table is forced. Switch the tool below to M:N and the third table appears.

तीन। Student, Course, र हरेक भर्नाका लागि एक पङ्क्ति राख्ने तेस्रो तालिका। "दुवैतर्फ धेरै" लाई एउटै foreign key ले राख्न सक्दैन — स्तम्भले एउटा मान राख्छ, त्यसैले एउटै पङ्क्ति देखाउँछ। सम्बन्ध धेरै–धेरै भएकै क्षण junction तालिका अनिवार्य हुन्छ। तलको औजारमा M:N छान्नुहोस्, तेस्रो तालिका देखिन्छ।

Interactive experiment · अन्तरक्रियात्मक प्रयोग

The ER visualiser — what a cardinality actually means

What you will learn: the difference between one-to-many and many-to-many, shown in rows rather than in notation — and why only one of them needs a third table. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: एक–धेरै र धेरै–धेरैको फरक, चिन्हमा होइन पङ्क्तिमा देखेर — र किन तीमध्ये एउटालाई मात्र तेस्रो तालिका चाहिन्छ।

What to notice

The notation at the top barely changes between the three buttons — one letter. The rows underneath change completely, and that is the real difference.

On 1:M, read the note under the list: every course points back at exactly one student. Now press M:N. The same note changes — Maths is now reached from two students. Both directions are "many", and that is the whole definition.

Only M:N produces a junction table. Watch it appear, and notice it holds nothing but two foreign keys — one row per link, which is exactly what the relationship is.

माथिको चिन्ह तीन बटनबीच झन्डै बदलिँदैन — एउटा अक्षर मात्र। तलका पङ्क्ति भने पूरै बदलिन्छन्, र वास्तविक फरक त्यही हो। 1:M मा सूचीमुनिको टिप्पणी पढ्नुहोस्: हरेक विषयले ठ्याक्कै एउटा विद्यार्थी देखाउँछ। अब M:N थिच्नुहोस् — Maths अब दुई विद्यार्थीबाट जोडिन्छ। दुवैतर्फ "धेरै", र पूरा परिभाषा यही हो। M:N ले मात्र junction तालिका बनाउँछ; त्यसमा दुई foreign key बाहेक केही हुँदैन।

Practise the judgement · निर्णय अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्

Read the sentence, name the cardinality

What you will learn: to test both directions every time, which is the one habit that separates a right answer from a guess. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: सधैं दुवै दिशा जाँच्ने बानी — सही उत्तर र अनुमानबीचको फरक यही एउटा बानीले पार्छ।

How to use this

For each case, read the two sentences as two separate questions: how many on the right for one on the left? and then how many on the left for one on the right? Only after both do you have a cardinality.

The last two cases are the ones worth slowing down for. One uses softer wording — "may be treated by several" is still "many". The other describes a relationship between entities that also relate in other ways: judge the relationship that is named, not the entities.

हरेक केसमा दुई वाक्यलाई दुई छुट्टै प्रश्न मानेर पढ्नुहोस्: बायाँको एउटालाई दायाँ कति? अनि दायाँको एउटालाई बायाँ कति? दुवैपछि मात्र cardinality थाहा हुन्छ। अन्तिम दुई केसमा बिस्तारै जानुहोस् — एउटामा शब्द नरम छ, अर्कोमा नाम दिइएको सम्बन्ध हेर्नुपर्छ, इन्टिटी होइन।
Example 1 Choosing the cardinality from a sentence

Question: decide the mapping cardinality for each, and give the reason.

Working
"Each citizen has one citizenship number, and each number belongs to one citizen." → 1:1 both directions are "one" "A class contains many students, but each student is in only one class." → 1:M many one way, one the other way "A student takes many courses, and a course is taken by many students." → M:N many BOTH ways

Always test both directions. A sentence that only tells you one direction has not told you the cardinality. "A class has many students" alone could be 1:M or M:N — you have to ask whether a student can be in two classes. सधैं दुवै दिशा जाँच्नुहोस्। एउटा दिशा मात्र बताउने वाक्यले cardinality बताएकै हुँदैन। "एउटा कक्षामा धेरै विद्यार्थी" भन्ने मात्रले 1:M कि M:N भन्न मिल्दैन — एउटा विद्यार्थी दुई कक्षामा हुन सक्छ कि भनी सोध्नुपर्छ।

2.6  Keys in DBMS

The two rules a primary key must obey

A primary key must be unique — no two rows may share its value — and it must be NOT NULL — it can never be left empty. Those two rules are what allow a row to be found reliably.

A foreign key is different in kind. It is not about identifying a row in its own table; it is a column whose value must already exist as a primary key in another table. That rule is what links two tables together, and it is called referential integrity.

A foreign key may be NULL — Gita has no class yet — and it may repeat, since many students share one class. A primary key may do neither.

Primary key ले पालना गर्नुपर्ने दुई नियम

Primary key अद्वितीय हुनुपर्छ — दुई पङ्क्तिको मान उही हुन नहुने — र NOT NULL हुनुपर्छ — कहिल्यै खाली छाड्न नहुने। यी दुई नियमले नै पङ्क्ति भरपर्दो रूपमा खोज्न दिन्छन्।

Foreign key फरक किसिमको हो। यसले आफ्नै तालिकाको पङ्क्ति चिनाउँदैन; यो त्यस्तो स्तम्भ हो जसको मान अर्को तालिकाको primary key मा पहिले नै हुनैपर्छ। यही नियमले दुई तालिका जोड्छ, र यसलाई referential integrity भनिन्छ।

Foreign key NULL हुन सक्छ — गीताको कक्षा अझै छैन — र दोहोरिन पनि सक्छ, किनभने धेरै विद्यार्थीको कक्षा एउटै हुन्छ। Primary key ले यी दुवै गर्न पाउँदैन।

Example 2 Identifying every key in a table

Question: a Student table holds roll, reg_no, name, class_id. Both roll and reg_no are unique. class_id exists in the Class table. Name every key.

Step 1 — find everything that identifies a row uniquely. These are the super keys, and there are many, because adding spare columns to a unique column keeps it unique:

Working
{roll} unique ✓ {reg_no} unique ✓ {roll, name} unique ✓ (roll alone was enough) {reg_no, class_id} unique ✓ (reg_no alone was enough) {name} NOT unique — two students may share a name

Step 2 — strip out the spare columns. A super key with nothing unnecessary left in it is a candidate key: {roll} and {reg_no}.

Step 3 — choose one. The school uses roll, so that is the primary key. The candidate key not chosen becomes the alternate key.

Answer
Super keys {roll}, {reg_no}, {roll, name}, … many Candidate keys {roll}, {reg_no} Primary key {roll} (chosen) Alternate key {reg_no} (the one not chosen) Foreign key {class_id} → Class.class_id

The examiner is checking that you know these are nested, not five unrelated things. Every primary key is a candidate key, and every candidate key is a super key. Saying so in one line is often worth a mark by itself. परीक्षकले यी पाँच असम्बन्धित कुरा होइनन्, एकभित्र अर्को हुन् भन्ने जान्नुभएको छ कि छैन हेर्छन्। हरेक primary key एउटा candidate key हो, र हरेक candidate key एउटा super key। यही एक हरफले प्रायः एक अंक दिन्छ।

Mistakes that cost marks here

  • Calling a weak entity "unimportant". It means the entity has no key of its own and must borrow its owner's. Draw it with a double rectangle. कमजोर इन्टिटीलाई "महत्त्वहीन" भन्नु। यसको अर्थ आफ्नो कुञ्जी नभएको र मालिकको कुञ्जी लिनुपर्ने भन्ने हो। दोहोरो आयतले कोर्नुहोस्।
  • Answering "many students in a class" as M:N. Check the other direction: a student is in one class, so it is 1:M. "एउटा कक्षामा धेरै विद्यार्थी" लाई M:N भन्नु। अर्को दिशा जाँच्नुहोस्: विद्यार्थी एउटै कक्षामा हुन्छ, त्यसैले 1:M।
  • Confusing degree with cardinality. Degree counts the entities in a relationship; cardinality counts how many rows link to how many. Degree र cardinality मिसाउनु। Degree ले सम्बन्धका इन्टिटी गन्छ; cardinality ले कति पङ्क्ति कतिसँग जोडिन्छ भन्छ।
  • Underlining nothing in an ER diagram. The key attribute must be underlined — that is the notation for a primary key, and a marker looks for it. ER चित्रमा कुनै पनि नाममुनि रेखा नकोर्नु। कुञ्जी एट्रिब्युटमुनि रेखा हुनैपर्छ — primary key को चिन्ह त्यही हो।
  • Writing a relationship as a noun. A diamond holds a verb: ENROLS, TEACHES, CONTAINS — not "enrolment". सम्बन्धलाई नामपदमा लेख्नु। चतुर्भुजभित्र क्रियापद हुन्छ: ENROLS, TEACHES, CONTAINS — "enrolment" होइन।

Exam focus — what is asked from this unit

This unit is examined mostly by drawing. The commonest question gives you two or three sentences describing a small system and asks for an ER diagram — entities, attributes with the key underlined, a relationship named as a verb, and the cardinality marked on both lines. Marks are given for each of those four things separately, so a diagram missing its cardinalities still earns most of them. Draw it big, and label everything. यो युनिट प्रायः कोरेर जाँचिन्छ। सामान्य प्रश्नले दुई–तीन वाक्यमा सानो प्रणाली वर्णन गरेर ER चित्र माग्छ — इन्टिटी, कुञ्जीमुनि रेखासहितका एट्रिब्युट, क्रियापदमा नाम दिइएको सम्बन्ध, र दुवै रेखामा cardinality। यी चार कुरालाई छुट्टाछुट्टै अंक हुन्छ, त्यसैले cardinality छुटे पनि धेरैजसो अंक आउँछ। ठूलो कोर्नुहोस् र सबैमा नाम लेख्नुहोस्।
2 marks Practice question

Define entity and attribute with one example of each. इन्टिटी र एट्रिब्युटको परिभाषा एक–एक उदाहरणसहित दिनुहोस्।

An entity is a real-world object or concept about which data is stored, and which can be identified distinctly. Example: a STUDENT. It is drawn as a rectangle and becomes a table.

An attribute is a property or characteristic of an entity. Example: the student's name. It is drawn as an ellipse and becomes a column.

दुवैको परिभाषा, एक–एक उदाहरण, र चाहेमा कुन आकारले कोरिन्छ भन्ने पनि।
4 marks Practice question

Explain the three mapping cardinalities with a suitable example of each. तीनै mapping cardinality लाई उपयुक्त उदाहरणसहित व्याख्या गर्नुहोस्।

ONE-TO-ONE (1:1) Each A relates to at most one B, and each B to at most one A. Example: a citizen and a citizenship number. ONE-TO-MANY (1:M) One A relates to many B, but each B to only one A. Example: a class contains many students; each student is in one class. MANY-TO-MANY (M:N) Many A relate to many B, in both directions. Example: a student takes many courses, and a course is taken by many students. → needs a third (junction) table.

One mark per cardinality with its example, and the fourth for stating that M:N requires an extra table.

हरेक cardinality र उदाहरणलाई एक–एक अंक, र M:N लाई थप तालिका चाहिन्छ भनेकोमा चौथो अंक।
4 marks Practice question

A library keeps books and members. A member may borrow many books, and a book may be borrowed by many members over time. Draw an ER diagram for this. एउटा पुस्तकालयले पुस्तक र सदस्यको अभिलेख राख्छ। एक सदस्यले धेरै पुस्तक लिन सक्छन्, र एउटै पुस्तक समयक्रममा धेरै सदस्यले लिन सक्छन्। यसको ER चित्र कोर्नुहोस्।

(member_id) (name) (book_id) (title) \ / \ / ┌──────────┐ M ◇ BORROWS ◇ N ┌────────┐ │ MEMBER │──────────────────────│ BOOK │ └──────────┘ └────────┘ member_id and book_id are UNDERLINED (key attributes) BORROWS is written in a diamond, as a verb M and N are marked on the two lines

One mark for the two entities as rectangles, one for the attributes as ellipses with the keys underlined, one for the relationship in a diamond named as a verb, and one for the M:N cardinality marked on both lines.

आयतमा दुई इन्टिटीलाई एक अंक, कुञ्जीमुनि रेखासहित दीर्घवृत्तका एट्रिब्युटलाई एक, चतुर्भुजमा क्रियापद रूपको सम्बन्धलाई एक, र दुवै रेखामा M:N लेखेकोमा एक।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • Rectangle = entity, ellipse = attribute, diamond = relationship. Double rectangle = weak entity, double ellipse = multivalued, dashed ellipse = derived, underlined = key. आयत = इन्टिटी, दीर्घवृत्त = एट्रिब्युट, चतुर्भुज = सम्बन्ध। दोहोरो आयत = कमजोर इन्टिटी, दोहोरो दीर्घवृत्त = बहुमान, धर्के = व्युत्पन्न, मुनि रेखा = कुञ्जी।
  • Degree counts the entities in a relationship; cardinality counts how many rows link to how many. They are different questions. Degree ले सम्बन्धका इन्टिटी गन्छ; cardinality ले कति पङ्क्ति कतिसँग जोडिन्छ। दुई फरक प्रश्न हुन्।
  • Always test cardinality in BOTH directions. Many one way and one the other is 1:M; many both ways is M:N. Cardinality सधैं दुवै दिशामा जाँच्नुहोस्। एकातिर धेरै र अर्कोतिर एक भए 1:M; दुवैतिर धेरै भए M:N।
  • M:N always needs a third table. 1:1 and 1:M do not. M:N लाई सधैं तेस्रो तालिका चाहिन्छ। 1:1 र 1:M लाई चाहिँदैन।
  • A primary key is unique and never NULL. A foreign key may be NULL and may repeat, but its value must already exist in the table it points at. Primary key अद्वितीय हुन्छ र कहिल्यै NULL हुँदैन। Foreign key NULL हुन र दोहोरिन सक्छ, तर यसको मान देखाइएको तालिकामा पहिले नै हुनुपर्छ।
  • Every primary key is a candidate key; every candidate key is a super key. The reverse is not true. हरेक primary key एउटा candidate key हो; हरेक candidate key एउटा super key। उल्टो होइन।
