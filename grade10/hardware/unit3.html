Overview on System's Core

3.1  The system BIOS

Definition — learn this wording

BIOS (Basic Input Output System) is firmware stored on a chip on the motherboard. It is the first program that runs when the machine is switched on — before any operating system exists in memory.

Its four functions:

1. POST (Power-On Self Test) — checks that the essential hardware is present and working. If something fails, it reports with beep codes, because the screen may not be available yet.

2. Bootstrap loader — finds the operating system on a drive and loads it into memory.

3. BIOS drivers — low-level control of the basic hardware so the machine can work before the OS drivers load.

4. CMOS setup — the screen where boot order, time and hardware settings are changed. Those settings are kept in CMOS memory, powered by a small battery.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

BIOS (Basic Input Output System) मदरबोर्डको चिपमा राखिएको firmware हो। मेसिन खोल्दा सबैभन्दा पहिले चल्ने प्रोग्राम यही हो — मेमोरीमा कुनै अपरेटिङ सिस्टम आउनुअघि नै।

यसका चार काम:

१. POST (Power-On Self Test) — आवश्यक हार्डवेयर छ र चलिरहेको छ कि जाँच्छ। केही बिग्रिए बीप कोड ले सुनाउँछ, किनकि स्क्रिन अझै उपलब्ध नहुन सक्छ।

२. Bootstrap loader — ड्राइभमा अपरेटिङ सिस्टम खोजेर मेमोरीमा लोड गर्छ।

३. BIOS drivers — आधारभूत हार्डवेयरको तल्लो तहको नियन्त्रण, ताकि OS का driver लोड हुनुअघि पनि मेसिन चल्न सकोस्।

४. CMOS setup — boot order, समय र हार्डवेयर सेटिङ बदल्ने स्क्रिन। ती सेटिङ CMOS मेमोरीमा रहन्छन्, जसलाई सानो ब्याट्रीले चलाउँछ।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A computer shows the wrong date every time it is switched on, and the BIOS settings keep returning to default. What is the most likely cause? कम्प्युटर खोल्दा हरेक पटक गलत मिति देखिन्छ, र BIOS सेटिङ बारम्बार पूर्वनिर्धारितमा फर्किन्छ। सबैभन्दा सम्भावित कारण के हो?

The CMOS memory that holds the settings and runs the clock needs power even when the machine is unplugged, and that comes from a small coin battery on the board. When it dies, everything CMOS remembers is lost each time mains power goes. It is a cheap part and a two-minute fix — but only if you read the symptom correctly instead of suspecting the disk.

सेटिङ राख्ने र घडी चलाउने CMOS मेमोरीलाई मेसिन अनप्लग हुँदा पनि बिजुली चाहिन्छ, र त्यो बोर्डको सानो सिक्का ब्याट्रीबाट आउँछ। त्यो सकिएपछि मुख्य बिजुली जाँदा CMOS ले सम्झेको सबै हराउँछ। सस्तो पार्ट र दुई मिनेटको काम हो — तर लक्षण ठीक पढेमा मात्र, disk मा शंका गरेर होइन।

3.2  The motherboard and its form factors

Definition — learn this wording

The motherboard is the main printed circuit board. Every other component either plugs into it or connects to it, and it carries the buses that let them communicate.

A form factor is the standard that fixes a board's size, its mounting-hole positions, and where its ports and power connector sit. ATX is the common desktop standard; Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX are smaller versions with fewer slots.

The form factor is why a replacement board is not simply "any board" — it must match the case it has to screw into and the power supply that has to feed it.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Motherboard मुख्य printed circuit board हो। अरू हरेक भाग या त यसमा जोडिन्छ या यससँग जडान हुन्छ, र तिनलाई कुरा गर्न दिने bus यसैले बोक्छ।

Form factor भनेको बोर्डको आकार, स्क्रु प्वालको स्थान, र पोर्ट तथा power connector कहाँ पर्ने भन्ने तय गर्ने मानक हो। ATX सामान्य डेस्कटप मानक हो; Micro-ATX र Mini-ITX कम slot भएका साना संस्करण हुन्।

Form factor कै कारण साट्ने बोर्ड "जुनसुकै बोर्ड" हुँदैन — यो अटाउने केस र बिजुली दिने पावर सप्लाई दुवैसँग मिल्नुपर्छ।

3.3  The PCI local bus

Definition — learn this wording

A bus is a set of parallel wires that carries data, addresses and control signals between components.

PCI (Peripheral Component Interconnect) is a local bus — "local" meaning it runs close to the processor and at high speed, rather than out on a slow peripheral path.

Its expansion slots take add-on cards: graphics, sound, network, additional ports. This is what makes a desktop upgradable — a capability the board does not have can be added as a card instead of replacing the whole machine.

PCI cards are also plug and play: the system detects the card and allocates the resources it needs, instead of the user setting jumpers by hand.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Bus भनेको भागहरूबीच data, ठेगाना र नियन्त्रण सङ्केत बोक्ने समानान्तर तारहरूको समूह हो।

PCI (Peripheral Component Interconnect) एउटा local bus हो — "local" भनेको यो ढिलो peripheral बाटोमा नभई प्रोसेसरको नजिक, उच्च गतिमा चल्छ।

यसका expansion slot ले थप कार्ड लिन्छन्: graphics, sound, network, थप पोर्ट। डेस्कटप अपग्रेड गर्न मिल्ने यसैले हुन्छ — बोर्डमा नभएको क्षमता पूरै मेसिन साट्नुको साटो कार्ड थपेर ल्याउन सकिन्छ।

PCI कार्ड plug and play पनि हुन्छन्: प्रयोगकर्ताले हातले jumper मिलाउनुपर्दैन, प्रणालीले कार्ड पत्ता लगाएर चाहिने साधन बाँडिदिन्छ।

3.4  The internal power supply

Definition — learn this wording

The SMPS (Switched Mode Power Supply) converts the AC mains supply into the low DC voltages the components need, and it must do so cleanly and steadily.

Its main parts: a transformer to change the voltage, a rectifier to convert AC into DC, a filter to smooth what is left of the ripple, a voltage regulator to hold each output steady as the load changes, and a cooling fan.

The outputs: +12 V for motors and the processor circuits, +5 V and +3.3 V for the board's logic and the drives.

A supply that is failing does not always die outright. It often produces voltages that are out of range under load, which shows up as random restarts and faults that move around — the hardest kind to diagnose.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

SMPS (Switched Mode Power Supply) ले मुख्य AC बिजुलीलाई भागहरूलाई चाहिने कम DC भोल्टेजमा बदल्छ, र त्यो सफा तथा स्थिर हुनुपर्छ।

यसका मुख्य भाग: भोल्टेज बदल्न transformer, AC लाई DC बनाउन rectifier, बाँकी ripple मिलाउन filter, भार बदलिँदा पनि हरेक आउटपुट स्थिर राख्न voltage regulator, र कुलिङ पंखा।

आउटपुट: मोटर र प्रोसेसर परिपथका लागि +१२ V, बोर्डको लजिक र ड्राइभका लागि +५ V र +३.३ V।

बिग्रँदै गएको सप्लाई सधैं एकैचोटि मर्दैन। भार पर्दा दायराबाहिरको भोल्टेज दिन्छ, जुन अनियमित रिस्टार्ट र ठाउँ बदलिरहने खराबीका रूपमा देखिन्छ — निदान गर्न सबैभन्दा गाह्रो किसिम।

3.5  Hard disk construction and operation

Definition — learn this wording

A hard disk drive stores data magnetically on rigid rotating platters.

Track — one concentric ring on a platter surface. Sector — one slice of a track, and the smallest unit that can be read or written. Cylinder — the same track number on every platter, taken together. Read-write head — mounted on an actuator arm, floating microns above the surface on a cushion of air.

Access time is made of three parts added together: seek time (the arm moves to the right track), rotational delay (the disk turns until the sector arrives), and transfer time (the data is read).

The head never touches the platter. When it does — from a knock while running — that is a head crash, and the damage is physical.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Hard disk drive ले घुम्ने कडा platter मा चुम्बकीय रूपमा data राख्छ।

Track — platter सतहको एउटा केन्द्रित रिङ। Sector — track को एउटा टुक्रा, र पढ्न–लेख्न मिल्ने सबैभन्दा सानो एकाइ। Cylinder — हरेक platter को उही नम्बरको track सँगै लिँदा। Read-write head — actuator arm मा जडित, हावाको तकियामा सतहभन्दा माइक्रोन मात्र माथि तैरिने।

Access time तीन भागको योग हो: seek time (arm ठीक track मा जाने), rotational delay (sector आइपुग्न डिस्क घुम्ने), र transfer time (data पढिने)।

Head ले platter छुँदैन। चलिरहेको बेला धक्का लागेर छोयो भने त्यो head crash हो, र क्षति भौतिक हुन्छ।

3.6  Partitioning and formatting

Definition — learn this wording

Partitioning divides one physical disk into separate areas that the operating system treats as independent drives.

A primary partition can be booted from. An extended partition is a container that holds logical drives; the extended partition itself gets no drive letter.

Drive lettering assigns C:, D:, E: and so on. Reasons to partition: separating the operating system from data so the OS can be reinstalled without losing files, running more than one OS, and keeping backups organised.

Formatting writes a file system onto a partition so files can be stored. Quick format clears the index only — the data is still physically there until it is overwritten. Full format also scans every sector for defects, which is why it takes far longer.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Partitioning ले एउटै भौतिक डिस्कलाई छुट्टाछुट्टै क्षेत्रमा बाँड्छ, जसलाई अपरेटिङ सिस्टमले स्वतन्त्र ड्राइभ मान्छ।

Primary partition बाट boot गर्न सकिन्छ। Extended partition भनेको logical drive राख्ने भाँडो हो; extended partition आफैँले ड्राइभ अक्षर पाउँदैन।

Drive lettering ले C:, D:, E: आदि दिन्छ। Partition गर्ने कारण: अपरेटिङ सिस्टमलाई data बाट छुट्याउनु ताकि फाइल नगुमाई OS फेरि इन्स्टल गर्न सकियोस्, एकभन्दा बढी OS चलाउनु, र ब्याकअप व्यवस्थित राख्नु।

Formatting ले partition मा file system लेख्छ ताकि फाइल राख्न सकियोस्। Quick format ले सूची मात्र मेटाउँछ — data नओभरराइट भएसम्म भौतिक रूपमा त्यहीँ हुन्छ। Full format ले हरेक sector मा खराबी पनि जाँच्छ, त्यसैले धेरै समय लाग्छ।

Example 1 Choosing a format before handing on a disk

Question: a school is donating old computers. A technician quick-formats the disks. Is the data safe from recovery?

Step 1 — what did the quick format actually do? It rewrote the file system index, so the drive reports itself as empty.

Step 2 — where is the data now? Still on the platters. Nothing overwrote the sectors that hold it; only the entries pointing at them were cleared.

Step 3 — so can it be read back? Yes. Recovery software scans sectors directly rather than trusting the index, and will find most of it.

Answer
No. A quick format does not remove data. Use a full format, or a wipe utility that overwrites every sector.

This is the practical reason the two format types are in the syllabus at all — the difference has consequences for privacy, not just for time taken. पाठ्यक्रममा दुई किसिमको format राखिनुको व्यावहारिक कारण यही हो — फरकले समय मात्र होइन, गोपनीयतामा पनि असर पार्छ।

Example 2 Working out a disk's capacity from its geometry

Question: a hard disk has 4 platter surfaces, 8192 cylinders, 63 sectors per track and 512 bytes per sector. What is its capacity?

Step 1 — what does one track hold? 63 sectors × 512 bytes = 32,256 bytes.

Step 2 — how many tracks are there in total? A cylinder is one track on every surface, so tracks = cylinders × surfaces = 8192 × 4 = 32,768 tracks.

Step 3 — multiply. 32,768 × 32,256 = 1,056,964,608 bytes.

Step 4 — convert. Divide by 1024 three times: ÷1024 = 1,032,192 KB, ÷1024 = 1008 MB, which is roughly 0.98 GB.

Answer
1,056,964,608 bytes ≈ 1008 MB ≈ 0.98 GB

Step 2 is where marks are lost. A cylinder is not one track — it is the same track number on every surface, so you must multiply by the number of surfaces. Miss that and your answer is four times too small. अंक चरण २ मै गुम्छ। Cylinder एउटा track होइन — हरेक सतहको उही नम्बरको track हो, त्यसैले सतह सङ्ख्याले गुणन गर्नैपर्छ। यो छुटे उत्तर चार गुणा सानो आउँछ।

Mistakes that cost marks here — यहाँ अङ्क गुमाउने गल्तीहरू

  • Saying the BIOS is stored on the hard disk. It is firmware on a chip on the motherboard — it has to run before any disk is read. BIOS hard disk मा हुन्छ भन्नु। यो मदरबोर्डको चिपमा रहेको firmware हो — कुनै disk पढिनुअघि नै यो चल्नुपर्छ।
  • Saying a quick format erases the data. It clears the index. The data stays on the platters until something overwrites it. Quick format ले data मेटाउँछ भन्नु। यसले सूची मात्र सफा गर्छ। कुनै कुराले नओभरराइट गरेसम्म data platter मै रहन्छ।
  • Treating a partition as a physical disk. C: and D: are very often two areas of one drive — if that drive fails, both go together. Partition लाई भौतिक डिस्क ठान्नु। C: र D: प्रायः एउटै ड्राइभका दुई क्षेत्र हुन् — ड्राइभ बिग्रिए दुवै सँगै जान्छन्।
  • Giving the extended partition a drive letter. It is a container. The logical drives inside it get the letters. Extended partition लाई ड्राइभ अक्षर दिनु। त्यो भाँडो हो। अक्षर त्यसभित्रका logical drive ले पाउँछन्।

Quick format hides the data; full format checks the disk. BIOS is on the board, not the disk. Quick format ले data लुकाउँछ; full format ले disk जाँच्छ। BIOS बोर्डमा हुन्छ, disk मा होइन।

Exam focus — what is asked from this unit — परीक्षा केन्द्रबिन्दु — यो युनिटबाट के सोधिन्छ

This is the heaviest unit for definitions — 12 hours and 9 marks — and it is where the practical exam gets its component questions. Expect list the functions of the BIOS (POST is always one of them), explain the construction and operation of a hard disk (track, sector, cylinder, head, and the three parts of access time), and differentiate questions on primary vs extended partition and quick vs full format. Be ready to label a motherboard diagram and to name the parts of an SMPS with its output voltages. परिभाषाका हिसाबले सबैभन्दा भारी युनिट यही हो — १२ घण्टा, ९ अंक — र व्यावहारिक परीक्षाका पार्ट्ससम्बन्धी प्रश्न यहीँबाट आउँछन्। BIOS का काम सूचीबद्ध गर्नुहोस् (POST सधैं एक हो), hard disk को बनावट र सञ्चालन व्याख्या गर्नुहोस् (track, sector, cylinder, head, र access time का तीन भाग), र primary–extended partition तथा quick–full format को फरक सोधिन्छ। मदरबोर्ड चित्रमा नाम लेख्न र SMPS का भाग तथा आउटपुट भोल्टेज भन्न तयार रहनुहोस्।

Q1 4 marks

What is BIOS? List its main functions. BIOS के हो? यसका मुख्य काम सूचीबद्ध गर्नुहोस्।

BIOS (Basic Input Output System) is firmware held on a chip on the motherboard, and it is the first program to run when the computer is switched on. Its main functions are: POST (Power-On Self Test), which checks the essential hardware and reports failures by beep codes; the bootstrap loader, which locates the operating system and loads it into memory; BIOS drivers, which give low-level control of basic hardware before the operating system's own drivers are available; and CMOS setup, which lets the user view and change hardware settings such as boot order and system time.

BIOS (Basic Input Output System) मदरबोर्डको चिपमा रहेको firmware हो, र कम्प्युटर खोल्दा सबैभन्दा पहिले चल्ने प्रोग्राम यही हो। यसका मुख्य काम: POST (Power-On Self Test), जसले आवश्यक हार्डवेयर जाँच्छ र बिग्रिएमा बीप कोडले सुनाउँछ; bootstrap loader, जसले अपरेटिङ सिस्टम खोजेर मेमोरीमा लोड गर्छ; BIOS drivers, जसले OS का आफ्नै driver उपलब्ध हुनुअघि आधारभूत हार्डवेयरको तल्लो तहको नियन्त्रण दिन्छ; र CMOS setup, जसबाट boot order र समयजस्ता सेटिङ हेर्न र बदल्न मिल्छ।

Q2 3 marks

Define track, sector and cylinder. State the three components of access time. Track, sector र cylinder को परिभाषा दिनुहोस्। Access time का तीन भाग लेख्नुहोस्।

A track is one concentric ring on the surface of a platter. A sector is one slice of a track and is the smallest unit that can be read or written. A cylinder is the set of tracks of the same number on all the platters taken together. Access time = seek time (moving the head to the correct track) + rotational delay (waiting for the sector to come round) + transfer time (reading or writing the data).

Track भनेको platter को सतहमा भएको एउटा केन्द्रित रिङ हो। Sector भनेको track को एउटा टुक्रा र पढ्न–लेख्न मिल्ने सबैभन्दा सानो एकाइ हो। Cylinder भनेको सबै platter का उही नम्बरका track सँगै लिँदा बन्ने समूह हो। Access time = seek time (head लाई ठीक track मा लाने) + rotational delay (sector आइपुग्न पर्खने) + transfer time (data पढ्ने वा लेख्ने)।

Q3 2 marks

Differentiate between quick formatting and full formatting. Quick formatting र full formatting बीचको फरक छुट्याउनुहोस्।

A quick format only rewrites the file system index, so the partition reports itself as empty while the data physically remains on the disk until overwritten; it takes a few seconds. A full format also scans every sector for defects and marks any bad ones so they are not used, which takes much longer but gives a genuine check of the disk's condition.

Quick format ले file system को सूची मात्र फेरि लेख्छ, त्यसैले partition खाली देखिन्छ तर data नओभरराइट भएसम्म भौतिक रूपमा डिस्कमै रहन्छ; केही सेकेन्ड लाग्छ। Full format ले हरेक sector मा खराबी पनि जाँच्छ र बिग्रिएका sector चिन्ह लगाएर प्रयोग नगर्ने बनाउँछ — समय धेरै लाग्छ तर डिस्कको अवस्थाको साँचो जाँच हुन्छ।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • BIOS is firmware on a motherboard chip. Its functions: POST, bootstrap loader, BIOS drivers, CMOS setup. BIOS मदरबोर्डको चिपमा रहेको firmware हो। काम: POST, bootstrap loader, BIOS drivers, CMOS setup।
  • A form factor fixes a board's size, mounting holes and port positions — ATX, Micro-ATX, Mini-ITX. Form factor ले बोर्डको आकार, स्क्रु प्वाल र पोर्टको स्थान तय गर्छ — ATX, Micro-ATX, Mini-ITX।
  • PCI is a local bus with expansion slots for add-on cards, and it is plug and play. PCI एउटा local bus हो, जसका expansion slot मा थप कार्ड राखिन्छ, र यो plug and play हो।
  • An SMPS has a transformer, rectifier, filter, regulator and fan, and outputs +12 V, +5 V and +3.3 V. SMPS मा transformer, rectifier, filter, regulator र पंखा हुन्छन्, र +१२ V, +५ V तथा +३.३ V दिन्छ।
  • Access time = seek time + rotational delay + transfer time. Access time = seek time + rotational delay + transfer time।
  • Primary partitions boot; an extended partition holds logical drives and gets no letter itself. Quick format clears the index; full format also checks every sector. Primary partition बाट boot हुन्छ; extended partition ले logical drive राख्छ र आफैँ अक्षर पाउँदैन। Quick format ले सूची सफा गर्छ; full format ले हरेक sector पनि जाँच्छ।
