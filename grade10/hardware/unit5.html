Repair and Maintenance

5.1  Preventive maintenance

Definition — learn this wording

Preventive maintenance is work done on a system that is still working, to stop failures before they happen and to extend the equipment's life.

Each task exists because of a specific failure it prevents — that pairing is what the exam wants, not the list on its own:

Cleaning dust from vents, fans and heatsinks — prevents overheating and the sudden shutdowns it causes.

Checking that all cables and cards are firmly seated — prevents intermittent faults that are very hard to trace.

Deleting temporary files and defragmenting the disk — prevents slowdowns and the disk filling up.

Updating the operating system and antivirus — closes the security holes malware uses to get in.

Taking regular backups — the only preventive measure that works after the hardware has already failed.

Using a UPS and keeping the machine in a cool, dry, ventilated place — prevents damage from power problems, heat and humidity.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Preventive maintenance भनेको अझै चलिरहेको प्रणालीमा गरिने काम हो, जसले खराबी हुनुअघि नै रोक्छ र उपकरणको आयु बढाउँछ।

हरेक काम कुनै निश्चित खराबी रोक्न छ — परीक्षाले खोज्ने त्यही जोडी हो, एक्लो सूची होइन:

प्वाल, पंखा र heatsink को धुलो सफा गर्ने — तात्ने समस्या र त्यसले ल्याउने अचानक बन्द हुने अवस्था रोक्छ।

सबै केबल र कार्ड कसिएका छन् कि जाँच्ने — पत्ता लगाउन गाह्रो हुने रुकिरुकी आउने खराबी रोक्छ।

अस्थायी फाइल मेटाउने र डिस्क defragment गर्ने — ढिलो हुने र डिस्क भरिने समस्या रोक्छ।

अपरेटिङ सिस्टम र antivirus अपडेट गर्ने — malware भित्रिने सुरक्षा प्वाल बन्द गर्छ।

नियमित ब्याकअप लिने — हार्डवेयर बिग्रिसकेपछि पनि काम लाग्ने एक मात्र निरोधात्मक उपाय।

UPS प्रयोग गर्ने र मेसिन चिसो, सुक्खा, हावा खेल्ने ठाउँमा राख्ने — बिजुली समस्या, ताप र आर्द्रताबाट हुने क्षति रोक्छ।

5.2  Power source and power protection

Definition — learn this wording

Four things can go wrong with mains power, and a different device protects against each:

Surge / spike — a brief, very large rise in voltage, often from lightning or heavy machinery switching off. Protected by a surge protector, which diverts the excess.

Sag / brownout — voltage that drops below normal but does not disappear. It can make a system unstable and restart without warning. Protected by a voltage stabiliser.

Blackout — power fails completely. Only a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) helps, because it holds a battery that carries the machine long enough to save work and shut down properly.

Noise — small unwanted variations riding on the supply. Filtered by a UPS or a conditioner.

A UPS is not a generator. It buys minutes, not hours, and its purpose is a clean shutdown.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

मुख्य बिजुलीमा चार किसिमका समस्या आउँछन्, र हरेकबाट फरक उपकरणले जोगाउँछ:

Surge / spike — छोटो तर धेरै ठूलो भोल्टेज बढाइ, प्रायः चट्याङ वा ठूलो मेसिन बन्द हुँदा। Surge protector ले जोगाउँछ, जसले बढी बिजुली अन्तै पठाउँछ।

Sag / brownout — भोल्टेज सामान्यभन्दा तल झर्नु, तर पूरै नजानु। प्रणाली अस्थिर बनाउँछ र चेतावनीबिनै रिस्टार्ट गराउँछ। Voltage stabiliser ले जोगाउँछ।

Blackout — बिजुली पूरै जानु। UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) ले मात्र काम गर्छ, किनकि यसमा ब्याट्री हुन्छ जसले काम बचाएर ठीकसँग बन्द गर्न पुग्ने बेर मेसिन चलाइराख्छ।

Noise — सप्लाईमै मिसिएर आउने साना अवाञ्छित उतारचढाव। UPS वा conditioner ले छान्छ।

UPS जेनेरेटर होइन। यसले घण्टा होइन, मिनेट दिन्छ, र यसको उद्देश्य सफासँग बन्द गर्नु हो।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A school has frequent power cuts and loses unsaved work every time. Which device solves the problem? एउटा विद्यालयमा बारम्बार बिजुली जान्छ र हरेक पटक नबचाइएको काम हराउँछ। कुन उपकरणले यो समस्या समाधान गर्छ?

Read what the problem actually is: the power disappears. A surge protector handles voltage that is too high and a stabiliser handles voltage that is too low — neither can supply power that is not there. Only a UPS carries its own battery, and that is why it is the only one of the three that helps in a blackout.

समस्या के हो पढ्नुहोस्: बिजुली हराउँछ। Surge protector ले धेरै बढी भोल्टेज सम्हाल्छ र stabiliser ले धेरै कम — नभएको बिजुली दुवैले दिन सक्दैनन्। UPS सँग मात्र आफ्नै ब्याट्री हुन्छ, र तीनमध्ये blackout मा काम लाग्ने एक मात्र यही हुनुको कारण त्यही हो।

5.3  Cooling, ventilation and processor power

Definition — learn this wording

A processor turns electrical power into work and heat. If the heat is not carried away as fast as it is produced, the temperature climbs until the board shuts the machine down to protect the chip.

How a processor is cooled: a heatsink of metal fins sits on the chip, with a thin layer of thermal paste filling the microscopic gaps so heat crosses into it. A fan moves air through the fins. The case provides the path: cool air in at the front, warm air out at the back.

Processor power and voltage: the board supplies the processor through its own regulator at a low, precisely held voltage. Too low and the system is unstable; too high and the chip is damaged. This is why overclocking without care destroys processors.

Thermal shutdown is protection, not a fault. A machine that turns itself off when hot is working correctly — the fault is whatever is stopping the heat leaving.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

प्रोसेसरले विद्युतीय शक्तिलाई काम र ताप मा बदल्छ। उत्पादन भएको गतिमै ताप बाहिर नगए तापक्रम बढ्दै जान्छ र चिप जोगाउन बोर्डले मेसिन बन्द गरिदिन्छ।

प्रोसेसर कसरी चिसो पारिन्छ: चिपमाथि धातुका पत्र भएको heatsink राखिन्छ, र सूक्ष्म खाली ठाउँ भर्न पातलो thermal paste लगाइन्छ ताकि ताप त्यसमा सरोस्। पंखा ले पत्रबीच हावा चलाउँछ। केस ले बाटो दिन्छ: अगाडिबाट चिसो हावा भित्र, पछाडिबाट न्यानो हावा बाहिर।

प्रोसेसरको बिजुली र भोल्टेज: बोर्डले आफ्नै regulator मार्फत प्रोसेसरलाई कम र ठ्याक्कै स्थिर भोल्टेज दिन्छ। कम भए प्रणाली अस्थिर हुन्छ; बढी भए चिप बिग्रिन्छ। ध्यान नपुर्‍याई overclock गर्दा प्रोसेसर नष्ट हुनुको कारण यही हो।

तापका कारण बन्द हुनु सुरक्षा हो, खराबी होइन। तातेपछि आफैँ बन्द हुने मेसिन ठीकसँग चलिरहेको छ — ताप बाहिर जान नदिने जुनसुकै कुरा नै खराबी हो।

Diagnose it yourself · आफैँ निदान गर्नुहोस्

The fault lab — it restarts by itself after a while

What you will learn: to use the timing of a fault as evidence. This scenario cannot be solved by naming a part — it is solved by noticing when the failure happens and what that rules out. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: खराबीको समय लाई प्रमाणका रूपमा प्रयोग गर्न। यो अवस्था पार्टको नाम भनेर समाधान हुँदैन — कहिले बिग्रिन्छ र त्यसले के हटाउँछ भनी ध्यान दिएर हुन्छ।

Why this matters: a fault that appears only after the machine warms up is one of the few whose cause is written in its timing. Students who never learn to read that will replace a processor to fix a dusty heatsink.किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ: मेसिन तातेपछि मात्र देखिने खराबी ती थोरैमध्ये एक हो जसको कारण समयमै लेखिएको हुन्छ। यो पढ्न नसिकेका विद्यार्थीले धुलो भरिएको heatsink मिलाउन प्रोसेसरै साट्छन्।

What you will learn: to choose a port from what is being connected rather than from the shape of the plug — because several connectors physically fit sockets they do not belong in, and "it fits" is the reasoning that produces a device that never works. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: प्लगको आकार होइन, के जोड्दै हुनुहुन्छ हेरेर पोर्ट छान्न — किनकि धेरै connector नमिल्ने सकेटमा पनि भौतिक रूपमा अटाउँछन्, र "अटायो" भन्ने तर्कले नै कहिल्यै नचल्ने उपकरण बनाउँछ।

Both "fit", both fail, and neither gives an error message.किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ: यी चारमध्ये दुई त्यस्ता खराबी हुन् जुन टेक्निसियनले बारम्बार भेट्छ — RJ-45 सकेटमा खुकुलो बसेको RJ-11 प्लग, र माइक्रोफोन ज्याकमा घुसाइएको हेडफोन। दुवै "अटाउँछन्", दुवै चल्दैनन्, र कुनैले त्रुटि सन्देश दिँदैन।

5.5  Wireless connection problems

In this order

1. Is the adapter switched on? Many laptops have a physical switch or a keyboard shortcut, and flight mode disables it entirely. This is the commonest cause and the fastest to check.

2. Is the network visible? If no networks appear at all, the fault is the adapter or its driver. If other networks appear but yours does not, the fault is at the router.

3. Does it connect but not reach the internet? Then the wireless link is fine and the problem is beyond the router — the connection to the provider.

4. Is the signal weak? Distance, thick walls and interference from other equipment all reduce it. Moving closer is a diagnostic step, not just a workaround.

5. Is it the password or the security setting? A wrong key gives a connection that keeps dropping and reconnecting rather than a clean failure.

यही क्रममा

१. Adapter खुला छ? धेरै ल्यापटपमा भौतिक स्विच वा किबोर्ड सर्टकट हुन्छ, र flight mode ले पूरै बन्द गर्छ। सबैभन्दा सामान्य कारण र सबैभन्दा छिटो जाँच यही हो।

२. नेटवर्क देखिन्छ? कुनै पनि नेटवर्क नदेखिए खराबी adapter वा यसको driver मा छ। अरू देखिने तर आफ्नो नदेखिने भए खराबी router मा छ।

३. जोडिन्छ तर इन्टरनेट आउँदैन? त्यसो भए वायरलेस जडान ठीक छ र समस्या router भन्दा पर — सेवा प्रदायकसँगको जडानमा छ।

४. सिग्नल कमजोर छ? दूरी, बाक्लो भित्ता र अरू उपकरणको हस्तक्षेपले घटाउँछ। नजिक सर्नु समाधान मात्र होइन, निदानको एउटा चरण पनि हो।

५. पासवर्ड वा security सेटिङ हो? गलत key ले सफासँग असफल हुनुको साटो बारम्बार जोडिने र छुट्ने जडान दिन्छ।

5.6  Viruses — detection, protection and prevention

Definition — learn this wording

A computer virus is a malicious program that attaches itself to another program or file, and copies itself to other files or computers when that host is run.

How infection spreads: infected USB drives and external media; email attachments; downloads from untrusted sites; pirated software; and network shares.

Signs of infection: the computer slows down for no clear reason; files disappear, change size or will not open; programs start or crash on their own; unexpected messages appear; free disk space falls quickly; the antivirus has been disabled.

Detection and protection: install antivirus software and keep it updated — an out-of-date scanner cannot recognise a new virus. Scan removable media before opening it. Enable a firewall. Keep the operating system patched.

Prevention: do not open attachments from unknown senders, do not install pirated software, disable autorun on removable media, and take regular backups — the last defence when everything else has failed.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Computer virus भनेको अर्को प्रोग्राम वा फाइलमा टाँसिने र त्यो चल्दा आफूलाई अन्य फाइल वा कम्प्युटरमा सार्ने हानिकारक प्रोग्राम हो।

कसरी सर्छ: सङ्क्रमित USB र बाहिरी मिडिया; इमेल attachment; अविश्वसनीय साइटबाट डाउनलोड; चोरी सफ्टवेयर; र नेटवर्क share।

सङ्क्रमणका लक्षण: स्पष्ट कारणबिनै कम्प्युटर ढिलो हुनु; फाइल हराउनु, आकार बदलिनु वा नखुल्नु; प्रोग्राम आफैँ खुल्नु वा बन्द हुनु; अनपेक्षित सन्देश देखिनु; खाली ठाउँ छिट्टै घट्नु; antivirus बन्द भइदिनु।

पत्ता लगाउने र जोगाउने: antivirus राख्नुहोस् र अपडेट गरिराख्नुहोस् — पुरानो स्क्यानरले नयाँ भाइरस चिन्न सक्दैन। बाहिरी मिडिया खोल्नुअघि स्क्यान गर्नुहोस्। Firewall खोल्नुहोस्। अपरेटिङ सिस्टम अपडेट राख्नुहोस्।

रोकथाम: नचिनेको पठाउनेको attachment नखोल्नुहोस्, चोरी सफ्टवेयर नराख्नुहोस्, हटाउन मिल्ने मिडियाको autorun बन्द गर्नुहोस्, र नियमित ब्याकअप लिनुहोस् — सबै असफल भएपछिको अन्तिम रक्षा यही हो।

Practise the judgement · निर्णय अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्

Which power problem is this, and what protects against it?

What you will learn: to match a described power event to the right protective device — which is exactly how this topic is examined, rather than as four definitions in a row. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: वर्णन गरिएको बिजुली घटनालाई ठीक सुरक्षा उपकरणसँग मिलाउन — यो विषय चार परिभाषा लहरै सोधेर होइन, ठ्याक्कै यसै गरी जाँचिन्छ।

Why this matters: choosing the wrong protective device is an expensive mistake in the real world. A surge protector on a site with frequent cuts protects against the one problem that site does not have.किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ: गलत सुरक्षा उपकरण छान्नु वास्तविक जीवनमा महँगो गल्ती हो। बारम्बार बिजुली जाने ठाउँमा surge protector राख्नु भनेको त्यहाँ नभएकै समस्याबाट जोगिनु हो।

Example 1 Choosing power protection for a real site

Question: a school computer lab has three problems: the voltage drops noticeably at peak hours, the power fails completely two or three times a week, and there are lightning storms in the monsoon. What do you install, and why?

Three problems are listed. Decide how many devices are actually needed before you read on.तीन समस्या लेखिएका छन्। पढ्नुअघि साँच्चै कति उपकरण चाहिन्छ निर्णय गर्नुहोस्।

Step 1 — name each problem. Voltage dropping at peak hours is a sag. Power failing completely is a blackout. Lightning is a surge.

Step 2 — match a device to each. Sag needs a stabiliser. Blackout needs a UPS. Surge needs a surge protector.

Step 3 — check for overlap before buying three things. A UPS already provides surge protection and filtering, and most units regulate voltage within a range as well. So a UPS with built-in voltage regulation covers all three.

Step 4 — state what it does not cover. A UPS gives minutes, not hours. If the cuts last longer than the battery, its job is a clean shutdown, and a generator is a separate decision.

Answer
A UPS with built-in voltage regulation — it answers all three, and only the blackout genuinely required a battery.

The examiner is testing whether you match the device to the problem. Recommending a surge protector for a site whose real complaint is power cuts protects against the one thing that site did not report. परीक्षकले तपाईंले उपकरणलाई समस्या सँग मिलाउनुहुन्छ कि हेर्छ। बिजुली जाने गुनासो भएको ठाउँमा surge protector सिफारिस गर्नु भनेको त्यहाँ नभनिएकै कुराबाट जोगाउनु हो।

Example 2 A monitor problem, narrowed in three checks

Question: a user reports that their screen "looks wrong". On inspection the picture is blurry, slightly stretched, and there is a faint pink tint on the left side. How do you proceed?

Step 1 — separate the complaints. There are two independent symptoms here: geometry (blurry and stretched) and colour (a pink tint). Treating them as one fault is what makes this look hard.

Step 2 — deal with the geometry first, because it costs nothing. Blurry and stretched on a flat panel means the resolution is not the monitor's native value. Set the recommended resolution; the picture becomes sharp and the correct shape.

Step 3 — the tint remains, so it is a separate fault. A colour cast confined to one area of the screen points at the signal, not the settings. Check the video cable for a bent or pushed-in pin, and reseat it at both ends.

Step 4 — confirm. One pin was not making contact. With the cable reseated the tint goes. Verify both symptoms are gone before closing the job.

Answer
Two faults, not one: wrong resolution (a setting) and a video cable pin (hardware). Fixing either alone would have left the user still complaining.

When a user reports several things at once, count the faults before you start. A "fix" that clears two of three symptoms is reported back as "it is still broken". प्रयोगकर्ताले एकैचोटि धेरै कुरा भन्दा सुरु गर्नुअघि खराबी कति छन् गन्नुहोस्। तीनमध्ये दुई लक्षण हटाउने "समाधान" फेरि "अझै बिग्रेकै छ" भनेर फर्किन्छ।

Mistakes that cost marks here — यहाँ अङ्क गुमाउने गल्तीहरू

  • Calling a thermal shutdown a fault. The shutdown is the protection working. The fault is the blocked airflow, the stopped fan or the dried thermal paste. तापले बन्द हुनुलाई खराबी भन्नु। बन्द हुनु सुरक्षा चलेको हो। खराबी भनेको रोकिएको हावा, नचल्ने पंखा वा सुकेको thermal paste हो।
  • Saying a surge protector protects against power cuts. It handles voltage that is too high. Only a UPS has a battery, so only a UPS helps in a blackout. Surge protector ले बिजुली जानुबाट जोगाउँछ भन्नु। यसले बढी भोल्टेज सम्हाल्छ। ब्याट्री UPS मा मात्र हुन्छ, त्यसैले blackout मा UPS मात्र काम लाग्छ।
  • Writing "install antivirus" without "and update it". An antivirus that is never updated cannot recognise anything released after it was installed. "अपडेट गर्ने" नलेखी "antivirus राख्ने" मात्र लेख्नु। कहिल्यै अपडेट नगरिएको antivirus ले इन्स्टल भएपछि आएको कुनै पनि कुरा चिन्न सक्दैन।
  • Cleaning inside a machine with a wet cloth or a household vacuum. Use dry compressed air. Moisture and static both destroy components. मेसिनभित्र भिजेको कपडा वा घरायसी भ्याकुमले सफा गर्नु। सुक्खा कम्प्रेस्ड एयर प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्। चिस्यान र static दुवैले पार्ट नष्ट गर्छन्।

Shutdown is the protection, not the fault. Only a UPS has a battery. बन्द हुनु सुरक्षा हो, खराबी होइन। ब्याट्री UPS मा मात्र हुन्छ।

Exam focus — what is asked from this unit — परीक्षा केन्द्रबिन्दु — यो युनिटबाट के सोधिन्छ

This unit is the widest in the subject — eleven topics for 9 marks — so questions are short and drawn from anywhere in it. The reliable ones are: list preventive maintenance tasks (pair each with the failure it prevents and you answer a 4-mark question with a 3-mark list), differentiate UPS, surge protector and voltage stabiliser, what is a computer virus and how do you prevent infection, and why must a processor be cooled. Expect at least one symptom-to-cause question drawn from the display and connection faults. यो विषयकै सबैभन्दा फराकिलो युनिट हो — ९ अंकका लागि एघार विषय — त्यसैले प्रश्न छोटा र जताबाट पनि आउन सक्ने हुन्छन्। भरपर्दा प्रश्न: निरोधात्मक सम्भारका काम सूचीबद्ध गर्नुहोस् (हरेकसँग रोकिने खराबी जोड्नुभयो भने ३ अंकको सूचीले ४ अंकको प्रश्न उत्तर हुन्छ), UPS, surge protector र voltage stabiliser छुट्याउनुहोस्, कम्प्युटर भाइरस के हो र सङ्क्रमण कसरी रोक्ने, र प्रोसेसर किन चिसो पार्नुपर्छ। Display र जडान खराबीबाट कम्तीमा एउटा लक्षण–कारण प्रश्न आउने अपेक्षा गर्नुहोस्।

Q1 4 marks

What is preventive maintenance? List any four tasks and state what each one prevents. Preventive maintenance के हो? कुनै चार काम सूचीबद्ध गर्नुहोस् र हरेकले के रोक्छ भन्नुहोस्।

Preventive maintenance is work carried out on a system that is still functioning, in order to prevent failures and extend equipment life. Cleaning dust from vents, fans and heatsinks prevents overheating and unexpected shutdowns. Checking that cables and cards are firmly seated prevents intermittent faults. Deleting temporary files and defragmenting the disk prevents slowdowns and the disk filling. Updating the operating system and antivirus closes the security holes malware uses. Taking regular backups prevents permanent data loss when hardware eventually fails.

Preventive maintenance भनेको अझै चलिरहेको प्रणालीमा खराबी रोक्न र उपकरणको आयु बढाउन गरिने काम हो। प्वाल, पंखा र heatsink को धुलो सफा गर्दा तात्ने र अचानक बन्द हुने समस्या रोकिन्छ। केबल र कार्ड कसिएका छन् कि जाँच्दा रुकिरुकी आउने खराबी रोकिन्छ। अस्थायी फाइल मेटाउँदा र डिस्क defragment गर्दा ढिलो हुने र डिस्क भरिने समस्या रोकिन्छ। OS र antivirus अपडेट गर्दा malware भित्रिने प्वाल बन्द हुन्छ। नियमित ब्याकअपले हार्डवेयर बिग्रिँदा हुने स्थायी data क्षति रोक्छ।

Q2 3 marks

Differentiate between a UPS, a surge protector and a voltage stabiliser. UPS, surge protector र voltage stabiliser बीचको फरक छुट्याउनुहोस्।

A surge protector guards against a surge or spike — a brief, very high rise in voltage — by diverting the excess away from the equipment. A voltage stabiliser guards against a sag or brownout by holding the output steady when the incoming voltage drops below normal. A UPS contains a battery and is the only one of the three that can supply power during a complete blackout, giving enough time to save work and shut down properly; it also filters noise.

Surge protector ले surge वा spike — छोटो तर धेरै ठूलो भोल्टेज बढाइ — बाट जोगाउँछ, बढी बिजुली उपकरणबाट अन्तै पठाएर। Voltage stabiliser ले आउने भोल्टेज सामान्यभन्दा तल झर्दा आउटपुट स्थिर राखेर sag वा brownout बाट जोगाउँछ। UPS मा ब्याट्री हुन्छ र पूरै blackout मा बिजुली दिन सक्ने तीनमध्ये एक मात्र यही हो, जसले काम बचाएर ठीकसँग बन्द गर्न पुग्ने समय दिन्छ; यसले noise पनि छान्छ।

Q3 2 marks

What is a computer virus? State any three ways of preventing infection. Computer virus के हो? सङ्क्रमण रोक्ने कुनै तीन उपाय लेख्नुहोस्।

A computer virus is a malicious program that attaches itself to another program or file and copies itself to other files or computers when the host is run. Prevention: install antivirus software and keep it updated; scan USB drives and other removable media before opening them; do not open email attachments from unknown senders; avoid pirated software; disable autorun; and keep regular backups.

Computer virus भनेको अर्को प्रोग्राम वा फाइलमा टाँसिने र त्यो चल्दा आफूलाई अन्य फाइल वा कम्प्युटरमा सार्ने हानिकारक प्रोग्राम हो। रोकथाम: antivirus राख्ने र अपडेट गरिराख्ने; USB र अन्य मिडिया खोल्नुअघि स्क्यान गर्ने; नचिनेकाको इमेल attachment नखोल्ने; चोरी सफ्टवेयर नचलाउने; autorun बन्द गर्ने; र नियमित ब्याकअप राख्ने।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • Preventive maintenance is done while the system still works. Pair each task with the failure it prevents. Preventive maintenance प्रणाली चलिरहेकै बेला गरिन्छ। हरेक कामसँग त्यसले रोक्ने खराबी जोड्नुहोस्।
  • Surge = too high, sag = too low, blackout = none. Only a UPS has a battery. Surge = धेरै बढी, sag = धेरै कम, blackout = बिल्कुलै छैन। ब्याट्री UPS मा मात्र।
  • A processor needs a heatsink, thermal paste and a fan, and the case needs a clear front-to-back airflow path. Thermal shutdown is protection working correctly. प्रोसेसरलाई heatsink, thermal paste र पंखा चाहिन्छ, र केसमा अगाडिदेखि पछाडिसम्म हावाको खुला बाटो। तापले बन्द हुनु सुरक्षा ठीकसँग चलेको हो।
  • Blurry screen → wrong resolution. Flicker → low refresh rate. Artefacts → video card overheating. धमिलो स्क्रिन → गलत resolution। झिमझिम → कम refresh rate। बिग्रिएका आकार → video card तात्दै।
  • A virus attaches to a host and spreads when it runs. Antivirus only works if it is kept updated, and backups are the last defence. भाइरस कुनै host मा टाँसिन्छ र त्यो चल्दा सर्छ। Antivirus अपडेट गरे मात्र काम लाग्छ, र ब्याकअप अन्तिम रक्षा हो।
