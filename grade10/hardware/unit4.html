4.1  General troubleshooting techniques

The principles a technician works by

Check the simplest and cheapest thing first. A loose cable costs five seconds to rule out; an opened case costs twenty minutes and a risk of damage.

Change one thing at a time. Change two and the fault goes away, you no longer know which change fixed it — and you cannot prevent it happening again.

Work from the symptom, not from a hunch. The symptom already tells you how far the machine got before it stopped, which rules out everything that happens later.

Do not replace parts to diagnose. Substituting a known-good part is a legitimate test; buying a new one because you suspect it is not.

Observe safety. Power off and unplug before opening a case. Never work inside a power supply or a CRT monitor — both store dangerous charge after being unplugged.

टेक्निसियनले पालना गर्ने सिद्धान्त

सबैभन्दा सजिलो र सस्तो कुरा पहिले जाँच्नुहोस्। खुकुलो केबल हटाउन पाँच सेकेन्ड लाग्छ; केस खोल्न बीस मिनेट र बिगार्ने जोखिम।

एकपटकमा एउटा मात्र कुरा बदल्नुहोस्। दुई बदल्दा खराबी हट्यो भने कुनले मिलायो थाहा हुँदैन — र फेरि हुन नदिन पनि सकिँदैन।

अनुमानबाट होइन, लक्षणबाट काम गर्नुहोस्। मेसिन रोकिनुअघि कहाँसम्म पुगेको थियो भन्ने लक्षणले नै बताउँछ, र त्यसपछि हुने सबै कुरा हट्छ।

निदानका लागि पार्ट नसाट्नुहोस्। चल्ने भनेर थाहा भएको पार्ट राखेर हेर्नु वैध परीक्षण हो; शंकाकै भरमा नयाँ किन्नु होइन।

सुरक्षा अपनाउनुहोस्। केस खोल्नुअघि बिजुली बन्द गरी प्लग निकाल्नुहोस्। पावर सप्लाई र CRT monitor भित्र कहिल्यै काम नगर्नुहोस् — प्लग निकालेपछि पनि दुवैमा खतरनाक चार्ज जम्मा रहन्छ।

4.2  The steps of troubleshooting

What each step produces

1. Identify the problem — a precise statement of what happens and when. "It does not work" is not one; "it powers on, fans spin, screen stays black" is.

2. Gather information — ask the user what changed. New hardware, a storm, a move, a spill, an update. Most faults have a cause the user can tell you about.

3. Establish a theory of probable cause — the most likely cause consistent with the symptom, not every possible cause.

4. Test the theory — one check, one change. If the test disproves the theory, go back to step 3 and form a new one. Do not start swapping parts.

5. Apply the fix and verify — confirm the original symptom is actually gone, and that nothing else broke while you were working.

6. Document — what the symptom was, what caused it, what fixed it. This is what makes the second occurrence quick.

हरेक चरणले के दिन्छ

१. समस्या पहिचान — के हुन्छ र कहिले हुन्छ भन्ने ठ्याक्कै कथन। "चल्दैन" कथन होइन; "बिजुली आउँछ, पंखा घुम्छ, स्क्रिन कालै रहन्छ" कथन हो।

२. जानकारी सङ्कलन — प्रयोगकर्तालाई के बदलियो सोध्नुहोस्। नयाँ हार्डवेयर, चट्याङ, सारेको, पानी खसेको, अपडेट। धेरैजसो खराबीको कारण प्रयोगकर्ताले नै बताउन सक्छ।

३. सम्भावित कारणको अनुमान बनाउनुहोस् — लक्षणसँग मिल्ने सबैभन्दा सम्भावित कारण, हरेक सम्भावित कारण होइन।

४. अनुमान परीक्षण — एउटा जाँच, एउटा परिवर्तन। परीक्षणले अनुमान गलत साबित गरे चरण ३ मा फर्किनुहोस् र नयाँ बनाउनुहोस्। पार्ट साट्न नथाल्नुहोस्।

५. समाधान लागू गरी पुष्टि — सुरुको लक्षण साँच्चै हट्यो कि, र काम गर्दा अरू केही बिग्रियो कि जाँच्नुहोस्।

६. अभिलेख — लक्षण के थियो, कारण के थियो, केले मिलायो। दोस्रो पटक छिटो हुनुको कारण यही हो।

4.3  Troubleshooting boot problems

Read how far it got

A boot problem is diagnosed by asking how far the machine reached before it stopped. Each stage rules out everything after it.

Nothing at all — no lights, no fans. Power is not arriving: mains, cable, switch, power supply.

Fans spin, screen black, beeps — power is fine and POST is running. The BIOS found a fault before video was available; memory is the usual cause.

Manufacturer logo, then an error message — POST passed. The hardware is working and the problem is with finding or loading the operating system.

OS starts, then fails — hardware and boot sector are fine. The fault is in the operating system, a driver, or software.

कहाँसम्म पुग्यो पढ्नुहोस्

Boot समस्याको निदान मेसिन रोकिनुअघि कहाँसम्म पुग्यो भनेर गरिन्छ। हरेक चरणले त्यसपछिका सबै सम्भावना हटाउँछ।

केही पनि छैन — बत्ती छैन, पंखा छैन। बिजुली नै आइरहेको छैन: मुख्य लाइन, केबल, स्विच, पावर सप्लाई।

पंखा घुम्छ, स्क्रिन कालो, बीप — बिजुली ठीक छ र POST चलिरहेको छ। Video उपलब्ध हुनुअघि नै BIOS ले खराबी भेट्यो; प्रायः मेमोरी कारण हुन्छ।

कम्पनीको लोगो, अनि त्रुटि सन्देश — POST पास भयो। हार्डवेयर चलिरहेको छ र समस्या अपरेटिङ सिस्टम भेट्न वा लोड गर्नमा छ।

OS सुरु हुन्छ, अनि बिग्रिन्छ — हार्डवेयर र boot sector ठीक छन्। खराबी अपरेटिङ सिस्टम, driver वा सफ्टवेयरमा छ।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A machine powers on, the fans spin, and it beeps repeatedly with a completely black screen. Which part of the boot has it reached? मेसिनमा बिजुली आउँछ, पंखा घुम्छ, र पूरै कालो स्क्रिनसहित बारम्बार बीप गर्छ। Boot को कुन चरणसम्म पुगेको हो?

Beeping is the POST reporting. It only happens once power has reached the board and the BIOS is running — so the power supply, the switch and the mains are all already ruled out. And it is beeping rather than displaying because the fault was found before video was initialised, which is why memory is the first suspect. The operating system is nowhere near loading yet.

बीप गर्नु नै POST ले सुनाएको हो। बोर्डसम्म बिजुली पुगेर BIOS चलेपछि मात्र यो हुन्छ — त्यसैले पावर सप्लाई, स्विच र मुख्य लाइन सबै पहिल्यै हट्छन्। र देखाउनुको साटो बीप गर्नुको कारण खराबी video सुरु हुनअघि भेटियो — त्यसैले पहिलो शंका मेमोरीमा जान्छ। अपरेटिङ सिस्टम लोड हुने अवस्था नजिकै छैन।

The fault lab — powers on, no display

4.5  Troubleshooting system slowdowns

The usual causes, and how to tell them apart

Too little RAM — the machine slows badly when several programs are open, and the disk light stays on as it swaps memory to disk. Closing programs helps immediately.

Too many startup programs — slow from the moment it boots, and slow at everything rather than at one task.

A nearly full or fragmented disk — everything involving files is slow. There is no room left for temporary files and virtual memory.

Malware — slow with no obvious cause, often with network activity when nothing is open, and often appearing suddenly rather than gradually.

Overheating — fast at first, then slow after a while, because the processor deliberately reduces its speed to protect itself. The timing is the clue.

सामान्य कारण, र छुट्याउने तरिका

RAM कम — धेरै प्रोग्राम खोल्दा एकदम ढिलो हुन्छ, र मेमोरी डिस्कमा सार्दा डिस्कको बत्ती बलिरहन्छ। प्रोग्राम बन्द गर्दा तुरुन्तै सहज हुन्छ।

Startup मा धेरै प्रोग्राम — बुट भएकै बेलादेखि ढिलो, र एउटै काममा होइन, सबैमा ढिलो।

डिस्क झन्डै भरिएको वा टुक्रिएको — फाइलसँग सम्बन्धित सबै काम ढिलो। अस्थायी फाइल र virtual memory लाई ठाउँ बाँकी हुँदैन।

Malware — स्पष्ट कारणबिनै ढिलो, प्रायः केही नखोल्दा पनि नेटवर्क चलिरहने, र बिस्तारै होइन अचानक सुरु हुने।

तात्ने समस्या — सुरुमा छिटो, केही बेरपछि ढिलो — किनकि प्रोसेसरले आफूलाई जोगाउन जानाजान गति घटाउँछ। सङ्केत भनेको समय हो।

Faded practice · क्रमशः कम सहयोग

Working a symptom down to a cause

What you will learn: to produce the diagnostic sequence yourself. The first problem is worked for you, the next shows fewer steps, and by the last one you write the reasoning with no prompts at all. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: निदानको क्रम आफैँ बनाउन। पहिलो समस्या तपाईंका लागि गरिदिइन्छ, अर्कोमा कम चरण देखिन्छन्, र अन्तिममा कुनै सङ्केतबिनै तपाईं आफैँ तर्क लेख्नुहुन्छ।

Why this matters: reading a worked diagnosis feels like learning and is not. The only way to find out whether you can do it is to be given a symptom with the working removed.किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ: गरिसकेको निदान पढ्दा सिकेजस्तो लाग्छ, तर सिकिँदैन। आफूले गर्न सक्छु कि सक्दिनँ भन्ने थाहा पाउने एउटै तरिका छ — काम हटाइएको लक्षण दिइनु।

Example 1 "No boot device found" — reasoning it out

Question: a computer shows the manufacturer logo, then "No boot device found". The user says it worked yesterday. Where do you look, and in what order?

Before reading on, answer one question: what has the message already proved is working?पढ्नुअघि एउटा प्रश्नको उत्तर दिनुहोस्: यो सन्देशले के चलिरहेको छ भनी पहिल्यै प्रमाणित गरिसक्यो?

Step 1 — read how far it got. A logo and a readable message mean video works and POST passed. Power, memory and the display path are all ruled out before you touch anything.

Step 2 — ask what changed. The user mentions the machine was moved to a new desk yesterday. A move disturbs cables; this is now the most probable cause.

Step 3 — check the cheapest thing consistent with that theory. Enter BIOS setup and look at the detected drives. The disk is not listed at all.

Step 4 — a disk that is not detected is a connection or a drive fault. Power off, unplug, and reseat both the data cable and the power cable at the drive and at the board.

Step 5 — verify. The drive now appears in BIOS and the machine boots.

Answer
A data cable loosened by the move. Found without opening a single component, because the message ruled out everything before the disk.

Notice that no part was replaced and nothing was guessed. Step 1 removed most of the machine from suspicion and step 2 supplied the theory — the actual repair took thirty seconds. ध्यान दिनुहोस्, कुनै पार्ट साटिएन र केही अनुमान गरिएन। चरण १ ले मेसिनको ठूलो हिस्सालाई शंकाबाट हटायो र चरण २ ले अनुमान दियो — वास्तविक मर्मत तीस सेकेन्डको थियो।

Example 2 Separating two causes of the same complaint

Question: two users both report "my computer is slow". How do you tell whether it is too little RAM or overheating, without opening either machine?

Step 1 — ask when it is slow, not how slow. The two causes have different timing, and timing is free to collect.

Step 2 — the first user says it is slow whenever several programs are open, from the moment it starts. Present from boot, and tied to load rather than to elapsed time. That is memory: the machine is swapping to disk because RAM has run out.

Step 3 — the second says it is fine for half an hour and then gets slow, every day. Absent at first and appearing only after the machine has warmed up. That is thermal: the processor is reducing its own speed to protect itself.

Step 4 — confirm each cheaply. For the first, watch the disk light while opening programs; for the second, read the temperature in BIOS setup after it has been running.

Answer
User 1 — insufficient RAM. User 2 — overheating. The same complaint, separated by one question about timing.

"It is slow" is not a symptom. "It is slow after twenty minutes" is. Most of the work in this unit is turning the first sentence into the second. "ढिलो छ" लक्षण होइन। "बीस मिनेटपछि ढिलो हुन्छ" लक्षण हो। यस युनिटको धेरैजसो काम पहिलो वाक्यलाई दोस्रोमा बदल्नु नै हो।

Mistakes that cost marks here — यहाँ अङ्क गुमाउने गल्तीहरू

  • Replacing parts to find the fault. Substituting a known-good part is a test; buying a new part on suspicion is not diagnosis, and the exam marks the method. खराबी खोज्न पार्ट साट्नु। चल्ने भनेर थाहा भएको पार्ट राखेर हेर्नु परीक्षण हो; शंकामा नयाँ किन्नु निदान होइन, र परीक्षाले तरिकाको अंक दिन्छ।
  • Changing two things at once. If the fault clears you have no idea which change did it, so you have not learned anything and cannot prevent a repeat. एकैचोटि दुई कुरा बदल्नु। खराबी हट्यो भने कुनले हटायो थाहै हुँदैन, त्यसैले केही सिकिँदैन र फेरि हुन रोक्न पनि सकिँदैन।
  • Skipping "ask the user what changed". It is a step in the method and it is free. Most faults follow a change the user can name. "के बदलियो?" सोध्ने चरण छोड्नु। यो विधिकै एउटा चरण हो र निःशुल्क छ। धेरैजसो खराबी प्रयोगकर्ताले नाम लिन सक्ने परिवर्तनपछि आउँछ।
  • Reinstalling the operating system for a hardware symptom. If the screen is blank before any OS could load, the OS cannot be the cause — and reinstalling destroys the user's data for nothing. हार्डवेयरको लक्षणमा अपरेटिङ सिस्टम फेरि इन्स्टल गर्नु। कुनै OS लोड हुनुअघि नै स्क्रिन खाली छ भने OS कारण हुनै सक्दैन — र फेरि इन्स्टल गर्दा प्रयोगकर्ताको data बेकारमा नष्ट हुन्छ।

Cheapest check first, one change at a time. The symptom says how far it got. सस्तो जाँच पहिले, एकपटकमा एउटा परिवर्तन। कहाँसम्म पुग्यो भन्ने लक्षणले नै भन्छ।

Exam focus — what is asked from this unit — परीक्षा केन्द्रबिन्दु — यो युनिटबाट के सोधिन्छ

This unit carries 9 marks in the written paper and is most of what the practical exam actually tests. The written questions are predictable: list the steps of troubleshooting (in order — the order is the answer), what would you check if… given a symptom, and state the causes of a slow system or a boot failure. The examiner is looking for a sequence of checks with reasons, not a single guessed cause. Write the order you would work in and say what each check rules out; that is where the marks are. यो युनिटले लिखित परीक्षामा ९ अंक बोक्छ र व्यावहारिक परीक्षाले जाँच्ने कुराको ठूलो हिस्सा यही हो। लिखित प्रश्न अनुमान गर्न सकिने खालका छन्: troubleshooting का चरण सूचीबद्ध गर्नुहोस् (क्रमैसँग — क्रम नै उत्तर हो), लक्षण दिएर के जाँच्नुहुन्छ, र ढिलो प्रणाली वा boot असफलताका कारण लेख्नुहोस्। परीक्षकले अनुमान गरिएको एउटा कारण होइन, कारणसहितको जाँच क्रम खोज्छ। तपाईं कुन क्रममा काम गर्नुहुन्छ लेख्नुहोस् र हरेक जाँचले के हटाउँछ भन्नुहोस्; अंक त्यहीँ छ।

Q1 3 marks

List the steps of troubleshooting in order. Troubleshooting का चरण क्रमैसँग सूचीबद्ध गर्नुहोस्।

1. Identify the problem — state exactly what happens and when. 2. Gather information — ask the user what changed. 3. Establish a theory of probable cause — the most likely cause consistent with the symptom. 4. Test the theory, changing one thing at a time; if it is disproved, return to step 3. 5. Apply the fix and verify the original symptom has gone. 6. Document the symptom, the cause and the solution.

१. समस्या पहिचान — के हुन्छ र कहिले हुन्छ ठ्याक्कै भन्ने। २. जानकारी सङ्कलन — प्रयोगकर्तालाई के बदलियो सोध्ने। ३. सम्भावित कारणको अनुमान — लक्षणसँग मिल्ने सबैभन्दा सम्भावित कारण। ४. अनुमानको परीक्षण, एकपटकमा एउटा कुरा बदलेर; गलत साबित भए चरण ३ मा फर्किने। ५. समाधान लागू गरी सुरुको लक्षण हट्यो कि पुष्टि गर्ने। ६. लक्षण, कारण र समाधानको अभिलेख राख्ने।

Q2 3 marks

A computer powers on with fans running but shows nothing on screen. Describe how you would find the fault. कम्प्युटरमा बिजुली आउँछ, पंखा घुम्छ, तर स्क्रिनमा केही देखिँदैन। खराबी कसरी पत्ता लगाउनुहुन्छ वर्णन गर्नुहोस्।

Work from the cheapest check outward. First confirm the monitor has power and the video cable is firm at both ends, and try a monitor known to work — this rules out the display path. Then listen at power-on: repeated beeps mean POST found a fault before video was available, which points to memory. Power off, unplug, and reseat the RAM modules one at a time until the clips close. If the beeps continue with a module known to be good, the slot or the board is at fault. Change only one thing at a time, and verify the display returns before closing the case.

सबैभन्दा सस्तो जाँचबाट बाहिरतिर बढ्नुहोस्। पहिले monitor मा बिजुली छ र video केबल दुवै छेउमा कसिएको छ पुष्टि गर्नुहोस्, र चल्ने भनेर थाहा भएको monitor जोडेर हेर्नुहोस् — यसले display को बाटो हटाउँछ। अनि बिजुली लाग्दा सुन्नुहोस्: बारम्बार बीप भनेको video उपलब्ध हुनुअघि POST ले खराबी भेट्यो, जसले मेमोरीतिर देखाउँछ। बिजुली बन्द गरी प्लग निकाली क्लिप बन्द नहुन्जेल RAM एक–एक गरी फेरि बसाल्नुहोस्। चल्ने मोड्युल राख्दा पनि बीप जारी रहे slot वा बोर्डमा खराबी छ। एकपटकमा एउटा मात्र कुरा बदल्नुहोस्, र केस बन्द गर्नुअघि स्क्रिन फर्कियो कि पुष्टि गर्नुहोस्।

Q3 3 marks

State four causes of a computer becoming slow, and how you would tell one of them apart from the others. कम्प्युटर ढिलो हुनुका चार कारण लेख्नुहोस्, र तीमध्ये एउटालाई अरूबाट कसरी छुट्याउनुहुन्छ भन्नुहोस्।

Causes: insufficient RAM; too many programs loading at startup; a nearly full or fragmented hard disk; malware infection; overheating causing the processor to reduce its speed. Overheating is told apart by its timing — the machine is normal when first switched on and only becomes slow after it has been running for a while, and sooner when a demanding program is open. The other causes are slow from the start.

कारण: RAM अपुग; startup मा धेरै प्रोग्राम लोड हुनु; hard disk झन्डै भरिनु वा टुक्रिनु; malware सङ्क्रमण; तात्दा प्रोसेसरले गति घटाउनु। तात्ने समस्या समयले छुट्टिन्छ — खोल्दा सामान्य हुन्छ र केही बेर चलेपछि मात्र ढिलो हुन्छ, र भारी प्रोग्राम खोले झन् चाँडो। अरू कारणमा सुरुदेखि नै ढिलो हुन्छ।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • Steps: identify, gather information, form a theory, test it, fix and verify, document. A failed test sends you back to forming a theory, not to swapping parts. चरण: पहिचान, जानकारी सङ्कलन, अनुमान, परीक्षण, समाधान र पुष्टि, अभिलेख। परीक्षण असफल भए पार्ट साट्न होइन, अनुमान बनाउनतिरै फर्किने।
  • Cheapest check first; change one thing at a time; never diagnose by buying parts. सस्तो जाँच पहिले; एकपटकमा एउटा परिवर्तन; पार्ट किनेर निदान कहिल्यै नगर्ने।
  • The symptom tells you how far the boot got, and that rules out everything after it. Beeps mean POST is running and video is not yet available. Boot कहाँसम्म पुग्यो लक्षणले भन्छ, र त्यसपछिका सबै सम्भावना हट्छन्। बीप भनेको POST चलिरहेको र video अझै नआएको।
  • "No boot device" points at boot order and cables; "CMOS checksum error" points at the battery. "No boot device" ले boot order र केबलतिर देखाउँछ; "CMOS checksum error" ले ब्याट्रीतिर।
  • Slowdowns: too little RAM, too many startup programs, a full or fragmented disk, malware, or overheating — which is the one identified by its timing. ढिलो हुनु: RAM कम, startup मा धेरै प्रोग्राम, डिस्क भरिएको वा टुक्रिएको, malware, वा तात्ने — जुन समयले चिनिन्छ।
