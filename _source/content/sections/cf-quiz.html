How this quiz works

Twelve questions are drawn from a larger bank each time, so a second attempt is a different paper — you cannot pass by remembering the order of the answers.

All seven units are now written, and the bank covers every one of them, weighted to the marks each unit carries in the written paper. Nothing here asks about a topic you have not been taught on this site.

These are practice questions written for Gyansetu. They follow the kinds of question this unit is examined with, but they are not past CDC or SEE papers and are not presented as such.
