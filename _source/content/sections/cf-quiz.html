How this quiz works

Eight questions are drawn from a larger bank each time, so a second attempt is a different paper — you cannot pass by remembering the order of the answers.

Only Unit 1 is written so far, so the bank asks only about Unit 1. It will grow as the other six units are added, and the draw will grow with it. Nothing here asks about a topic you have not been taught on this site.

These are practice questions written for Gyansetu. They follow the kinds of question this unit is examined with, but they are not past CDC or SEE papers and are not presented as such.
