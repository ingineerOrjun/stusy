How this quiz works

Twelve questions are drawn from a larger bank each time, so a second attempt is a different paper — you cannot pass by remembering the order of the answers.

The bank covers all seven units, weighted to the marks each unit carries in the written paper, so Control Flow Statements comes up more often than Pointers. Nothing here asks about a topic this site has not taught you.

Many of the questions are code questions: read the program, work out what it prints, then choose. Doing that on paper first is the practice, not the clicking.

These are practice questions written for Gyansetu. They follow the kinds of question this subject is examined with, but they are not past CDC or SEE papers and are not presented as such.

बैंकले सातै युनिट समेट्छ — लिखित परीक्षामा हरेक युनिटले बोक्ने अंकअनुसार भार मिलाएर। त्यसैले Control Flow Statements बाट Pointers भन्दा बढी सोधिन्छ। यहाँ यो साइटमा नपढाइएको कुनै विषय सोधिँदैन।

धेरै प्रश्न कोडका प्रश्न हुन्: प्रोग्राम पढ्नुहोस्, के छाप्छ पत्ता लगाउनुहोस्, अनि छान्नुहोस्। अभ्यास भनेको कागजमा पहिले त्यो गर्नु हो, क्लिक गर्नु होइन।

यी Gyansetu का लागि लेखिएका अभ्यास प्रश्न हुन्। यस विषयमा सोधिने किसिमका प्रश्न पछ्याइएका छन्, तर यी CDC वा SEE का पुराना प्रश्नपत्र होइनन् र त्यसो भनिएको पनि छैन।
