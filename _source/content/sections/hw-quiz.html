How this quiz works

Twelve questions are drawn from a larger bank each time, so a second attempt is a different paper — you cannot pass by remembering the order of the answers.

The bank covers all six units and mixes the kinds of thinking this subject asks for: recalling a definition, telling apart two things that sound alike, and — the one that matters most here — reading a symptom and working out what it points to.

Questions and options are given in both languages, and follow whichever language mode you have chosen at the top of the page.
