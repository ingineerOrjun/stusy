6.1  What a transaction is

Definition — learn this wording

A transaction is a single logical unit of work made up of one or more database operations, which must be carried out completely or not at all.

The standard example is a bank transfer of Rs 1000 from Ram to Sita. It is two operations:

1. Ram's balance = Ram's balance − 1000 2. Sita's balance = Sita's balance + 1000

Neither line is a transfer on its own. If the power fails between them, Rs 1000 has left Ram's account and arrived nowhere. Grouping them into one transaction is the promise that this cannot happen: either both lines take effect, or neither does.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

ट्रान्ज्याक्सन भनेको एक वा बढी डाटाबेस कार्य मिलेर बनेको एउटै तार्किक काम हो, जुन पूरै हुनुपर्छ वा बिल्कुल हुनुहुँदैन।

सामान्य उदाहरण: रामबाट सीतालाई रु १००० पठाउनु। यो दुई काम हो।

कुनै एक हरफ आफैंमा रकम सार्नु होइन। दुईबीच बिजुली गयो भने रु १००० रामको खाताबाट निस्क्यो तर कतै पुगेन। दुवैलाई एउटै ट्रान्ज्याक्सनमा राख्नु भनेको यस्तो हुन नदिने वचन हो: या दुवै लागू हुन्छन्, या कुनै पनि हुँदैन।

COMMIT and ROLLBACK. COMMIT makes every change of the transaction permanent — after it, the work survives a crash. ROLLBACK undoes every change the transaction made, returning the database to the state it was in before the transaction began. Exactly one of the two ends a transaction. COMMIT ले ट्रान्ज्याक्सनका सबै परिवर्तन स्थायी बनाउँछ — त्यसपछि crash भए पनि काम बाँच्छ। ROLLBACK ले गरेका सबै परिवर्तन फिर्ता लैजान्छ र डाटाबेसलाई सुरुको अवस्थामा पुर्‍याउँछ। ट्रान्ज्याक्सन यी दुईमध्ये ठ्याक्कै एउटाले टुङ्गिन्छ।

6.2  Concurrency

Why two users at once is a problem

Concurrency is several transactions running at the same time. A database with one user at a time would be safe and useless — a bank has thousands of users at once — so concurrency is necessary, and the DBMS must control it.

The classic failure is the lost update. Two clerks both read a balance of 5000. One subtracts 1000 and writes 4000. The other, working from the value it read before that, subtracts 2000 and writes 3000. The first withdrawal has vanished — the balance should be 2000.

Nothing was written incorrectly. Each clerk did correct arithmetic on a value that was correct when they read it. The fault is entirely in the interleaving, which is why the DBMS controls it with locks: a row being changed is locked until the transaction ends.

एकैचोटि दुई प्रयोगकर्ता किन समस्या

Concurrency भनेको धेरै ट्रान्ज्याक्सन एकैचोटि चल्नु हो। एक पटकमा एक जना मात्र चलाउने डाटाबेस सुरक्षित त होला, तर काम लाग्दैन — बैंकमा हजारौं प्रयोगकर्ता एकैचोटि हुन्छन् — त्यसैले concurrency चाहिन्छ, र DBMS ले नियन्त्रण गर्नुपर्छ।

क्लासिक समस्या lost update हो। दुई कर्मचारीले ५००० मौज्दात पढे। एउटाले १००० घटाएर ४००० लेख्यो। अर्कोले, त्योभन्दा अघि पढेको मानबाट २००० घटाएर ३००० लेख्यो। पहिलो निकासी हरायो — मौज्दात २००० हुनुपर्थ्यो।

कसैले गलत लेखेको होइन। दुवैले पढ्दाको सही मानमा सही गणित गरे। दोष पूरै क्रम मिसिनु मा छ, त्यसैले DBMS ले lock ले नियन्त्रण गर्छ: बदलिँदै गरेको पङ्क्ति ट्रान्ज्याक्सन नसकिँदासम्म बन्द रहन्छ।

Watch a withdrawal disappear

What you will learn: how two people can each do everything correctly and still lose one of the two withdrawals — and what a lock changes. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: दुई जनाले सबै सही गर्दा पनि दुई निकासीमध्ये एक कसरी हराउँछ — र lock ले के फरक पार्छ।

What to notice

Step through Both at once and watch the three boxes. At step 2 both clerks hold 5000 — and that is already the bug, four steps before anything looks wrong.

The arithmetic is never wrong. Clerk 2 computes 5000 − 2000 = 3000 correctly; the value they started from was simply out of date by then. Nobody makes a mistake and the money is still lost. That is what makes this hard to believe from a definition.

Now switch to One at a time. The lock forces clerk 2 to read after clerk 1 has written, so they start from 4000 and the final balance is 2000. Same two transactions, same arithmetic, different order.

दुवै एकैचोटि मा चरण–चरण जानुहोस् र तीन बाकस हेर्नुहोस्। चरण २ मै दुवै कर्मचारीसँग ५००० छ — केही बिग्रेको देखिनुभन्दा चार चरण अगाडि नै समस्या सुरु भइसक्यो। गणित कहिल्यै गलत हुँदैन; कर्मचारी २ ले सुरु गरेको मान मात्र पुरानो भइसकेको थियो। कसैले गल्ती गर्दैन, तैपनि पैसा हराउँछ। अब एक–एक गरी मा जानुहोस्: lock ले कर्मचारी २ लाई कर्मचारी १ ले लेखेपछि मात्र पढ्न दिन्छ।
Example 1 Reading a concurrency problem from a schedule

Question: two transactions run on a balance of 5000. State the final balance, the correct balance, and name the problem.

Schedule
T1: read balance (5000) T2: read balance (5000) T1: balance = 5000 − 1000 (4000) T2: balance = 5000 − 2000 (3000) T1: write balance (4000) T2: write balance (3000)

The whole schedule is above. Work out the balance the database is left holding, and the balance it should have held — the gap between those two numbers is the name of the problem. पूरा schedule माथि छ। डाटाबेसमा अन्तमा बाँकी रहने रकम, र रहनुपर्ने रकम निकाल्नुहोस् — ती दुई सङ्ख्याको फरक नै समस्याको नाम हो।

Step 1 — find where the two reads happen. Both read before either writes, so both are working from 5000.

Step 2 — find the last write. T2 writes last, so T2's value survives and T1's is overwritten.

Answer
Final balance 3000 Correct balance 2000 (5000 − 1000 − 2000) Problem lost update Prevented by locking — T2 must wait until T1 has written and released

The giveaway in any schedule is two reads before the first write. Look for that pattern and you can name the problem without tracing the arithmetic at all. कुनै पनि schedule मा सङ्केत यही हो: पहिलो write अघि दुई read। यो ढाँचा भेटे गणित नै नगरी समस्याको नाम भन्न सकिन्छ।

6.3  ACID properties

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A transfer has debited Ram but the power fails before Sita is credited. Which ACID property guarantees the money is not lost? रकम सार्दा रामको खाताबाट कटिसक्यो तर सीताको खातामा जम्मा हुनुअघि बिजुली गयो। पैसा नहराओस् भन्ने कुन ACID गुणले सुनिश्चित गर्छ?

Atomicity. The transaction never committed, so on restart the database rolls the debit back — as if the transfer never began. Durability is the opposite promise: it protects work that did commit. The test is always the same — had it committed? No → atomicity rolls it back. Yes → durability keeps it.

Atomicity। ट्रान्ज्याक्सन commit नै भएको थिएन, त्यसैले पुनः सुरु हुँदा डाटाबेसले कटौती फिर्ता लैजान्छ — सुरु नै नभएको जस्तो। Durability उल्टो वचन हो: यसले commit भइसकेको काम जोगाउँछ। जाँच सधैं एउटै — commit भएको थियो? थिएन → atomicity ले फिर्ता। थियो → durability ले जोगाउँछ।

6.4  States of a transaction

{{dia:txnStates}}
Reading the state diagram

A transaction begins in ACTIVE and stays there while its statements run. When the last statement finishes it moves to PARTIALLY COMMITTED — the work is done in memory but not yet safely on disk.

From there, if all is well it reaches COMMITTED and the changes are permanent. If anything goes wrong — at any point, from either of the first two states — it moves to FAILED, and from FAILED it is rolled back to ABORTED.

An aborted transaction leaves no trace. The database is exactly as it was before the transaction started, which is atomicity being enforced.

Only COMMITTED and ABORTED are final. Every transaction ends in one of them.

अवस्था चित्र पढ्ने तरिका

ट्रान्ज्याक्सन ACTIVE बाट सुरु हुन्छ र कथन चलेसम्म त्यहीँ रहन्छ। अन्तिम कथन सकिएपछि PARTIALLY COMMITTED मा जान्छ — काम मेमोरीमा सकियो तर डिस्कमा सुरक्षित भइसकेको छैन।

त्यहाँबाट सबै ठीक भए COMMITTED मा पुग्छ र परिवर्तन स्थायी हुन्छ। केही बिग्रियो भने — पहिलो दुई अवस्थामध्ये जुनसुकैबाट — FAILED मा जान्छ, र FAILED बाट फिर्ता लगिएर ABORTED हुन्छ।

Abort भएको ट्रान्ज्याक्सनले कुनै चिन्ह छाड्दैन। डाटाबेस सुरु हुनुअघिकै अवस्थामा रहन्छ — यही atomicity लागू भएको हो।

COMMITTED र ABORTED मात्र अन्तिम अवस्था हुन्। हरेक ट्रान्ज्याक्सन यीमध्ये एउटामा टुङ्गिन्छ।

Example 2 Tracing a transfer through the states

Question: Ram has 5000 and Sita has 2000. A transaction transfers 1000 from Ram to Sita, but the system crashes immediately after the first update. Trace the states and state the final balances.

Working
BEGIN → ACTIVE UPDATE Ram SET bal = 4000 → ACTIVE (written in memory) -- CRASH before the second update -- → FAILED recovery rolls the change back → ABORTED Final: Ram 5000, Sita 2000

Why 5000, not 4000. The transaction never reached COMMITTED, so nothing it did is allowed to survive. On restart, recovery finds no COMMIT record in the log and undoes the change. Atomicity is the property being enforced.

If the crash had come after COMMIT, the answer would be Ram 4000 and Sita 3000 — and the property being enforced would be durability instead. The COMMIT record is the only thing that decides which. Crash COMMIT पछि आएको भए उत्तर राम ४००० र सीता ३००० हुन्थ्यो — र लागू हुने गुण durability हुन्थ्यो। कुन हो भन्ने तय गर्ने एउटै कुरा COMMIT रेकर्ड हो।

Mistakes that cost marks here

  • Leaving FAILED and ABORTED out of the state diagram. The failure path is half the diagram and usually half the marks. अवस्था चित्रमा FAILED र ABORTED नराख्नु। असफल बाटो चित्रको आधा हो र प्रायः अंकको पनि आधा।
  • Confusing atomicity with durability. Atomicity protects an uncommitted transaction by undoing it; durability protects a committed one by keeping it. Atomicity र durability मिसाउनु। Atomicity ले commit नभएको फिर्ता लैजान्छ; durability ले commit भएको जोगाउँछ।
  • Describing a transaction as "one SQL statement". It is one logical unit of work, which is usually several statements. ट्रान्ज्याक्सनलाई "एउटा SQL कथन" भन्नु। यो एउटा तार्किक काम हो, जसमा प्रायः धेरै कथन हुन्छन्।
  • Saying concurrency is bad and should be avoided. It is necessary; what must be avoided is uncontrolled concurrency. Concurrency नराम्रो हो, हटाउनुपर्छ भन्नु। यो आवश्यक हो; हटाउनुपर्ने कुरा अनियन्त्रित concurrency हो।

No FAILED, no ABORTED, no marks. The failure path is half the diagram. FAILED र ABORTED नभए अङ्क आउँदैन। असफलताको बाटो नै आधा चित्र हो।

Exam focus — what is asked from this unit

Two questions come from this unit almost every year. The first asks you to explain the ACID properties — four marks, one per property, and each answer is one clear sentence plus an example of what goes wrong without it. The second asks for the transaction state diagram, where the marks are for including the failure path, which is the half most answers leave out. यस युनिटबाट प्रायः हरेक वर्ष दुई प्रश्न आउँछन्। पहिलोले ACID गुण व्याख्या गर्न भन्छ — चार अंक, हरेकलाई एक, र हरेक उत्तर एउटा स्पष्ट वाक्य र नभए के बिग्रन्छ भन्ने उदाहरण। दोस्रोले ट्रान्ज्याक्सनको अवस्था चित्र माग्छ, जहाँ अंक असफल बाटो समावेश गरेकोमा हुन्छ — प्रायः उत्तरमा छुट्ने आधा त्यही हो।

4 marks Practice question

Explain the ACID properties of a transaction. ट्रान्ज्याक्सनका ACID गुण व्याख्या गर्नुहोस्।

ATOMICITY All operations of the transaction happen, or none do. Without it: money leaves one account and arrives nowhere. CONSISTENCY The database obeys all its rules before and after. Without it: a student row points at a class that does not exist. ISOLATION Concurrent transactions do not see each other's partial work. Without it: two clerks read the same balance and both subtract. DURABILITY Once committed, changes survive any later failure. Without it: a power cut erases work already confirmed.

One mark per property. Naming the property, stating the promise and giving the failure it prevents is a complete answer to each.

हरेक गुणलाई एक अंक। नाम, वचन र नभए के बिग्रन्छ — यत्ति भए पूरा उत्तर।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • A transaction is one logical unit of work that must happen completely or not at all. ट्रान्ज्याक्सन भनेको एउटै तार्किक काम हो — पूरै हुनुपर्ने वा बिल्कुल नहुनुपर्ने।
  • COMMIT makes the changes permanent; ROLLBACK undoes them. One of the two ends every transaction. COMMIT ले परिवर्तन स्थायी बनाउँछ; ROLLBACK ले फिर्ता लैजान्छ। हरेक ट्रान्ज्याक्सन यीमध्ये एउटाले टुङ्गिन्छ।
  • ACID: Atomicity (all or nothing), Consistency (rules always hold), Isolation (no half-done work is visible), Durability (committed work survives a crash). ACID: Atomicity (सबै वा केही होइन), Consistency (नियम सधैं पालना), Isolation (आधा काम नदेखिने), Durability (commit भएको crash पछि पनि रहने)।
  • Atomicity undoes an uncommitted transaction; durability keeps a committed one. The COMMIT record decides which applies. Atomicity ले commit नभएको फिर्ता लैजान्छ; durability ले commit भएको राख्छ। COMMIT रेकर्डले तय गर्छ।
  • States: ACTIVE → PARTIALLY COMMITTED → COMMITTED, or → FAILED → ABORTED. Only COMMITTED and ABORTED are final. अवस्था: ACTIVE → PARTIALLY COMMITTED → COMMITTED, वा → FAILED → ABORTED। अन्तिम अवस्था COMMITTED र ABORTED मात्र।
  • Concurrency is necessary, not optional. Uncontrolled concurrency causes the lost update problem; locks prevent it. Concurrency आवश्यक हो, वैकल्पिक होइन। अनियन्त्रित भए lost update समस्या आउँछ; lock ले रोक्छ।
