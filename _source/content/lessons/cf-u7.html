7.1  What "emerging technology" means — "उदीयमान प्रविधि" भनेको के हो

Not new — becoming important

Emerging technologies are technologies that are still developing, are not yet in ordinary everyday use, and are expected to change how people live and work within the next several years.

"New" is not the test. A new model of phone is new and changes nothing. A technology is emerging when it is moving out of the laboratory and into use, fast enough that the rules and the jobs around it have not settled yet.

That also means the list changes. Everything in this unit is emerging as this curriculum is written; some of it will be ordinary by the time you are working, and that is what the word predicts.

नयाँ होइन — महत्त्वपूर्ण बन्दै गरेको

उदीयमान प्रविधि भनेका ती प्रविधि हुन् जो अझै विकसित हुँदै छन्, दैनिक सामान्य प्रयोगमा आइसकेका छैनन्, र आउँदा केही वर्षभित्रै मानिसको जीवन र कामको तरिका बदल्ने अपेक्षा गरिन्छ।

जाँच "नयाँ" भन्नु होइन। फोनको नयाँ मोडेल नयाँ त हो, तर केही बदल्दैन। कुनै प्रविधि प्रयोगशालाबाट निस्केर प्रयोगमा यति छिटो सर्दै छ कि वरिपरिका नियम र पेसा नै थामिइसकेका छैनन् भने त्यो उदीयमान हो।

यसको अर्थ सूची बदलिन्छ पनि। यो पाठ्यक्रम लेखिँदा यस युनिटका सबै उदीयमान छन्; तपाईं काम गर्ने बेलासम्म केही सामान्य भइसक्नेछन् — शब्दले भनेकै त्यही हो।

7.2  Artificial Intelligence (AI) — आर्टिफिसियल इन्टेलिजेन्स (AI)

Machines doing what used to need a person

Artificial Intelligence is the branch of computer science concerned with making machines perform tasks that normally require human intelligence — understanding language, recognising images and speech, making decisions, and learning from experience.

The phrase that carries the mark is learning from experience. An ordinary program does exactly what it was told. An AI system is given examples and works out the rule itself, which is why it can improve without being rewritten.

Examples you already meet: voice assistants, face unlock on a phone, the suggestions in a search box, machine translation between Nepali and English, and the fraud checks a bank runs on your transactions.

पहिले मान्छे चाहिने काम मेसिनले गर्नु

Artificial Intelligence कम्प्युटर विज्ञानको त्यो शाखा हो जसले मेसिनलाई सामान्यतया मानवीय बुद्धि चाहिने काम गर्न लगाउँछ — भाषा बुझ्ने, तस्बिर र बोली चिन्ने, निर्णय गर्ने, र अनुभवबाट सिक्ने।

अंक बोक्ने वाक्यांश अनुभवबाट सिक्ने हो। साधारण प्रोग्रामले भनेकै गर्छ। AI प्रणालीलाई उदाहरण दिइन्छ र नियम आफैं निकाल्छ — त्यसैले फेरि नलेखिकनै सुध्रन सक्छ।

तपाईंले भेटिसकेका उदाहरण: भ्वाइस असिस्टेन्ट, फोनको face unlock, खोज बाकसका सुझाव, नेपाली–अंग्रेजी मेसिन अनुवाद, र बैंकले तपाईंको कारोबारमा गर्ने ठगी जाँच।

7.3  Cloud computing and distributed computing — Cloud computing र distributed computing

Two ideas that share a picture and answer different questions

Cloud computing is using computing resources — storage, processing, software — that are owned and run by somebody else and reached over the Internet, paying for what you use instead of buying the machines. Google Drive, Gmail and online backup are cloud services.

Distributed computing is splitting one large task across many computers that work on it together, so that it finishes faster than any one of them could manage. Weather forecasting and large scientific calculations are done this way.

The examination asks you to separate them, and the clean separation is this: cloud computing is about WHO OWNS the computer; distributed computing is about HOW MANY computers share the work. A cloud service may be distributed underneath, and a distributed system may sit entirely inside one organisation's own building. They are not opposites and they are not the same.

Cloud computing's advantages: no hardware to buy, reachable from anywhere, grows when you need more. Its disadvantages: it needs a working Internet connection, your data sits on someone else's computer, and the monthly cost never stops — all three matter in Nepal, where connections fail and electricity is not guaranteed.

एउटै चित्र बाँड्ने, फरक प्रश्नको उत्तर दिने दुई विचार

Cloud computing भनेको अर्कैले किनेर चलाएका कम्प्युटिङ स्रोत — भण्डारण, प्रशोधन, सफ्टवेयर — इन्टरनेटमार्फत प्रयोग गर्नु हो, मेसिन किन्नुको साटो जति चलायो त्यति तिरेर। Google Drive, Gmail र अनलाइन ब्याकअप cloud सेवा हुन्।

Distributed computing भनेको एउटै ठूलो काम धेरै कम्प्युटरमा बाँडेर सँगै गराउनु हो, ताकि एउटैले सक्ने भन्दा छिटो सकियोस्। मौसम पूर्वानुमान र ठूला वैज्ञानिक गणना यसरी गरिन्छन्।

परीक्षाले यी छुट्याउन भन्छ, र सफा फरक यही हो: cloud computing कम्प्युटर कसको हो भन्ने कुरा हो; distributed computing काम कति कम्प्युटरले बाँड्छन् भन्ने। Cloud सेवा भित्री रूपमा distributed हुन सक्छ, र distributed प्रणाली एउटै संस्थाकै भवनभित्र हुन सक्छ। यी उल्टा पनि होइनन्, उही पनि होइनन्।

Cloud का फाइदा: हार्डवेयर किन्नु नपर्ने, जहाँबाट पनि पुग्न सकिने, चाहिएअनुसार बढ्ने। बेफाइदा: चल्ने इन्टरनेट चाहिने, तपाईंको data अर्कैको कम्प्युटरमा रहने, र मासिक खर्च कहिल्यै नसकिने — नेपालमा तीनै कुरा महत्त्वपूर्ण छन्, जहाँ जडान बिग्रन्छ र बिजुलीको ग्यारेन्टी छैन।

7.4  Internet of Things (IoT) — इन्टरनेट अफ थिङ्स (IoT)

Ordinary objects that can report and be told

The Internet of Things is the connection of everyday physical objects — fitted with sensors and software — to the Internet, so that they can collect data, send it, and be controlled remotely.

The test for an IoT device is simple: it is a thing that is not a computer, and it is on the network. A laptop on Wi-Fi is not IoT, because a laptop is a computer. A water tank with a level sensor that messages you when it is low is IoT, because a water tank is not a computer.

Examples: smart meters that report electricity use without a reader visiting; soil sensors that tell a farmer when to irrigate; health bands; vehicle trackers; street lights that switch themselves on. The risk is the same in every case — a thing on the network can be reached by people you did not intend.

जानकारी दिन र आदेश लिन सक्ने सामान्य वस्तु

Internet of Things भनेको दैनिक प्रयोगका भौतिक वस्तुहरूलाई — सेन्सर र सफ्टवेयर राखेर — इन्टरनेटमा जोड्नु हो, ताकि तिनले data जम्मा गरून्, पठाऊन् र टाढैबाट नियन्त्रण गर्न सकियोस्।

IoT हो कि होइन भन्ने जाँच सरल छ: त्यो कम्प्युटर नभएको वस्तु हो, र नेटवर्कमा छ। Wi-Fi मा जोडिएको ल्यापटप IoT होइन, किनभने ल्यापटप कम्प्युटर हो। पानीको ट्यांकीमा लेभल सेन्सर राखेर कम भएपछि सन्देश पठाउने प्रणाली IoT हो, किनभने ट्यांकी कम्प्युटर होइन।

उदाहरण: मिटर पढ्न कोही नआई बिजुली खपत पठाउने स्मार्ट मिटर; कहिले सिँचाइ गर्ने भन्ने किसानलाई बताउने माटोको सेन्सर; स्वास्थ्य ब्यान्ड; सवारी ट्र्याकर; आफैं बल्ने सडक बत्ती। जोखिम हरेक ठाउँमा उही हो — नेटवर्कमा भएको वस्तुमा तपाईंले नचाहेका मानिस पनि पुग्न सक्छन्।

7.5  Big data — Big data

Too big, too fast, too mixed for ordinary tools

Big data means collections of data so large, so fast-arriving and so varied that ordinary database software cannot store or process them usefully.

It is usually described by three Vs: Volume — terabytes and beyond; Velocity — arriving continuously rather than in batches; Variety — text, images, video, sensor readings and records all mixed together.

Size alone is not the point. A single enormous video file is large and is not big data. Big data is large and messy and continuous — which is why it needs different tools, not just a bigger disk.

Examples: every transaction of a mobile wallet; every post on a social network; the readings of thousands of weather sensors; a telecom company's call records.

साधारण उपकरणका लागि निकै ठूलो, छिटो र मिश्रित

Big data भनेको यति ठूलो, यति छिटो आउने र यति विविध data को संग्रह हो कि साधारण डाटाबेस सफ्टवेयरले काम लाग्ने गरी राख्न वा प्रशोधन गर्नै सक्दैन।

यसलाई प्रायः तीन V ले वर्णन गरिन्छ: Volume — टेराबाइट र त्योभन्दा माथि; Velocity — पोकामा होइन, निरन्तर आउने; Variety — पाठ, तस्बिर, भिडियो, सेन्सरका मान र अभिलेख सबै मिसिएका।

ठूलो हुनु मात्र मुख्य कुरा होइन। एउटै विशाल भिडियो फाइल ठूलो त हो, big data होइन। Big data ठूलो र अव्यवस्थित र निरन्तर हुन्छ — त्यसैले ठूलो डिस्क होइन, फरक उपकरण चाहिन्छ।

उदाहरण: मोबाइल वालेटका हरेक कारोबार; सामाजिक सञ्जालका हरेक पोस्ट; हजारौं मौसम सेन्सरका मान; टेलिकम कम्पनीका कल रेकर्ड।

7.6  Data mining — Data mining

Digging patterns out of data you already have

Data mining is the process of examining large amounts of stored data to discover patterns and relationships that were not obvious, and using them to predict or to decide.

Big data and data mining are a pair and are not the same. Big data is the material; data mining is the activity. One is a noun you have; the other is a verb you do to it. An examination answer that says "big data is mining large data" has merged the two and loses the mark.

The classic example: a shop analyses thousands of receipts and finds that customers who buy bread often buy eggs on the same visit — so the two are placed near each other. Nobody told the computer to look for bread and eggs; it found the pattern. In Nepal the same technique finds which crops fail together in a district, or which transactions look like fraud.

भएकै data बाट बान्की खोतल्नु

Data mining भनेको भण्डारण गरिएको ठूलो मात्राको data केलाएर स्पष्ट नदेखिएका बान्की र सम्बन्ध पत्ता लगाउने र त्यसैबाट अनुमान वा निर्णय गर्ने प्रक्रिया हो।

Big data र data mining जोडी हुन्, उही होइनन्। Big data सामग्री हो; data mining काम। एउटा तपाईंसँग भएको नाम हो, अर्को त्यसमाथि गरिने क्रिया। "Big data भनेको ठूलो data को mining हो" लेख्ने उत्तरले दुईलाई मिसाएको हुन्छ र अंक गुमाउँछ।

उत्कृष्ट उदाहरण: पसलले हजारौं बिल केलाउँदा पाउरोटी किन्नेले प्रायः उही पटक अन्डा पनि किन्छन् भन्ने भेट्छ — त्यसैले दुवै नजिक राखिन्छ। पाउरोटी र अन्डा खोज् भनेर कसैले भनेको थिएन; बान्की कम्प्युटरले भेट्यो। नेपालमा यही तरिकाले कुन जिल्लामा कुन बाली सँगै बिग्रन्छ वा कुन कारोबार ठगी जस्तो देखिन्छ पत्ता लाग्छ।

7.7  Cryptography — encryption and decryption — Cryptography — encryption र decryption

Making a message unreadable on purpose

Cryptography is the practice of protecting information by converting it into a form that cannot be read by anyone who is not meant to read it.

Encryption converts plaintext — the readable message — into ciphertext, using an algorithm and a key. Decryption is the reverse: ciphertext back to plaintext, using the key.

The key is the part worth understanding. The algorithm is usually public — everyone knows how the method works. What the sender and receiver keep secret is the key. Security that depends on hiding the method fails as soon as somebody works the method out; security that depends on a key survives, because the method can be published and the key changed.

Where you already rely on it: the padlock in a browser when you pay online, the encryption on a messaging app, and the password stored by a website — stored encrypted, so that stealing the file does not hand over the passwords.

जानीजानी सन्देश नपढिने बनाउनु

Cryptography भनेको सूचनालाई पढ्न नहुनेले पढ्नै नसक्ने रूपमा बदलेर जोगाउने अभ्यास हो।

Encryption ले plaintext — पढ्न मिल्ने सन्देश — लाई एल्गोरिदम र key प्रयोग गरी ciphertext मा बदल्छ। Decryption उल्टो हो: key कै प्रयोगले ciphertext बाट फेरि plaintext।

बुझ्नुपर्ने भाग key हो। एल्गोरिदम प्रायः सार्वजनिक हुन्छ — तरिका सबैलाई थाहा हुन्छ। पठाउने र पाउनेले गोप्य राख्ने भनेको key हो। तरिका लुकाएर पाइने सुरक्षा कसैले तरिका पत्ता लगाउने बित्तिकै सकिन्छ; key मा अडेको सुरक्षा टिक्छ, किनभने तरिका छापे पनि key फेर्न सकिन्छ।

तपाईं अहिल्यै भर परिरहेका ठाउँ: अनलाइन तिर्दा browser मा देखिने ताल्चा, messaging app को encryption, र वेबसाइटले राख्ने पासवर्ड — encrypt गरेर राखिन्छ, ताकि फाइल चोरिए पनि पासवर्ड नपाइयोस्।

Do it yourself · आफैं गरेर हेर्नुहोस्

Encrypt a message, then decrypt it

What you will learn: what the key actually does. Move it and the ciphertext changes; the algorithm never changes. That is why secrecy lives in the key, and why decryption is the same operation run backwards. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: key ले साँच्चै के गर्छ। सार्नुहोस्, ciphertext बदलिन्छ; एल्गोरिदम कहिल्यै बदलिँदैन। गोपनीयता key मै बस्नुको र decryption उही कामको उल्टो हुनुको कारण यही हो।

Three things to try

1. Set the key to 3 and read the ciphertext — this exact cipher is about 2000 years old. 2. Set it to 0 and see why a key of zero is no key at all. 3. Notice that the spaces do not move: the shape of the message leaks even when the letters do not. A real weakness, and one reason this cipher is not used for anything that matters — a computer breaks it by trying all 25 keys in well under a second.

१. Key ३ राखेर ciphertext पढ्नुहोस् — यही cipher करिब दुई हजार वर्ष पुरानो हो। २. ० राखेर हेर्नुहोस्, शून्य key किन key नै होइन। ३. खाली ठाउँ नसरेको ध्यान दिनुहोस्: अक्षर नसरे पनि सन्देशको आकार चुहिन्छ। यो साँचो कमजोरी हो, र यस cipher लाई महत्त्वपूर्ण काममा नचलाइनुको एउटा कारण — कम्प्युटरले २५ वटै key एक सेकेन्डभित्रै प्रयास गरेर फोड्छ।

7.8  Virtual Reality (VR) — भर्चुअल रियालिटी (VR)

The real world replaced

Virtual Reality is a computer-generated environment that replaces the user's surroundings entirely. Wearing a headset, you see and hear only what the computer produces; the room you are standing in is gone.

Uses: pilot and surgical training where a mistake must not cost anything real; walking through a building before it is built; games; and visiting a place — a museum, a mountain — that you cannot travel to.

वास्तविक संसार नै प्रतिस्थापित

Virtual Reality भनेको कम्प्युटरले बनाएको त्यस्तो वातावरण हो जसले प्रयोगकर्ताको वरिपरिको संसार पूरै प्रतिस्थापन गर्छ। हेडसेट लगाएपछि तपाईंले कम्प्युटरले देखाएको र सुनाएको मात्र पाउनुहुन्छ; उभिएको कोठा हराउँछ।

प्रयोग: गल्तीले वास्तविक क्षति नहुनुपर्ने पाइलट र शल्यक्रिया तालिम; नबनेकै भवनभित्र हिँड्ने; खेल; र पुग्नै नसकिने ठाउँ — संग्रहालय, हिमाल — घुम्ने।

7.9  Augmented Reality (AR) — अग्मेन्टेड रियालिटी (AR)

The real world added to

Augmented Reality adds computer-generated information to the real world instead of replacing it. Through a phone camera or clear glasses you still see the room, with extra things drawn on top of it.

Uses: a phone showing how furniture would look in your own room; navigation arrows drawn on the street ahead; a repair manual that labels the parts of the engine you are actually looking at; and classroom apps that place a 3D heart on your desk.

The whole difference is what you can still see. VR: the real world is gone. AR: the real world is there with something added. If an examination question describes a headset that blocks everything out, it is VR; if the user can still see the road, it is AR.

वास्तविक संसारमा थपिएको

Augmented Reality ले वास्तविक संसार हटाउने होइन, त्यसमाथि कम्प्युटरले बनाएको सूचना थप्छ। फोनको क्यामेरा वा पारदर्शी चस्माबाट तपाईंले कोठा देख्दै गर्नुहुन्छ, माथि थप कुरा कोरिएको हुन्छ।

प्रयोग: आफ्नै कोठामा फर्निचर कस्तो देखिन्छ देखाउने फोन; अगाडिको सडकमै कोरिएका बाटो देखाउने तीर; हेरिरहेकै इन्जिनका भाग नाम लेखिदिने मर्मत निर्देशिका; र डेस्कमै ३डी मुटु राख्ने कक्षा app।

पूरै फरक तपाईंले अझै के देख्न सक्नुहुन्छ भन्नेमा छ। VR: वास्तविक संसार हराउँछ। AR: वास्तविक संसार छँदै छ, केही थपिएको। प्रश्नमा सबै छेक्ने हेडसेटको कुरा छ भने VR; प्रयोगकर्ताले सडक अझै देख्छ भने AR।

What you will learn: to identify a technology from a situation, which is how this unit is examined. The pairs that get confused — cloud against distributed, big data against data mining, VR against AR — are all here. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: परिस्थितिबाटै प्रविधि चिन्न — यो युनिट यसरी नै जाँचिन्छ। अलमल पार्ने जोडी — cloud र distributed, big data र data mining, VR र AR — सबै यहीँ छन्।

How to use this

Three questions settle most of them: whose computer is it, is the user still able to see the room, and is this the data or the digging?

तीन प्रश्नले धेरैजसो टुङ्ग्याउँछन्: कम्प्युटर कसको हो, प्रयोगकर्ताले कोठा अझै देख्छ कि देख्दैन, र यो data हो कि खोतल्ने काम?

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A cipher's algorithm is published in a textbook that anyone can buy. Does publishing it make messages encrypted with that algorithm insecure? कुनै cipher को एल्गोरिदम जोसुकैले किन्न सक्ने पाठ्यपुस्तकमा छापिएको छ। छापिएकै कारण त्यस एल्गोरिदमले encrypt गरिएका सन्देश असुरक्षित हुन्छन्?

No, and this is the central idea of the whole topic. Security that depends on hiding the method collapses the moment somebody works the method out, and it cannot be repaired — you would have to invent a new cipher. Security that depends on a key survives publication: the algorithm can be printed, studied and attacked by experts, and if a key is ever exposed you simply change the key.

That is why the same published algorithm can protect millions of different conversations at once. Each pair of people uses a different key, and knowing the method tells you nothing about any of them.

हुँदैनन्, र पूरै विषयको केन्द्रीय विचार यही हो। तरिका लुकाएर पाइने सुरक्षा कसैले तरिका पत्ता लगाउने बित्तिकै ढल्छ, र मर्मत गर्न मिल्दैन — नयाँ cipher नै बनाउनुपर्छ। Key मा अडेको सुरक्षा छापिए पनि टिक्छ: एल्गोरिदम छापेर विज्ञहरूले अध्ययन र आक्रमण गर्न दिन सकिन्छ, र key चुहिए key फेरे पुग्छ। एउटै छापिएको एल्गोरिदमले एकैपटक लाखौं फरक कुराकानी जोगाउन सक्नुको कारण यही हो। हरेक जोडीले फरक key चलाउँछ, र तरिका थाहा हुँदैमा कुनैको key थाहा हुँदैन।
Example 1 Encrypt and decrypt by hand

Question: encrypt the word NEPAL with a shift of 4, then decrypt XSVOH with the same key.

Do both on paper before opening this. Remember that the alphabet wraps: after Z comes A again. खोल्नुअघि दुवै कागजमै गर्नुहोस्। वर्णमाला घुम्छ भन्ने नबिर्सनुहोस्: Z पछि फेरि A आउँछ।

Working
ENCRYPT shift each letter FORWARD 4 N -> R E -> I P -> T A -> E L -> P NEPAL -> RITEP DECRYPT shift each letter BACK 4 X -> T S -> O V -> R O -> K H -> D XSVOH -> TORKD Check the wrap: with a shift of 4, W -> A, X -> B, Y -> C, Z -> D. Nothing falls off the end of the alphabet.

Notice that decryption used no new method — the same operation, run in the other direction with the same key. That is the point the bench above is making, and it is what makes the key, not the algorithm, the thing worth keeping secret. Decryption मा नयाँ तरिका लागेन — उही काम, उही key, उल्टो दिशामा। माथिको बेन्चले भन्न खोजेको कुरा यही हो, र एल्गोरिदम होइन key नै गोप्य राख्नुपर्ने कारण पनि।

Example 2 Naming the technology in one paragraph

Question: "A municipality fits sensors to 500 water taps. The readings go to a server rented from a company in Singapore. Software examines two years of those readings and finds which wards lose the most water at night." Name the three technologies, in order.

Three different things are happening in three sentences. Name each before opening this. तीन वाक्यमा तीन फरक कुरा भइरहेका छन्। खोल्नुअघि हरेकको नाम भन्नुहोस्।

Working
1 sensors on 500 taps IoT a tap is not a computer, and now it is on the network 2 a server rented in Singapore CLOUD COMPUTING whose computer? somebody else's, reached over the Internet. NOT distributed — nothing says the work is split across many machines 3 examining two years of DATA MINING readings to FIND a pattern the readings themselves are big data; the examining is the mining

Questions in this unit often hide three technologies in one short paragraph, exactly like this. Read it sentence by sentence rather than looking for one answer. यस युनिटका प्रश्नले प्रायः छोटो अनुच्छेदमै तीन प्रविधि लुकाउँछन्, ठ्याक्कै यसरी। एउटा उत्तर खोज्नुभन्दा वाक्य–वाक्य गरी पढ्नुहोस्।

Mistakes that cost marks here

  • Saying cloud computing and distributed computing are the same. Cloud is about whose computer; distributed is about how many.
  • Defining big data as "mining big data". Big data is the material, data mining is the activity.
  • Calling a laptop on Wi-Fi an IoT device. IoT is for things that are not computers.
  • Swapping VR and AR. VR replaces the world, AR adds to it.
  • Saying encryption hides the method. The algorithm is usually public — the key is what is secret.
Cloud र distributed उस्तै भन्नु: cloud — कम्प्युटर कसको; distributed — कतिवटा। Big data लाई "ठूलो data को mining" भन्नु: big data सामग्री, data mining काम। Wi-Fi मा जोडिएको ल्यापटपलाई IoT भन्नु: IoT कम्प्युटर नभएका वस्तुका लागि हो। VR र AR साट्नु: VR ले संसार हटाउँछ, AR ले थप्छ। Encryption ले तरिका लुकाउँछ भन्नु: एल्गोरिदम प्रायः सार्वजनिक हुन्छ — गोप्य त key हो।

Memory hook — VR replaces, AR adds. Big data is the pile; data mining is the digging. सम्झने तरिका — VR ले हटाउँछ, AR ले थप्छ। Big data थुप्रो, data mining खन्ने काम।

2 marks Practice question

What is artificial intelligence? Write any two of its applications. Artificial intelligence भनेको के हो? यसका कुनै दुई प्रयोग लेख्नुहोस्।

Artificial intelligence is the branch of computer science that develops machines and programs able to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognising images, making decisions and learning from experience.

Applications: voice assistants; face recognition; machine translation; medical diagnosis; self-driving vehicles; fraud detection. Any two.

AI भनेको कम्प्युटर विज्ञानको त्यो शाखा हो जसले सामान्यतया मानवीय बुद्धि चाहिने काम — भाषा बुझ्ने, तस्बिर चिन्ने, निर्णय गर्ने र अनुभवबाट सिक्ने — गर्न सक्ने मेसिन र प्रोग्राम बनाउँछ। प्रयोग: भ्वाइस असिस्टेन्ट, अनुहार पहिचान, मेसिन अनुवाद, रोग पहिचान, स्वचालित सवारी, ठगी पत्ता लगाउने — कुनै दुई।
4 marks Practice question

Differentiate between virtual reality and augmented reality with examples. Virtual reality र augmented reality बीचको फरक उदाहरणसहित लेख्नुहोस्।

Virtual Reality (VR) creates a completely computer-generated environment that replaces the real world. The user wears a headset and cannot see the real surroundings. It needs special hardware. Example: a flight simulator used for pilot training; a VR game.

Augmented Reality (AR) adds computer-generated information on top of the real world, which the user can still see. It usually works on an ordinary smartphone. Example: an app that shows how furniture would look in your room; navigation arrows drawn on a live camera view.

The single difference to state clearly: VR replaces reality, AR adds to it.

VR ले पूरै कम्प्युटरले बनाएको वातावरण दिन्छ जसले वास्तविक संसार प्रतिस्थापन गर्छ; हेडसेट लगाएपछि वरिपरि देखिँदैन; विशेष हार्डवेयर चाहिन्छ। उदाहरण: पाइलट तालिमको फ्लाइट सिमुलेटर। AR ले वास्तविक संसारमाथि सूचना थप्छ, प्रयोगकर्ताले वरिपरि देख्दै गर्छ; प्रायः साधारण स्मार्टफोनमै चल्छ। उदाहरण: कोठामा फर्निचर कस्तो देखिन्छ देखाउने app। मुख्य फरक: VR ले हटाउँछ, AR ले थप्छ।
2 marks Practice question

What is cloud computing? Write any one advantage and one disadvantage. Cloud computing भनेको के हो? कुनै एक फाइदा र एक बेफाइदा लेख्नुहोस्।

Cloud computing is the use of computing resources — storage, processing and software — owned and maintained by a service provider and accessed over the Internet, paying for what is used rather than buying the equipment.

Advantage: no hardware to purchase or maintain; data can be reached from anywhere; capacity can be increased when needed.

Disadvantage: it requires a reliable Internet connection; data is stored on someone else's computer, raising privacy and security concerns; the cost is ongoing.

Cloud computing भनेको सेवा प्रदायकले राखेका कम्प्युटिङ स्रोत — भण्डारण, प्रशोधन, सफ्टवेयर — इन्टरनेटमार्फत प्रयोग गर्नु हो; उपकरण किन्नुको साटो जति चलायो त्यति तिर्ने। फाइदा: हार्डवेयर किन्नु/मर्मत गर्नु नपर्ने; जहाँबाट पनि पुग्न सकिने; चाहिएअनुसार बढाउन मिल्ने। बेफाइदा: भरपर्दो इन्टरनेट चाहिने; data अर्कैको कम्प्युटरमा रहने; खर्च निरन्तर चलिरहने।
2 marks Practice question

What is encryption? What is the role of the key? Encryption भनेको के हो? Key को भूमिका के हो?

Encryption is the process of converting plaintext (a readable message) into ciphertext (an unreadable form) so that only an authorised person can read it. Decryption is the reverse process.

The key is the secret value used by the algorithm to perform the conversion. The algorithm is usually publicly known; the security depends on keeping the key secret, and a different key produces a different ciphertext from the same message.

Encryption भनेको plaintext (पढ्न मिल्ने सन्देश) लाई ciphertext (पढ्न नमिल्ने रूप) मा बदल्ने प्रक्रिया हो, ताकि अधिकार पाएकोले मात्र पढोस्। Decryption उल्टो प्रक्रिया। Key भनेको एल्गोरिदमले रूपान्तरण गर्न प्रयोग गर्ने गोप्य मान हो। एल्गोरिदम प्रायः सार्वजनिक हुन्छ; सुरक्षा key गोप्य राख्नुमा भर पर्छ, र फरक key ले उही सन्देशबाट फरक ciphertext बनाउँछ।

Key points from this unit

  • Emerging means moving into use fast enough that the rules have not settled — not merely new.
  • AI: machines doing what needs human intelligence, and learning from experience.
  • Cloud = whose computer. Distributed = how many computers.
  • IoT: things that are not computers, put on the network.
  • Big data is the material (volume, velocity, variety); data mining is the activity.
