1.1  What a computer is

The definition, and why it is worded that way

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it according to a set of stored instructions, produces information as output, and can store both for later use.

Every part of that sentence is doing work. Electronic rules out an abacus. Stored instructions is the important one: a calculator also processes data, but you must press each key. A computer holds the whole list of steps — the program — inside itself and runs it without being told again at every step.

The name is misleading. "Computer" means "one who computes", and calculation is only one of the things it does. It also stores, sorts, searches, compares, draws and communicates. Judge a machine by the four stages below, not by its name or its size.

परिभाषा, र यसरी नै किन लेखिन्छ

कम्प्युटर एउटा इलेक्ट्रोनिक उपकरण हो, जसले data लिन्छ, भण्डारण गरिएका निर्देशनअनुसार त्यसलाई प्रशोधन गर्छ, आउटपुटका रूपमा information दिन्छ, र दुवैलाई पछि प्रयोग गर्न राख्न सक्छ।

यो वाक्यको हरेक भागको काम छ। इलेक्ट्रोनिक भनेपछि अबाकस बाहिरियो। सबैभन्दा महत्त्वपूर्ण भण्डारण गरिएका निर्देशन हो: क्यालकुलेटरले पनि data प्रशोधन गर्छ, तर हरेक बटन तपाईंले थिच्नुपर्छ। कम्प्युटरले चरणहरूको पूरै सूची — अर्थात् प्रोग्राम — आफैंभित्र राख्छ र हरेक चरणमा फेरि नभनिकनै चलाउँछ।

नाम भने अलमल्याउने खालको छ। "Computer" को अर्थ "गणना गर्ने" हो, तर गणना त उसले गर्ने धेरै काममध्ये एउटा मात्र हो। उसले राख्छ, क्रम मिलाउँछ, खोज्छ, तुलना गर्छ, कोर्छ र सञ्चार पनि गर्छ। मेसिनलाई नाम वा आकारले होइन, तलका चार चरणले जाँच्नुहोस्।

Data and information are not the same word. Data is raw and on its own means little: 72, 65, 88. Information is data that has been processed into something a person can use: "the class average is 75". The computer's whole job is turning the first into the second — which is why the definition names both. Data र information उस्तै शब्द होइनन्। Data कच्चा हुन्छ र आफैंमा थोरै अर्थ राख्छ: ७२, ६५, ८८। Information भनेको प्रशोधन भइसकेको, मान्छेले काम लगाउन सक्ने कुरा हो: "कक्षाको औसत ७५ छ"। कम्प्युटरको पूरै काम पहिलोलाई दोस्रो बनाउनु हो — त्यसैले परिभाषामा दुवैको नाम आउँछ।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A pocket calculator takes numbers in, adds them, and shows a result. By the definition above, is it a computer? पकेट क्यालकुलेटरले संख्या लिन्छ, जोड्छ र नतिजा देखाउँछ। माथिको परिभाषाअनुसार के यो कम्प्युटर हो?

No. It is electronic and it does process input into output, so it passes two parts of the test. What it cannot do is hold a list of instructions and follow them on its own — you press every key yourself. That is the line the definition draws, and it is why "stored instructions" is in it. A programmable scientific calculator sits much closer to the line, which is a fair thing to notice.

होइन। यो इलेक्ट्रोनिक छ र इनपुटलाई आउटपुटमा बदल्छ पनि, त्यसैले जाँचका दुई भाग पास गर्छ। तर निर्देशनको सूची राखेर आफैं पछ्याउन सक्दैन — हरेक बटन तपाईं आफैं थिच्नुहुन्छ। परिभाषाले कोर्ने रेखा यही हो, र "भण्डारण गरिएका निर्देशन" त्यसैले राखिएको हो।

1.2  Characteristics of a computer

What a computer is good at

Speed — it works in millionths and billionths of a second. A task that would take a clerk a week can finish while you wait.

Accuracy — it does not make mistakes in its own arithmetic.

Diligence — it does not get bored or tired. The ten-thousandth calculation is done exactly as carefully as the first.

Versatility — the same machine prints a bill, plays a song and checks spelling, because changing the program changes the job.

Storage — it keeps very large amounts of data and finds any part of it again.

Automation — once started, it carries out a whole job without a person at each step.

Reliability — it gives the same answer to the same question every time.

कम्प्युटर केमा राम्रो छ

गति — ऊ सेकेन्डको दशलाख र अर्बौं भागमा काम गर्छ। कर्मचारीलाई हप्ता लाग्ने काम तपाईं पर्खिरहँदै सकिन्छ।

शुद्धता — आफ्नै गणनामा ऊ गल्ती गर्दैन।

लगनशीलता — ऊ दिक्क हुँदैन, थाक्दैन। दश हजारौं गणना पनि पहिलोजत्तिकै ध्यान दिएर हुन्छ।

बहुउपयोगिता — उही मेसिनले बिल छाप्छ, गीत बजाउँछ र हिज्जे जाँच्छ, किनभने प्रोग्राम फेर्दा काम फेरिन्छ।

भण्डारण — ऊ धेरै ठूलो मात्रामा data राख्छ र त्यसको जुनसुकै भाग फेरि भेट्टाउँछ।

स्वचालन — एकपटक सुरु गरेपछि हरेक चरणमा मान्छे नचाहिने गरी पूरै काम सिध्याउँछ।

भरपर्दोपन — उही प्रश्नको उत्तर उसले हरेक पटक उही दिन्छ।

GIGO — the one that costs marks. "Accuracy" means the machine does not make arithmetic mistakes. It does not mean the answer is right. If you type a student's marks as 850 instead of 85, the computer will average 850 perfectly and hand you a wrong result. Garbage In, Garbage Out. Accuracy is a property of the machine; correctness is a property of the data you gave it. GIGO — यहीँ अंक जान्छ। "शुद्धता" भनेको मेसिनले गणनामा गल्ती गर्दैन भन्ने हो। उत्तर नै सही हुन्छ भनेको होइन। विद्यार्थीको अंक ८५ का साटो ८५० टाइप गर्नुभयो भने कम्प्युटरले ८५० लाई ठ्याक्कै सही औसत निकालिदिन्छ, र गलत नतिजा दिन्छ। Garbage In, Garbage Out. शुद्धता मेसिनको गुण हो; सहीपन तपाईंले दिएको data को गुण हो।
Example 1 Which characteristic is the answer?

Question: a school enters 1,200 students' marks. The computer calculates every average in two seconds, and every one of them is wrong because the marks were entered from the wrong register. Which characteristic does this show, and which does it NOT disprove?

Two characteristics are in play and the question separates them. Decide which one the two seconds demonstrates, and which one the wrong answers do not touch, before you open this. दुई विशेषता खेलमा छन् र प्रश्नले तिनलाई छुट्याउँछ। दुई सेकेन्डले कुन देखाउँछ, र गलत उत्तरले कुनलाई छुँदैन — खोल्नुअघि निर्णय गर्नुहोस्।

Working
Two seconds for 1,200 averages -> SPEED Every average arithmetically correct -> ACCURACY is intact The answers are useless -> the DATA was wrong, not the machine

The marks lost here are usually lost by writing "this shows the computer is not accurate". It is accurate — it averaged exactly what it was given. The example is GIGO, and naming GIGO is what earns the mark. यहाँ अंक प्रायः "यसले कम्प्युटर शुद्ध छैन भन्ने देखाउँछ" लेखेर जान्छ। ऊ शुद्ध छ — उसलाई दिइएकै कुराको ठ्याक्कै औसत निकाल्यो। यो उदाहरण GIGO हो, र GIGO नाम लिनुले नै अंक दिलाउँछ।

1.3  Modern applications of the computer

Where computers are actually used in Nepal

Education — school records and results, online classes, and the material you are reading now.

Banking and money — accounts, ATMs, mobile banking, and the remittance that reaches a village from abroad in minutes rather than weeks.

Government — citizenship and land records, the national ID, online passport applications, and tax filing.

Health — patient records at a hospital or health post, laboratory machines, and telemedicine that lets a doctor in Kathmandu advise a health worker in a hill district.

Communication — email, messaging and video calls.

Transport and tourism — online bus and flight booking, and hotel systems.

Weather and disaster warning — forecasting the monsoon and giving flood warning downstream, which in Nepal is a use that saves lives.

Agriculture — market prices, weather advice and soil records reaching farmers by phone.

नेपालमा कम्प्युटर साँच्चै कहाँ प्रयोग हुन्छ

शिक्षा — विद्यालयको अभिलेख र नतिजा, अनलाइन कक्षा, र तपाईंले अहिले पढिरहेको यो सामग्री।

बैंकिङ र पैसा — खाता, ATM, मोबाइल बैंकिङ, र विदेशबाट गाउँसम्म हप्तौंमा होइन मिनेटमै पुग्ने रेमिट्यान्स।

सरकारी काम — नागरिकता र जग्गाको अभिलेख, राष्ट्रिय परिचयपत्र, अनलाइन राहदानी आवेदन, र कर दाखिला।

स्वास्थ्य — अस्पताल र स्वास्थ्य चौकीमा बिरामीको अभिलेख, प्रयोगशालाका मेसिन, र काठमाडौंको डाक्टरले पहाडी जिल्लाको स्वास्थ्यकर्मीलाई सल्लाह दिन मिल्ने टेलिमेडिसिन।

सञ्चार — इमेल, म्यासेज र भिडियो कल।

यातायात र पर्यटन — अनलाइन बस र हवाई टिकट, होटेलका प्रणाली।

मौसम र विपद् सूचना — मनसुनको पूर्वानुमान र तल्लो तटमा बाढीको चेतावनी — नेपालमा यो ज्यान बचाउने प्रयोग हो।

कृषि — बजार भाउ, मौसमको सल्लाह र माटोको अभिलेख फोनबाटै किसानसम्म।

1.4  Classification of computers

Five questions, not one list

Computers are classified on five different bases, and this is where the unit's marks usually are. The mistake to avoid is treating them as one long list. They are five separate questions, and every computer has an answer to all five at once.

By size — supercomputer, mainframe, minicomputer, microcomputer. This asks how much work the machine can do at once.

By data handling — analog, digital, hybrid. This asks what kind of signal it works with. Analog measures something that changes smoothly, like a voltage. Digital counts in 0s and 1s. Hybrid does both: a fuel pump measures flowing petrol and prints a digital bill.

By purpose — general purpose (you can load new programs onto it) or special purpose (built for one job, like the computer in a washing machine).

By model — the physical form: desktop, laptop, palmtop and similar.

By brand — IBM PC, IBM compatible, Apple.

पाँच प्रश्न, एउटै सूची होइन

कम्प्युटरको वर्गीकरण पाँच फरक आधार मा हुन्छ, र यस युनिटका अंक प्रायः यहीँ हुन्छन्। बच्नुपर्ने गल्ती — यिनलाई एउटै लामो सूची ठान्नु। यी पाँच छुट्टाछुट्टै प्रश्न हुन्, र हरेक कम्प्युटरसँग पाँचैको उत्तर एकैचोटि हुन्छ।

आकार अनुसार — supercomputer, mainframe, minicomputer, microcomputer। यसले मेसिनले एकैपटक कति काम गर्न सक्छ सोध्छ।

डाटा ह्यान्डलिङ अनुसार — analog, digital, hybrid। यसले कस्तो सङ्केतसँग काम गर्छ सोध्छ। Analog ले बिस्तारै बदलिने कुरा नाप्छ, जस्तै भोल्टेज। Digital ले ० र १ मा गन्छ। Hybrid ले दुवै गर्छ: पेट्रोल पम्पले बग्ने पेट्रोल नाप्छ र digital बिल छाप्छ।

प्रयोजन अनुसार — general purpose (नयाँ प्रोग्राम हाल्न मिल्ने) वा special purpose (एउटै कामका लागि बनेको, जस्तै वासिङ मेसिनभित्रको कम्प्युटर)।

मोडेल अनुसार — भौतिक आकार: desktop, laptop, palmtop र यस्तै।

ब्रान्ड अनुसार — IBM PC, IBM compatible, Apple।

Where these categories come from

The curriculum names the five bases and stops there. Its exact words for topic 1.4 are "on the basis of size, on the data handling, on the purpose, on the model, on the brand" — and it lists no categories at all under any of them.

So the lists above are the standard textbook treatment, not quoted curriculum text. For size, data handling and purpose that distinction barely matters: every book and every paper uses the same categories. Model and brand are different. Books disagree about them, and IBM PC / IBM compatible / Apple is a convention from an older generation of computing that some teachers no longer use. Check these two against your own textbook and what your teacher writes on the board, and follow those if they differ from this page.

पाठ्यक्रमले पाँच आधार मात्र नाम लिन्छ, त्यहीँ रोकिन्छ। विषय १.४ मा यसका ठ्याक्कै शब्द — "on the basis of size, on the data handling, on the purpose, on the model, on the brand" — र कुनै पनि आधारमुनि कुनै वर्ग लेखिएको छैन। त्यसैले माथिका सूची मानक पाठ्यपुस्तकको प्रचलन हुन्, पाठ्यक्रमकै उद्धरण होइनन्। आकार, डाटा ह्यान्डलिङ र प्रयोजनमा यो फरकले खासै अर्थ राख्दैन — सबै पुस्तक र सबै प्रश्नपत्रले उही वर्ग चलाउँछन्। मोडेल र ब्रान्ड भने फरक हुन्। पुस्तकहरूबीच मत बाझिन्छ, र IBM PC / IBM compatible / Apple पुरानो पुस्ताको प्रचलन हो, जुन कतिपय शिक्षकले अहिले चलाउनुहुन्न। यी दुईलाई आफ्नै पाठ्यपुस्तक र शिक्षकले बोर्डमा लेख्नुभएकोसँग मिलाएर हेर्नुहोस्, र फरक परे त्यही मान्नुहोस्।

Practise the judgement · निर्णय अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्

Which basis of classification is being used?

What you will learn: to tell the five bases apart on a real case rather than by reciting the list. Six cases, and two of them answer the same basis for different reasons. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: सूची घोकेर होइन, वास्तविक केसमा पाँच आधार छुट्याउन। छ केस छन्, र तीमध्ये दुईले फरक कारणले उही आधारको उत्तर दिन्छन्।

How to use this

Before pressing anything, say out loud what question the case is answering. How much work? size. What kind of signal? data handling. How many different jobs? purpose. What shape? model. Who made it? brand.

Case 3 is the one worth reading the reason for even if you get it right. The washing machine is also micro and also digital — more than one answer is true of the machine, and only one answers the question asked.

केही थिच्नुअघि यो केसले कुन प्रश्नको उत्तर दिँदै छ भन्नुहोस्। कति काम? आकार। कस्तो सङ्केत? डाटा ह्यान्डलिङ। कति फरक काम? प्रयोजन। कस्तो आकार? मोडेल। कसले बनायो? ब्रान्ड। केस ३ को कारण सही भए पनि पढ्नुहोस्: वासिङ मेसिन micro पनि हो, digital पनि — मेसिनका लागि एकभन्दा बढी उत्तर सही हुन्छन्, तर सोधिएको प्रश्नको उत्तर एउटै हुन्छ।
Example 2 Classify one machine five ways

Question: a student's laptop, made by Apple, used for homework, browsing and music. Place it in all five classifications.

Work down the five bases in order and answer each one separately. Write your five answers before opening this. पाँच आधार क्रमैसँग लिनुहोस् र हरेकको छुट्टै उत्तर दिनुहोस्। खोल्नुअघि आफ्ना पाँच उत्तर लेख्नुहोस्।

Working
by size MICROCOMPUTER one person uses it at a time by data handling DIGITAL it works in 0s and 1s by purpose GENERAL PURPOSE homework, browsing and music are different jobs by model LAPTOP that is its physical form by brand APPLE who made it

One machine, five answers, none of them competing. A question that gives you a machine and asks for "the classification" is asking for all five unless it names a basis. एउटै मेसिन, पाँच उत्तर, कुनै पनि एकअर्कासँग बाझिँदैनन्। मेसिन दिएर "वर्गीकरण" सोध्ने प्रश्नले आधार नतोकेसम्म पाँचै खोजेको हुन्छ।

Mistakes that cost marks here

  • Saying a computer is "not accurate" when the output is wrong. If the input was wrong, that is GIGO. Accuracy describes the machine's arithmetic, not your typing. आउटपुट गलत हुँदा कम्प्युटर "शुद्ध छैन" भन्नु। इनपुट गलत थियो भने त्यो GIGO हो। शुद्धताले मेसिनको गणना जनाउँछ, तपाईंको टाइपिङ होइन।
  • Mixing the five bases into one list. "Supercomputer, digital, laptop" is not a classification — it is one answer from each of three different questions. पाँच आधारलाई एउटै सूचीमा मिसाउनु। "Supercomputer, digital, laptop" वर्गीकरण होइन — यी तीन फरक प्रश्नका एक–एक उत्तर हुन्।
  • Calling a calculator a computer. It has no stored program; you press every key. क्यालकुलेटरलाई कम्प्युटर भन्नु। त्यसमा भण्डारण गरिएको प्रोग्राम छैन; हरेक बटन तपाईं थिच्नुहुन्छ।
  • Using "data" and "information" as the same word. Data goes in, information comes out; the processing in between is the difference. "Data" र "information" लाई उही शब्द ठान्नु। Data भित्र जान्छ, information बाहिर आउँछ; बीचको प्रशोधन नै फरक हो।

Data in, information out. Accuracy is the machine's; correctness is yours. Data भित्र, information बाहिर। शुद्धता मेसिनको, सहीपन तपाईंको।

Practical work — this unit's other 10 hours

Why this section exists

This unit is 18 hours, not 8. The 8 hours above are theory; the curriculum gives this unit 10 more hours of practical work, and lists six activities for them.

Those activities are worth real marks. Of the 50 internal marks for this subject, 18 are practical work (15 for doing it, 3 for keeping records) and 12 are project work (10 for doing it, 2 for records). A further 5 are a viva in which you explain your own practical and project work.

This website cannot do any of them for you. They are chart paper, presentations and a speech to your class. What it can do is tell you exactly what is required, what you need, and what to keep so that the marks are there when they are counted.

Record keepingThe 3 marks most often thrown away

Three of the 18 practical marks are for records, not for the work. A record is kept per activity and should contain: the activity number and title, the date, what you did, what you used, what you produced (with a photograph if it is a chart), and one or two sentences on what you learned or what went wrong. Keep them in one file, in order.

The curriculum adds two things worth knowing in advance. The practical examination is conducted in the presence of internal and external supervisors, and it assesses both the product and your skill in using equipment — so being able to explain your chart matters as much as the chart. And project work is assessed on the report and the presentation of it; when a project is presented by a group, every member submits their own short reflection on the report, so a group project is still individual work at the end.

Independent practiceTwo you can do on your own, today

Not from the curriculum's list — these are short exercises to test whether the theory above actually holds in your hands. No marks, no teacher needed.

One. Pick any five machines you can see from where you are sitting — a phone, a calculator, a TV, a fridge with a display, a laptop. For each, decide whether it contains a computer by the definition in 1.1, and if it does, place it on all five bases from 1.4. Write the five answers down. Where you hesitate is where to re-read.

Two. Find one wrong output you have actually seen — a bill, a result sheet, a mistyped name on a document. Decide whether the machine or the data was at fault, and write one sentence saying which and why. If it was the data, you have found a real GIGO example, and it will serve you better in the exam than the one in this lesson.

2 marks Practice question

Define a computer. कम्प्युटरको परिभाषा दिनुहोस्।

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it according to a set of stored instructions, gives information as output, and stores data and instructions for later use.

Name all four stages — input, process, output, storage — and the stored program. An answer that says only "a machine that calculates" does not reach full marks.

चारै चरण — input, process, output, storage — र भण्डारण गरिएको प्रोग्रामको नाम लिनुहोस्। "गणना गर्ने मेसिन" मात्र लेखेको उत्तरले पूरा अंक पाउँदैन।
2 marks Practice question

Differentiate between data and information with one example of each. Data र information बीचको फरक, हरेकको एक–एक उदाहरणसहित लेख्नुहोस्।

Data is raw, unprocessed facts that mean little on their own — for example the marks 72, 65, 88.

Information is data that has been processed into a usable form — for example "the class average is 75".

The difference is the processing in between, which is what the computer does.

Data कच्चा तथ्य, जस्तै अंक ७२, ६५, ८८। Information प्रशोधन भएर काम लाग्ने रूपमा आएको, जस्तै "कक्षाको औसत ७५ छ"। बीचको प्रशोधन नै फरक हो।

4 marks Practice question

A computer produced a wrong salary report. The company says the computer is unreliable. Explain whether you agree, using a characteristic of computers. कम्प्युटरले गलत तलब प्रतिवेदन निकाल्यो। कम्पनी भन्छ कम्प्युटर भरपर्दो छैन। कम्प्युटरको एउटा विशेषता प्रयोग गरी तपाईं सहमत हुनुहुन्छ कि छैन व्याख्या गर्नुहोस्।

Usually disagree. A computer is accurate and reliable: it processes exactly what it is given, and gives the same answer every time. A wrong report almost always means wrong input or a wrong program — Garbage In, Garbage Out.

Say which it is: wrong data entered, or a mistake in the instructions. Both are human errors, not machine ones.

प्रायः असहमत। कम्प्युटर शुद्ध र भरपर्दो हुन्छ: दिइएकै कुरा ठ्याक्कै प्रशोधन गर्छ र हरेक पटक उही उत्तर दिन्छ। गलत प्रतिवेदन भनेको प्रायः गलत इनपुट वा गलत प्रोग्राम — Garbage In, Garbage Out। कुन हो भन्नुहोस्: गलत data भरियो, कि निर्देशनमै गल्ती। दुवै मानवीय गल्ती हुन्, मेसिनको होइन।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • A computer accepts data, processes it by stored instructions, outputs information and stores both. The stored program is what a calculator lacks. कम्प्युटरले data लिन्छ, भण्डारण गरिएका निर्देशन ले प्रशोधन गर्छ, information दिन्छ र दुवै राख्छ। क्यालकुलेटरमा नहुने कुरा भण्डारण गरिएको प्रोग्राम नै हो।
  • Data is raw; information is processed. The computer's job is the arrow between them. Data कच्चा; information प्रशोधित। कम्प्युटरको काम दुईबीचको तीर हो।
  • Speed, accuracy, diligence, versatility, storage, automation, reliability. गति, शुद्धता, लगनशीलता, बहुउपयोगिता, भण्डारण, स्वचालन, भरपर्दोपन।
  • GIGO. Accuracy is the machine's property; correctness depends on your data. GIGO। शुद्धता मेसिनको गुण; सहीपन तपाईंको data मा भर पर्छ।
  • Five bases: size, data handling, purpose, model, brand — and every computer answers all five at once. पाँच आधार: आकार, डाटा ह्यान्डलिङ, प्रयोजन, मोडेल, ब्रान्ड — र हरेक कम्प्युटरले पाँचैको उत्तर एकैचोटि दिन्छ।
  • Analog measures, digital counts, hybrid does both. Analog नाप्छ, digital गन्छ, hybrid दुवै गर्छ।
