1.1  Matter, molecule and atom

Definition — learn this wording

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space. A wire, a chip, the air around the board — all matter.

A molecule is the smallest particle of a substance that still has the properties of that substance. Split a water molecule and you no longer have water.

An atom is the smallest particle of an element. It is built from three subatomic particles: the proton (positive) and neutron (no charge) in the nucleus, and the electron (negative) orbiting in shells.

Electronics is the study of what those outermost electrons do. Everything in this subject — conduction, doping, a diode — comes back to whether an electron is free to move.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Matter भनेको जुनसुकै कुरा जसको द्रव्यमान हुन्छ र ठाउँ ओगट्छ। तार, चिप, बोर्ड वरिपरिको हावा — सबै matter हुन्।

Molecule भनेको कुनै पदार्थको त्यस्तो सबैभन्दा सानो कण, जसमा त्यही पदार्थका गुण कायम रहन्छन्। पानीको molecule फुटाए त्यो पानी रहँदैन।

Atom भनेको तत्व को सबैभन्दा सानो कण हो। यसमा तीन उप-आणविक कण हुन्छन्: nucleus भित्र proton (धनात्मक) र neutron (चार्ज नभएको), अनि शेलमा घुम्ने electron (ऋणात्मक)।

इलेक्ट्रोनिक्स भनेको ती सबैभन्दा बाहिरी electron ले के गर्छन् भन्ने अध्ययन हो। यस विषयका सबै कुरा — चालकता, doping, diode — electron चल्न स्वतन्त्र छ कि छैन भन्नेमै फर्किन्छ।

1.2  Kirchhoff's laws — KCL and KVL

Definition — learn this wording

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL): at any junction, the total current flowing in equals the total current flowing out. Nothing is created or lost at a joint.

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL): around any closed loop, the supply voltage equals the sum of the voltage drops. Energy given by the source is entirely used up by the loop.

These two laws are why a technician can reason about a board without measuring every point: if three currents at a junction are known, the fourth is fixed.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL): कुनै पनि जोडमा भित्र आउने कुल current र बाहिर जाने कुल current बराबर हुन्छ। जोडमा केही बन्दैन, केही हराउँदैन।

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL): कुनै पनि बन्द loop मा सप्लाईको भोल्टेज र सबै भोल्टेज ड्रपको योग बराबर हुन्छ। स्रोतले दिएको ऊर्जा loop ले पूरै खपत गर्छ।

यी दुई नियमकै कारण टेक्निसियनले हरेक बिन्दु नापेर हेर्नु पर्दैन: जोडमा तीन current थाहा भए चौथो आफैँ निश्चित हुन्छ।

Example 1 Finding a missing current with KCL

Question: at a junction, 5 A and 3 A flow in. One branch carries 6 A out. What does the remaining branch carry?

Work it out before you read on — the whole law is one sentence, and you already have it.पढ्नुअघि आफैँ निकाल्नुहोस् — पूरै नियम एउटै वाक्य हो, र त्यो तपाईंसँग छ।

Step 1 — add everything going in. 5 + 3 = 8 A.

Step 2 — add everything going out that you know. 6 A on the one named branch.

Step 3 — KCL says the two totals must match. 8 = 6 + I, so I = 2 A.

Answer
I = 2 A, flowing OUT of the junction

Say the direction, not just the number. A junction question with an unsigned answer is only half answered, because "2 A" is true of a current going either way. सङ्ख्या मात्र होइन, दिशा पनि लेख्नुहोस्। दिशा नलेखिएको उत्तर आधा मात्र हो — किनकि "२ A" दुवै दिशाको current लाई मिल्छ।

Example 2 Finding a missing voltage drop with KVL

Question: a 12 V supply feeds a loop with three resistors. R₁ drops 5 V and R₂ drops 4 V. What does R₃ drop?

Step 1 — write the law for this loop. Supply = sum of drops, so 12 = 5 + 4 + R₃.

Step 2 — collect the known drops. 5 + 4 = 9 V.

Step 3 — subtract. R₃ = 12 − 9 = 3 V.

Answer
R₃ drops 3 V

This is the check a technician makes on a suspect power rail: if the drops do not add up to the supply, either a reading is wrong or there is a fault in the loop. बिग्रिएको पावर रेल जाँच्दा टेक्निसियनले यही हेर्छ: ड्रपको योग सप्लाईसँग मिलेन भने या त कुनै रिडिङ गलत छ, या loop मै खराबी छ।

1.3  Semiconductors and doping

Definition — learn this wording

A semiconductor conducts better than an insulator but worse than a conductor. Silicon and germanium are the two used. Each has four valence electrons, so a pure crystal has none spare and barely conducts.

Doping is deliberately adding a small amount of impurity to a pure semiconductor to increase its conductivity.

N-type — add a pentavalent impurity (5 valence electrons: phosphorus, arsenic). Four electrons bond; the fifth is spare and free to move.

P-type — add a trivalent impurity (3 valence electrons: boron, gallium). One bond is left unfilled, and that gap is a hole.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

Semiconductor ले insulator भन्दा राम्रो तर conductor भन्दा कम बिजुली चलाउँछ। Silicon र germanium दुई प्रयोग हुन्छन्। दुवैमा चार valence electron हुन्छन्, त्यसैले शुद्ध क्रिस्टलमा फाल्तु electron हुँदैन र मुस्किलले चल्छ।

Doping भनेको चालकता बढाउन शुद्ध semiconductor मा जानाजान थोरै अशुद्धि मिसाउनु हो।

N-type — pentavalent अशुद्धि (५ valence electron: phosphorus, arsenic) मिसाउनुहोस्। चार वटाले बन्धन बनाउँछन्; पाँचौं फाल्तु रहन्छ र चल्न स्वतन्त्र हुन्छ।

P-type — trivalent अशुद्धि (३ valence electron: boron, gallium) मिसाउनुहोस्। एउटा बन्धन खाली रहन्छ, र त्यही खाली ठाउँ hole हो।

Majority and minority carriers

Both kinds of carrier exist in both materials. What changes is which one dominates.

In N-type: electrons are the majority carrier, holes the minority.

In P-type: holes are the majority carrier, electrons the minority.

The minority carriers come from heat breaking bonds in the silicon itself, which is why a hot device leaks more current than a cold one.

बहुसंख्यक र अल्पसंख्यक वाहक

दुवै किसिमका वाहक दुवै पदार्थमा हुन्छन्। फरक यत्ति हो कि कुन हावी हुन्छ।

N-type मा: electron बहुसंख्यक, hole अल्पसंख्यक वाहक हो।

P-type मा: hole बहुसंख्यक, electron अल्पसंख्यक वाहक हो।

अल्पसंख्यक वाहक silicon भित्रै तापले बन्धन टुट्दा बन्छन् — त्यसैले तातो उपकरणले चिसो भन्दा बढी current चुहाउँछ।

Practise the judgement · निर्णय अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्

P-type or N-type?

What you will learn: to read a description and decide which material it is, so the "differentiate P-type and N-type" question is answered from recognition rather than from a memorised pair of sentences. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: विवरण पढेर कुन पदार्थ हो छुट्याउन — ताकि "P-type र N-type फरक छुट्याउनुहोस्" भन्ने प्रश्न घोकेको वाक्यबाट होइन, चिनेरै उत्तर दिन सकियोस्।

Why this matters: every diode, transistor and chip on a motherboard is built from these two materials joined together. If you cannot tell them apart, the PN junction in 1.4 has nothing to stand on.किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ: मदरबोर्डको हरेक diode, transistor र चिप यिनै दुई पदार्थ जोडेर बनेको हुन्छ। यी छुट्याउन नसके १.४ को PN junction अडिने ठाउँ नै रहँदैन।

1.4  The PN junction, forward bias and reverse bias

Definition — learn this wording

A PN junction is formed when a P-type and an N-type material are joined in a single crystal. It is the basis of the diode.

At the join, free electrons from the N side cross and fill holes on the P side. That leaves a thin strip with no free carriers — the depletion region — and exposed ions that create a barrier potential of about 0.7 V in silicon (0.3 V in germanium).

Forward bias: P to the positive terminal, N to the negative. The depletion region narrows, and once the supply exceeds the barrier, current flows.

Reverse bias: the connections are swapped. Carriers are pulled away from the join, the depletion region widens, and current stops — apart from a tiny leakage current carried by the minority carriers.

परिभाषा — यही शब्दमा सम्झनुहोस्

PN junction भनेको एउटै क्रिस्टलमा P-type र N-type पदार्थ जोडिँदा बन्ने जोड हो। Diode यसैमा आधारित छ।

जोडमा N तर्फका स्वतन्त्र electron पारि गएर P तर्फका hole भर्छन्। त्यसले स्वतन्त्र वाहक नभएको पातलो पट्टी छोड्छ — depletion region — र खुला ion हरूले silicon मा करिब ०.७ V (germanium मा ०.३ V) को barrier potential बनाउँछन्।

Forward bias: P धनात्मक टर्मिनलमा, N ऋणात्मकमा। Depletion region साँघुरो हुन्छ, र सप्लाईले barrier नाघेपछि current बग्छ।

Reverse bias: जडान उल्टिन्छ। वाहकहरू जोडबाट टाढा तानिन्छन्, depletion region चौडा हुन्छ, र current रोकिन्छ — अल्पसंख्यक वाहकले बोक्ने अति सानो leakage current बाहेक।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A silicon diode is connected in reverse bias. What happens to the depletion region? एउटा silicon diode reverse bias मा जोडियो। Depletion region मा के हुन्छ?

Reverse bias pulls both kinds of carrier away from the junction, so the region empty of carriers gets wider and the barrier grows. The diode blocks. It never disappears — the depletion region exists even with no supply connected at all.

Reverse bias ले दुवै किसिमका वाहकलाई जोडबाट टाढा तान्छ, त्यसैले वाहकविहीन क्षेत्र चौडा हुन्छ र barrier बढ्छ। Diode ले रोक्छ। यो कहिल्यै हराउँदैन — कुनै सप्लाई नजोडे पनि depletion region हुन्छ।

Mistakes that cost marks here

  • Saying N-type is negatively charged and P-type positively charged. Both are electrically neutral. The impurity atom brings its own protons with it — doping adds a carrier, not a charge. N-type ऋणात्मक र P-type धनात्मक चार्ज भएको भन्नु। दुवै विद्युतीय रूपमा उदासीन छन्। अशुद्धिको atom ले आफ्नै proton ल्याउँछ — doping ले वाहक थप्छ, चार्ज होइन।
  • Writing that no current at all flows in reverse bias. A very small leakage current flows, carried by the minority carriers. "No current" loses the mark that "negligible leakage current" earns. Reverse bias मा बिल्कुलै current बग्दैन भन्नु। अल्पसंख्यक वाहकले बोकेको सानो leakage current बग्छ। "Current छैन" भन्दा "नगण्य leakage current" ले अंक पाउँछ।
  • Confusing a hole with an electron that has moved. A hole is the absence of an electron in a bond, and it behaves like a positive carrier. Hole लाई सरेको electron सम्झनु। Hole भनेको बन्धनमा electron को अनुपस्थिति हो, र यो धनात्मक वाहकजस्तै व्यवहार गर्छ।
  • Mixing up KCL and KVL. Current at a junction, voltage round a loop. If the question shows a node, it is KCL; if it shows a closed path, it is KVL. KCL र KVL मिसाउनु। जोडमा current, loop मा voltage। प्रश्नले node देखाए KCL, बन्द बाटो देखाए KVL।

Doping adds a carrier, not a charge. Forward narrows, reverse widens. Doping ले वाहक थप्छ, चार्ज होइन। Forward ले साँघुरो, reverse ले चौडा बनाउँछ।

Define doping. Name one pentavalent and one trivalent impurity. Doping को परिभाषा दिनुहोस्। एउटा pentavalent र एउटा trivalent अशुद्धिको नाम लेख्नुहोस्।

Doping भनेको चालकता बढाउने उद्देश्यले शुद्ध semiconductor मा जानाजान थोरै अशुद्धि मिसाउनु हो। Pentavalent: phosphorus (वा arsenic, antimony)। Trivalent: boron (वा gallium, indium)।

Q2 3 marks

State Kirchhoff's Current Law. At a junction 4 A and 7 A flow in, and 5 A flows out on one branch. Find the current in the remaining branch. Kirchhoff's Current Law भन्नुहोस्। एउटा जोडमा ४ A र ७ A भित्र आउँछन्, र एउटा शाखाबाट ५ A बाहिर जान्छ। बाँकी शाखाको current निकाल्नुहोस्।

KCL: at any junction the total current flowing in equals the total current flowing out. In = 4 + 7 = 11 A. Known out = 5 A. So the remaining branch carries 11 − 5 = 6 A out of the junction.

KCL: कुनै पनि जोडमा भित्र आउने कुल current र बाहिर जाने कुल current बराबर हुन्छ। भित्र = ४ + ७ = ११ A। थाहा भएको बाहिर = ५ A। त्यसैले बाँकी शाखाले ११ − ५ = ६ A जोडबाट बाहिर बोक्छ।

Q3 3 marks

Explain how a depletion region forms at a PN junction, and what happens to it under reverse bias. PN junction मा depletion region कसरी बन्छ, र reverse bias मा त्यसमा के हुन्छ — व्याख्या गर्नुहोस्।

When P-type and N-type are joined, free electrons from the N side diffuse across and recombine with holes on the P side. The strip either side of the join is left with no free carriers and with exposed ions, forming the depletion region and a barrier potential of about 0.7 V in silicon. Under reverse bias the supply pulls carriers away from the junction, so the depletion region widens, the barrier increases, and conduction stops apart from a small leakage current due to minority carriers.

P-type र N-type जोडिँदा N तर्फका स्वतन्त्र electron पारि सरेर P तर्फका hole सँग पुनर्संयोजन हुन्छन्। जोडको दुवैतिरको पट्टीमा स्वतन्त्र वाहक रहँदैन र खुला ion बाँकी रहन्छन् — यसैले depletion region र silicon मा करिब ०.७ V को barrier potential बन्छ। Reverse bias मा सप्लाईले वाहकलाई जोडबाट टाढा तान्छ, त्यसैले depletion region चौडा हुन्छ, barrier बढ्छ, र अल्पसंख्यक वाहकको सानो leakage current बाहेक चालन रोकिन्छ।

Key points to revise — दोहोर्‍याउनुपर्ने मुख्य बुँदा

  • Matter → molecule → atom → proton, neutron, electron. Proton positive, neutron neutral, electron negative. Matter → molecule → atom → proton, neutron, electron। Proton धनात्मक, neutron उदासीन, electron ऋणात्मक।
  • KCL: current in = current out at a junction. KVL: supply = sum of drops around a loop. KCL: जोडमा भित्रको current = बाहिरको current। KVL: loop मा सप्लाई = ड्रपहरूको योग।
  • Silicon and germanium have four valence electrons. Doping adds a pentavalent impurity for N-type or a trivalent one for P-type. Silicon र germanium मा चार valence electron हुन्छन्। N-type लाई pentavalent, P-type लाई trivalent अशुद्धि मिसाइन्छ।
  • N-type: electrons majority, holes minority. P-type: holes majority, electrons minority. Both are electrically neutral. N-type: electron बहुसंख्यक, hole अल्पसंख्यक। P-type: hole बहुसंख्यक, electron अल्पसंख्यक। दुवै उदासीन छन्।
  • The depletion region has no free carriers and sets up a 0.7 V barrier in silicon. Forward bias narrows it and current flows; reverse bias widens it and current stops apart from leakage. Depletion region मा स्वतन्त्र वाहक हुँदैन र silicon मा ०.७ V को barrier बन्छ। Forward bias ले साँघुरो बनाउँछ र current बग्छ; reverse bias ले चौडा बनाउँछ र leakage बाहेक current रोकिन्छ।
