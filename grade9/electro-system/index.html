Grade 9 · Subject outline

Fundamentals of Electro-System

इलेक्ट्रो–सिस्टमका आधारभूत कुरा

syllabus outline 7 units 64 theory hrs 4 credit hrs · 128 working hrs
What this page is

This is the complete official syllabus for this subject, taken straight from the CDC Computer Engineering curriculum (2078). Use it as your revision checklist — everything the written exam can ask comes from this list.

Full bilingual notes, simulators and a quiz for this subject are being written next, in the same style as Data Structure & OOP using C++.

यो पेज के हो

यो CDC कम्प्युटर इन्जिनियरिङ पाठ्यक्रम (२०७८) बाट सिधै लिइएको यस विषयको पूरा आधिकारिक सिलेबस हो। यसैलाई दोहोर्‍याउने चेकलिस्ट बनाउनुहोस् — लिखित परीक्षामा सोधिने सबै कुरा यही सूचीबाट आउँछ।

यस विषयका पूरा द्विभाषिक नोट्स, सिमुलेटर र क्विज क्रमशः लेखिँदैछन् — डाटा स्ट्रक्चर र OOP (C++) कै शैलीमा।

≡

Units and contents

युनिट र विषयवस्तु

64 hrs total
1

Introduction to Electrostatics

स्थिर विद्युतको परिचय
9 hrs
  • Introduction to Electricity
  • History of Electricity
  • Types of Electricity — Dynamic, Static
  • Application and Uses of electricity
  • Atom and its components (electron, proton, neutron)
  • Atomic number, atomic weight, free electrons and electric charge
  • Coulomb's law and its derivation
  • Electric field, potential and potential difference
  • Electric Energy, voltage and its unit
2

Electric Fundamentals

विद्युतका आधारभूत कुरा
13 hrs
  • Basic electric terms — Voltage, Current, Resistance
  • Movement of electrons in a conductor
  • Sources of electricity — Hydro, Nuclear fission/fusion, Wind, Thermal, Solar
  • Conventional direction of electric current and its uses
  • Electrical resistance and its unit
  • Use and application of resistance in a circuit
  • Classification of objects by resistance — Conductor, Semiconductor, Insulator
  • Factors affecting the resistance
3

Electric Circuit

विद्युत परिपथ
13 hrs
  • Introduction to electric circuit
  • Types — Open, Close, Leakage, Series, Parallel, Mixed circuit
  • Resistance in series and parallel circuit
  • Ohm's Law
  • Kirchhoff's Current Law
  • Kirchhoff's Voltage Law
  • Numericals
4

Electrical Power and Theory

विद्युत शक्ति र सिद्धान्त
6 hrs
  • Introduction to electrical power
  • Unit of electrical power and its practical concept
  • Electrical energy, its unit and applications
  • Numericals
5

Cell and Capacitor

सेल र क्यापासिटर
10 hrs
  • Introduction to cell and battery
  • Types of cell — Primary, Secondary
  • Series and Parallel connection of a cell
  • Capacitor, capacitance and its units
  • Factors affecting capacitance
  • Characteristics of capacitance
  • Series and parallel plate capacitor
6

Magnetism and Electromagnetism

चुम्बकत्व र विद्युत चुम्बकत्व
8 hrs
  • Introduction to magnet and magnetism
  • Types of Magnet — Temporary, Permanent
  • Magnetic and non-magnetic materials
  • Magnetic terminologies — magnetic field, field density, lines of magnetic flux, flux density
  • Magnetic effect of current and its application
  • Principle of electromagnetism
  • Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction
7

Fundamentals of Current and Phase Current

करेन्ट र फेज करेन्टका आधारभूत कुरा
5 hrs
  • Introduction to AC and DC
  • Differences between AC and DC
  • Frequency, Amplitude and Time
  • Difference between single phase and three phase system
  • Uses and applications of three phase systems
