5

Internet and its Application

इन्टरनेट र यसको प्रयोग

10 hrs · 8 marks · 12 practical hrs

By the end of this unit you can… — यो युनिट सकिँदा तपाईं यी कुरा गर्न सक्नुहुनेछ

  1. Define the Internet in a way that does not confuse it with the Web.
  2. State its advantages, and the disadvantages an honest answer includes.
  3. List what is needed to connect, and say why each item is needed.
  4. Explain what a modem does and why the name is MOdulator-DEModulator.
  5. Name the nine Internet services in the syllabus and match each to a task.
  6. Give a Nepali example of e-commerce and of e-governance.
यो युनिट सकिँदा: इन्टरनेटलाई वेबसँग नमिसाई परिभाषित गर्न, फाइदा र इमानदार उत्तरले समेट्ने बेफाइदा भन्न, जोड्न के–के चाहिन्छ र किन चाहिन्छ भन्न, modem ले के गर्छ र नाम MOdulator-DEModulator किन हो बुझाउन, पाठ्यक्रमका नौ इन्टरनेट सेवा नाम लिई कामसँग मिलाउन, र e-commerce तथा e-governance का नेपाली उदाहरण दिन सक्नुहुनेछ।

5.1  What the Internet is, and what it gives you — इन्टरनेट के हो, र यसले के दिन्छ

A network of networks

The Internet is a global network of computer networks, connected by agreed rules called protocols, that lets any connected computer exchange information with any other.

The word to be careful with is Web. The Internet is the connection — the cables, the routers, the agreement on how to address a message. The World Wide Web is one service that runs on it, the one made of pages and links. E-mail is another service; so is file transfer. Saying "the Internet is a collection of websites" loses the mark, because it describes one service and calls it the whole network.

Advantages: information on almost any subject within seconds; communication by e-mail, message and video call at the price of the connection rather than the distance; online learning; e-commerce; e-governance; entertainment; and work that can be done from anywhere.

Disadvantages, which a four-mark answer should include: false information presented as fact; fraud and theft of money or identity; viruses; loss of privacy; wasted time; and content that is not suitable for children. A student who writes only advantages has answered half the question.

नेटवर्कहरूको नेटवर्क

इन्टरनेट भनेको कम्प्युटर नेटवर्कहरूको विश्वव्यापी नेटवर्क हो, जुन प्रोटोकल भनिने सहमत नियमले जोडिएको हुन्छ र जोडिएको जुनसुकै कम्प्युटरले अर्कोसँग सूचना साट्न सक्छ।

ध्यान दिनुपर्ने शब्द वेब हो। इन्टरनेट भनेको जडान हो — तार, राउटर, सन्देशको ठेगाना कसरी लेख्ने भन्ने सहमति। World Wide Web त्यसमाथि चल्ने एउटा सेवा हो, पृष्ठ र लिंकले बनेको। E-mail अर्को सेवा; file transfer अर्को। "इन्टरनेट भनेको वेबसाइटहरूको संग्रह" भन्दा अंक जान्छ, किनभने त्यसले एउटा सेवालाई पूरै नेटवर्क भनेको हुन्छ।

फाइदा: झन्डै जुनसुकै विषयको सूचना सेकेन्डमै; इमेल, सन्देश र भिडियो कलबाट दूरीको होइन, जडानको मूल्यमा सञ्चार; अनलाइन सिकाइ; e-commerce; e-governance; मनोरञ्जन; र जहाँबाट पनि गर्न मिल्ने काम।

बेफाइदा, जुन ४ अंकको उत्तरमा हुनुपर्छ: तथ्य जस्तो देखाइएको झूटो सूचना; ठगी र पैसा वा परिचयको चोरी; भाइरस; गोपनीयताको क्षति; समय खेर जानु; र बालबालिकालाई नसुहाउने सामग्री। फाइदा मात्र लेख्ने विद्यार्थीले आधा प्रश्नको उत्तर दिएको हुन्छ।

5.2  What you need to connect — जोड्न के–के चाहिन्छ

A chain, and every link is required

The requirements group into hardware, software and a service you pay for.

Hardware: a computer or phone; a modem or router; and a line — telephone wire, fibre-optic cable, coaxial cable, or a wireless or mobile-data link. A network interface card or Wi-Fi adapter sits inside the device.

Software: an operating system with TCP/IP — the protocols that say how a message is addressed and delivered — and a browser or other application to use the service you want.

Service: an account with an Internet Service Provider (ISP) — in Nepal, Nepal Telecom, WorldLink, Subisu, Vianet and others. The ISP already has a connection to the wider Internet; you are renting a share of it.

The modem is the part worth understanding rather than memorising. Its name is MOdulator-DEModulator. A computer produces digital signals; a telephone line carries a different kind of signal. Modulating turns the computer's digital output into something the line can carry; demodulating turns the arriving signal back into digital. It is a translator, and it has to work in both directions.

एउटा शृंखला, हरेक कडी अनिवार्य

आवश्यकता हार्डवेयर, सफ्टवेयर र तिर्नुपर्ने सेवा मा बाँडिन्छ।

हार्डवेयर: कम्प्युटर वा फोन; modem वा router; र एउटा लाइन — टेलिफोन तार, अप्टिकल फाइबर, कोएक्सियल केबल, वा वायरलेस/मोबाइल डाटा। उपकरणभित्र network interface card वा Wi-Fi adapter हुन्छ।

सफ्टवेयर: TCP/IP भएको operating system — सन्देशको ठेगाना कसरी लेख्ने र पुर्‍याउने भन्ने प्रोटोकल — र चाहिएको सेवा चलाउन browser वा अर्को app।

सेवा: Internet Service Provider (ISP) को खाता — नेपालमा Nepal Telecom, WorldLink, Subisu, Vianet आदि। ISP सँग विश्व इन्टरनेटको जडान छँदै छ; तपाईं त्यसैको एक अंश भाडामा लिनुहुन्छ।

घोक्नुभन्दा बुझ्नुपर्ने भाग modem हो। नाम नै MOdulator-DEModulator। कम्प्युटरले digital सङ्केत निकाल्छ; टेलिफोन लाइनले अर्कै किसिमको सङ्केत बोक्छ। Modulate गर्नु भनेको कम्प्युटरको digital आउटपुटलाई लाइनले बोक्न सक्ने रूपमा बदल्नु; demodulate भनेको आउने सङ्केतलाई फेरि digital बनाउनु। यो अनुवादक हो, र दुवै दिशामा काम गर्नैपर्छ।

5.3  Applications of the Internet — इन्टरनेटका प्रयोग

Nine services, each solving a different problem

The syllabus names nine. They are easiest to hold if you learn the problem each one solved.

  • WWW (World Wide Web) — pages of text, pictures and video, joined by links and read with a browser. The service most people mean when they say "the Internet".
  • E-mail — sending a written message to an address that is waiting for it, whether or not the other person is online. Store and forward.
  • Newsgroup — a public discussion area on a topic, where messages are posted for everybody rather than sent to one person. The ancestor of today's online forums.
  • Telnet — logging in to a distant computer and using it as though you were sitting at it. Old, and insecure because it sends passwords unencrypted.
  • IRC (Internet Relay Chat) — live typed conversation in channels, in real time. The ancestor of messaging apps.
  • E-commerce — buying and selling over the Internet: Daraz, SastoDeal, airline booking, eSewa and Khalti payments.
  • Search engine — a service that indexes pages and finds the ones matching your words. Google, Bing. Without one the Web would be unusable.
  • E-governance — government services delivered online: the Nepal PAN registration portal, online passport applications, result publication, land-revenue payment.
  • Remote control — taking over another computer's screen and keyboard across the network to fix it or to use software on it.

Telnet and remote control sound the same and are not. Telnet gives you a text command line on the far machine; remote control gives you its whole desktop as a picture.

नौ सेवा, हरेकले फरक समस्या हल गर्ने

पाठ्यक्रमले नौ वटा नाम लिन्छ। हरेकले कुन समस्या हल गर्‍यो सिक्यो भने सम्झन सजिलो हुन्छ।

  • WWW — पाठ, तस्बिर र भिडियोका पृष्ठ, लिंकले जोडिएका, browser ले पढिने। "इन्टरनेट" भन्दा धेरैले बुझ्ने सेवा यही हो।
  • E-mail — पर्खिरहेको ठेगानामा लिखित सन्देश पठाउने, अर्को व्यक्ति अनलाइन होस् या नहोस्। Store and forward।
  • Newsgroup — कुनै विषयमा सार्वजनिक छलफलको ठाउँ, जहाँ सन्देश एक जनालाई होइन सबैका लागि राखिन्छ। आजका अनलाइन फोरमका पुर्खा।
  • Telnet — टाढाको कम्प्युटरमा लगइन गरी त्यहीँ बसेजस्तै चलाउने। पुरानो, र पासवर्ड नलुकाई पठाउने भएकाले असुरक्षित।
  • IRC — च्यानलमा वास्तविक समयमै टाइप गरेर गरिने कुराकानी। Messaging app का पुर्खा।
  • E-commerce — इन्टरनेटबाट किनबेच: Daraz, SastoDeal, हवाई टिकट, eSewa र Khalti भुक्तानी।
  • Search engine — पृष्ठको सूची बनाएर तपाईंका शब्दसँग मिल्ने खोज्ने सेवा। Google, Bing। यो नभए वेब चलाउनै गाह्रो हुन्थ्यो।
  • E-governance — सरकारी सेवा अनलाइन: PAN दर्ता पोर्टल, अनलाइन राहदानी आवेदन, नतिजा प्रकाशन, मालपोत भुक्तानी।
  • Remote control — नेटवर्कबाटै अर्को कम्प्युटरको पर्दा र किबोर्ड चलाएर मर्मत गर्ने वा त्यहाँको सफ्टवेयर प्रयोग गर्ने।

Telnet र remote control उस्तै सुनिन्छन्, उस्तै होइनन्। Telnet ले पारिको मेसिनमा पाठको command line दिन्छ; remote control ले पूरै डेस्कटप तस्बिरका रूपमा।

Practise the judgement · निर्णय अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्

Which Internet service does this task need?

What you will learn: to match a real task to the service designed for it. Two of the cases are the pair students most often swap. तपाईंले के सिक्नुहुन्छ: वास्तविक काम र त्यसैका लागि बनेको सेवा मिलाउन। दुई केस विद्यार्थीले सबैभन्दा धेरै साटिने जोडी हुन्।

How to use this

Ask who the message is for. One named person, everybody, a machine you are operating, or a shop you are paying? The service follows from the answer.

सन्देश कसका लागि हो सोध्नुहोस्। एक जना तोकिएको व्यक्ति, सबै, तपाईंले चलाइरहेको मेसिन, कि तिर्न लागेको पसल? उत्तरबाटै सेवा निस्कन्छ।

Predict first · पहिले अनुमान गर्नुहोस्

A household has a computer, a router, a fibre line to the house and a browser installed — but no account with any ISP. Can it reach the Internet? एउटा घरमा कम्प्युटर, router, घरसम्म फाइबर लाइन र browser सबै छन् — तर कुनै ISP को खाता छैन। के इन्टरनेट चल्छ?

No. The fibre runs to the house and stops there. An ISP is the company that already has a connection to the wider Internet; what you buy is a share of theirs. Without an account the line reaches a door that will not open.

This is why the ISP belongs in the requirements list and why leaving it out is the commonest way to lose a mark on that question. The hardware alone connects you to nothing — which is also, in plainer terms, why the connection stops when the bill is unpaid.

चल्दैन। फाइबर घरसम्म आएर त्यहीँ टुङ्गिन्छ। ISP त्यो कम्पनी हो जोसँग विश्व इन्टरनेटको जडान पहिल्यै छ; तपाईंले किन्ने भनेको त्यसकै एक अंश हो। खाता नभए लाइन नखुल्ने ढोकासम्म पुग्छ। त्यसैले आवश्यकताको सूचीमा ISP पर्छ र यही छुटाउनु अंक गुमाउने सबैभन्दा सामान्य तरिका हो। सीधा भाषामा भन्दा — बिल नतिरे जडान किन रोकिन्छ भन्ने कारण पनि यही हो।
Example 1 Choosing the service for four tasks

Question: match each task to the Internet service it needs — (a) send your homework to one teacher, (b) pay the municipality, (c) find pages about the Himalayas, (d) a technician in Pokhara repairs a computer in Butwal.

For each one ask who the message is for: one named person, everybody, the state, or a machine you are operating. Write your four answers first. हरेकमा सन्देश कसका लागि हो सोध्नुहोस्: एक जना तोकिएको व्यक्ति, सबै, राज्य, कि तपाईंले चलाइरहेको मेसिन। पहिले चार उत्तर लेख्नुहोस्।

Working
(a) homework to one teacher E-MAIL one named recipient, and it waits until they look (b) pay the municipality E-GOVERNANCE a government service online. Money moves, which makes e-commerce tempting — but e-commerce is customer and business, not paying the state what you owe (c) find pages SEARCH ENGINE the Web holds the pages; the search engine is what makes finding one among billions possible (d) repair a distant computer REMOTE CONTROL the whole desktop. Telnet would give only a text command line

Two of these — (b) and (d) — are the ones most often answered wrongly, and both are wrong for the same reason: the obvious word is chosen before the question is read to the end. यीमध्ये (ख) र (घ) सबैभन्दा धेरै गलत हुन्छन्, र दुवै एउटै कारणले: प्रश्न अन्त्यसम्म नपढी स्पष्ट देखिने शब्द छानिन्छ।

Example 2 Why a village school chooses mobile data

Question: a school is twelve kilometres from the nearest fibre line. Explain which requirement is the obstacle, and what it can use instead.

Go through the requirements one at a time and find the one that fails. Which items are unaffected by distance? आवश्यकता एक–एक गरी हेर्नुहोस् र कुन पूरा हुँदैन भेट्टाउनुहोस्। दूरीले कुन–कुनलाई असर गर्दैन?

Working
device available not the obstacle browser, TCP/IP available not the obstacle ISP account can be bought not the obstacle THE LINE fibre is 12 km away — THIS is the obstacle Alternative lines that do not need a cable to the building: · mobile data (4G) through a router with a SIM · a wireless link to a tower within sight The modem/router is still required. A mobile router does the same modulating job for a radio signal that a fibre modem does for light.

The requirements list is not a formality. Working through it names the single thing that is missing, which is what an engineer — or an examiner — is asking for. आवश्यकताको सूची औपचारिकता होइन। एक–एक गरी हेर्दा छुटेको ठ्याक्कै एउटा कुरा थाहा हुन्छ — इन्जिनियर वा परीक्षकले खोजेकै त्यही हो।

Mistakes that cost marks here — यहाँ अङ्क गुमाउने गल्तीहरू

  • Saying the Internet and the WWW are the same. The Internet is the network; the Web is one service running on it.
  • Listing only advantages of the Internet. A question worth four marks almost always wants disadvantages too.
  • Forgetting the ISP in the requirements list. The hardware alone connects you to nothing.
  • Saying a modem is only for telephone lines. Any line that does not carry raw digital signals needs conversion — fibre included.
  • Mixing up Telnet and remote control. Telnet gives a command line; remote control gives a desktop.
इन्टरनेट र WWW उस्तै भन्नु: इन्टरनेट नेटवर्क हो, वेब त्यसमाथिको एउटा सेवा। फाइदा मात्र लेख्नु: ४ अंकको प्रश्नले प्रायः बेफाइदा पनि खोज्छ। ISP छुटाउनु: हार्डवेयरले मात्र कसैसँग जोड्दैन। Modem टेलिफोन लाइनका लागि मात्र भन्नु: कच्चा digital नबोक्ने हरेक लाइनलाई रूपान्तरण चाहिन्छ। Telnet र remote control मिसाउनु: Telnet ले command line, remote control ले डेस्कटप।

Memory hook — The Internet is the road. The Web is one kind of vehicle on it. सम्झने तरिका — इन्टरनेट सडक हो। वेब त्यसमा गुड्ने एक किसिमको सवारी।

This unit has the largest practical allocation in the subject — 12 hours — for only three activities, so each is meant to be substantial. 5.3 is the strongest project-work candidate here. पाठ्यक्रमकै युनिट ५ को सूचीबाट; यसले यिनलाई नमुना भनेको छ। यस युनिटमा विषयकै सबैभन्दा धेरै — १२ घण्टा — तर तीन मात्र क्रियाकलाप छन्, त्यसैले हरेक ठूलो हुनुपर्ने अपेक्षा छ। यहाँ ५.३ प्रोजेक्ट कामका लागि सबैभन्दा उपयुक्त छ।

5.1 Collect the names of the ISPs in your locality.

You need: to ask around your neighbourhood — shops, neighbours, your own household bill. No internet connection is required to do this, which matters if yours is unreliable. Hand in: the list, and for each one the monthly price and the speed advertised, if you can find out. Builds on: 5.2. चाहिन्छ: छिमेकमा सोध्ने — पसल, छिमेकी, आफ्नै घरको बिल। यसका लागि इन्टरनेट जडान चाहिँदैन, जुन तपाईंको जडान भरपर्दो नभए महत्त्वपूर्ण हुन्छ। बुझाउने: सूची, र सकेसम्म हरेकको मासिक मूल्य तथा विज्ञापित गति।

5.2 Make a presentation on Internet applications.

You need: presentation software or paper slides. Builds on: 5.3. Hand in: the file and your notes. Cover all nine, and give each a Nepali example — that is what turns a list into an explanation. चाहिन्छ: प्रस्तुतीकरण सफ्टवेयर वा कागजकै स्लाइड। आधार: ५.३। बुझाउने: फाइल र नोट। नौवटै समेट्नुहोस् र हरेकलाई नेपाली उदाहरण दिनुहोस् — सूचीलाई व्याख्या बनाउने त्यही हो।

5.3 Prepare a PowerPoint presentation on "Current trends of Internet and its use in Nepal".

You need: presentation software, and sources you can name. This is project-sized — treat it as your project work if your teacher agrees. Hand in: the file, your notes, and a list of where each figure came from. Any number you quote needs a source; a presentation of unsourced statistics is weaker than one with three figures you can defend. चाहिन्छ: प्रस्तुतीकरण सफ्टवेयर र नाम भन्न मिल्ने स्रोत। यो प्रोजेक्ट आकारको हो — शिक्षक सहमत हुनुहुन्छ भने प्रोजेक्ट काम नै बनाउनुहोस्। बुझाउने: फाइल, नोट, र हरेक तथ्याङ्क कहाँबाट आयो भन्ने सूची। उद्धृत गरेको हरेक संख्याको स्रोत चाहिन्छ; स्रोतविहीन तथ्याङ्कभन्दा बचाउ गर्न सकिने तीन संख्या बलियो हुन्छ।

Exam focus — what is asked from this unit — परीक्षा केन्द्र — यस युनिटबाट के सोधिन्छ

This unit carries 8 of the 50 external marks and 10 of the 64 theory hours. Expect: define the Internet; write its advantages and disadvantages; list the requirements for an Internet connection; and short notes on two or three of the nine services — e-commerce and e-governance are asked most often, and both want a Nepali example. यो युनिटले बाह्य ५० मध्ये ८ अंक र ६४ मध्ये १० घण्टा ओगट्छ। अपेक्षा: इन्टरनेटको परिभाषा; फाइदा र बेफाइदा; जडानका लागि चाहिने कुराको सूची; र नौमध्ये दुई–तीन सेवामा छोटो टिप्पणी — e-commerce र e-governance सबैभन्दा धेरै सोधिन्छन्, र दुवैले नेपाली उदाहरण खोज्छन्।

2 marks Practice question

What is the Internet? How does it differ from the World Wide Web? इन्टरनेट भनेको के हो? यो World Wide Web भन्दा कसरी फरक छ?

The Internet is a global network of interconnected computer networks that communicate using common protocols such as TCP/IP.

The World Wide Web is one service that runs on the Internet — a collection of linked pages viewed with a browser. The Internet also carries e-mail, file transfer and other services, so the Web is a part of it and not the same thing.

इन्टरनेट भनेको आपसमा जोडिएका कम्प्युटर नेटवर्कहरूको विश्वव्यापी नेटवर्क हो, जसले TCP/IP जस्ता साझा प्रोटोकल प्रयोग गर्छ। World Wide Web चाहिँ त्यसमाथि चल्ने एउटा सेवा हो — browser ले हेरिने जोडिएका पृष्ठहरूको संग्रह। इन्टरनेटले इमेल र file transfer पनि बोक्छ, त्यसैले वेब यसको एक भाग हो, उही होइन।
4 marks Practice question

List the requirements for an Internet connection. इन्टरनेट जडानका लागि चाहिने कुराहरू लेख्नुहोस्।

Hardware: a computer or mobile device; a modem or router; a transmission line (telephone, fibre-optic, cable, or wireless/mobile data); a network interface card or Wi-Fi adapter.

Software: an operating system supporting TCP/IP protocols; a browser or other client application.

Service: an account with an Internet Service Provider (ISP).

Grouping them into hardware, software and service is what makes this a four-mark answer rather than a list.

हार्डवेयर: कम्प्युटर वा मोबाइल; modem वा router; प्रसारण लाइन (टेलिफोन, फाइबर, केबल, वायरलेस/मोबाइल डाटा); NIC वा Wi-Fi adapter। सफ्टवेयर: TCP/IP भएको OS; browser वा अर्को app। सेवा: ISP को खाता। हार्डवेयर–सफ्टवेयर–सेवा गरी समूहमा लेख्दा सूची नभई चार अंकको उत्तर बन्छ।
2 marks Practice question

What is a modem? Why is it needed? Modem भनेको के हो? किन चाहिन्छ?

A modem (MOdulator-DEModulator) is a device that converts the digital signals of a computer into a form that can travel over a transmission line, and converts arriving signals back into digital form.

It is needed because a computer works with digital signals while a telephone or similar line carries a different kind of signal; without conversion in both directions no data could pass.

Modem (MOdulator-DEModulator) ले कम्प्युटरको digital सङ्केतलाई लाइनमा जान सक्ने रूपमा बदल्छ र आउने सङ्केतलाई फेरि digital बनाउँछ। कम्प्युटर digital मा चल्छ तर लाइनले अर्कै सङ्केत बोक्छ, त्यसैले दुवै दिशाको रूपान्तरण विना data जान सक्दैन।
4 marks Practice question

Write short notes on e-commerce and e-governance with examples from Nepal. E-commerce र e-governance मा नेपालका उदाहरणसहित छोटो टिप्पणी लेख्नुहोस्।

E-commerce is the buying and selling of goods and services over the Internet, including the payment. The customer browses, orders and pays without going to a shop. Examples in Nepal: Daraz, SastoDeal, online airline booking, and payment through eSewa or Khalti.

E-governance is the delivery of government services and information to citizens over the Internet. It reduces travel and queuing and makes records easier to check. Examples in Nepal: online PAN registration, online passport application, publication of examination results, and online land-revenue payment.

Two marks each: one for the explanation, one for a real example.

E-commerce — इन्टरनेटबाट वस्तु र सेवाको किनबेच, भुक्तानीसहित। नेपालमा: Daraz, SastoDeal, अनलाइन हवाई टिकट, eSewa/Khalti भुक्तानी. E-governance — सरकारी सेवा र सूचना नागरिकसम्म इन्टरनेटबाट पुर्‍याउनु; यात्रा र लाइन घट्छ, अभिलेख जाँच्न सजिलो हुन्छ। नेपालमा: अनलाइन PAN दर्ता, राहदानी आवेदन, नतिजा प्रकाशन, मालपोत भुक्तानी। प्रत्येकको दुई अंक: एक व्याख्याको, एक वास्तविक उदाहरणको।

Key points from this unit

  • The Internet is the network of networks; the Web is one service on it.
  • An honest answer on the Internet gives disadvantages as well as advantages.
  • Requirements group into hardware, software and an ISP account — and every link is required.
  • A modem modulates outgoing digital into line signal and demodulates incoming back.
  • Nine services: WWW, e-mail, newsgroup, Telnet, IRC, e-commerce, search engine, e-governance, remote control.
  • Telnet gives a command line; remote control gives a whole desktop.
इन्टरनेट = नेटवर्कहरूको नेटवर्क; वेब = त्यसमाथिको एउटा सेवा। इमानदार उत्तरले फाइदासँगै बेफाइदा पनि दिन्छ। आवश्यकता: हार्डवेयर, सफ्टवेयर र ISP खाता — हरेक कडी अनिवार्य। Modem ले जाने digital लाई modulate र आउनेलाई demodulate गर्छ। नौ सेवा: WWW, e-mail, newsgroup, Telnet, IRC, e-commerce, search engine, e-governance, remote control। Telnet — command line; remote control — पूरै डेस्कटप।
