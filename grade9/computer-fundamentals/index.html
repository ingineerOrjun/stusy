Fundamentals of Computer and Application

The subject that answers the first question: what IS a computer, and what is it made of? It starts with the definition and the five ways computers are classified, then works through the parts you can point at — input, output, memory, processor — before reaching software, the internet, multimedia, and the technologies arriving now.

पहिलो प्रश्नको उत्तर दिने विषय: कम्प्युटर भनेको के हो, र यो केले बनेको हुन्छ? सुरु परिभाषा र वर्गीकरणका पाँच आधारबाट हुन्छ, अनि औंल्याएर देखाउन सकिने भाग — input, output, memory, processor — हुँदै सफ्टवेयर, इन्टरनेट, मल्टिमिडिया र अहिले आइरहेका प्रविधिसम्म पुगिन्छ।
